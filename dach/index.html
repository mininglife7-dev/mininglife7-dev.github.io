Unadhi DACH guides

A starting point for teams in Germany, Austria and Switzerland that build, buy or sell AI. Market-specific guides are being added; check official sources before acting. General information, not legal advice.

Rules to know

  • EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): Article 4 AI literacy since 2 February 2025; Article 50 transparency from 2 August 2026
  • GDPR with Germany's BDSG and Austria's DSG
  • Switzerland: revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG/nFADP, in force since 1 September 2023); Swiss firms serving EU users can fall under the EU AI Act
  • Germany UWG §7 and Austria TKG 2021 §174: B2B cold email generally needs prior consent
