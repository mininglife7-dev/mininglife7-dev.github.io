Weekly e-mail digest (opt-in)

Every Monday a scheduled job re-checks our open vendor catalogue and the deadline list and writes a public digest: latest digest. To get it by e-mail, send us a subscribe e-mail. Your address stays in our mailbox only; it is never published or stored in a repository. Reply UNSUBSCRIBE at any time.

The digest covers public catalogue and deadline changes, not your own tools: this page sends nothing and your tool list never leaves your browser. Personalised automatic alerts are coming.

Team licence

Team members ($19/month or $190/year, EURO AI Register membership) get unlimited exports here with the same licence key. The key is checked with Gumroad from your browser; nothing else is sent. Your key is in your Gumroad receipt e-mail and in your Gumroad Library. If the check fails: paste the key without spaces, make sure the membership is active, then e-mail us.
