AI chatbot and voice-agent disclosure in Ireland: Article 50 applies since 2 Aug 2026

Art 50 of the EU AI Act requires that people be told when they are interacting with an AI system. It has applied since 2 Aug 2026. Machine-readable marking for generative systems already on the market gets a grace period to 2 Dec 2026 (European Parliament legislative train). Applies to: website chatbots, AI receptionists, WhatsApp agents, and outbound AI calls. What to do: add a clear disclosure at the start of every conversation, record where it appears, and train staff (Art 4 AI literacy, since 2 Feb 2025).
