Is AIDA law in Canada? (October 2026)

Short answer: no. The Artificial Intelligence and Data Act was Part 3 of Bill C-27. The bill died when Parliament was prorogued on 6 January 2025. Its last step had been referral to committee in April 2023 (LEGISinfo C-27). AIDA created no obligations.

What applies to your AI today

Rule Who When
PIPEDA: consent, purpose, safeguards and accountability for personal information, including when AI uses it Private sector In force (OPC)
Quebec Law 25, s.12.1: notice and review for decisions made exclusively by automated processing Enterprises handling Quebec personal information In force since 22 Sep 2023 (LégisQuébec)
OSFI Guideline E-23: model inventory and risk rating, AI/ML included Federally regulated financial institutions 1 May 2027 (OSFI)
Ontario ESA: say in job postings if AI screens applicants Ontario employers with 25+ staff 1 Jan 2026 (Ontario)
Directive on Automated Decision-Making: Algorithmic Impact Assessment Federal departments, and in practice their vendors Updated 24 Jun 2025 (TBS)

What is only proposed

Bill C-36 (45th Parliament) would replace part of PIPEDA and add transparency for automated decisions. It was introduced on 15 June 2026 and is not law. Check its stage on LEGISinfo.
