OSFI E-23: building your AI model inventory before 1 May 2027

OSFI published the final Guideline E-23, Model Risk Management, on 11 September 2025. It takes effect on 1 May 2027 for banks, foreign bank branches, insurers and trust & loan companies (OSFI). Pension plans were taken out of scope (OSFI letter). It is supervisory guidance, not a statute, and it applies in proportion to an institution's size and risk.

A starter inventory record (our suggestion, not OSFI's form)

  • Model ID, name, version
  • Type: statistical, AI/ML, generative AI, or vendor/third-party
  • Business owner and developer
  • Use: which decisions it supports
  • Risk rating, with the criteria you used
  • Data sources, and whether they include personal information
  • Last validation, next review, known limitations
  • Monitoring metrics and drift thresholds
  • Approval and change history; decommission date

Credit unions and provincial institutions

E-23 binds only federally regulated institutions. Provincially regulated credit unions answer to their provincial regulator, but many use E-23 as a benchmark. Check with yours.
