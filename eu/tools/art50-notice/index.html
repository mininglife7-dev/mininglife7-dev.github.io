FAQ

When does Article 50 apply?

From 2 August 2026, the general date of application in Article 113 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Providers of generative AI systems that were already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 to meet the Article 50(2) marking duty (new Article 111(4), added by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744).

Does a visible "made with AI" line cover Article 50(2)?

No. Article 50(2) asks providers to mark outputs in a machine-readable format so they are detectable as AI-generated (for example metadata or watermarking). A visible label helps people, but it does not replace machine-readable marking. The Commission's Code of Practice on transparency of AI-generated content describes practical measures.

I use someone else's chatbot on my website. Is Article 50(1) my duty?

Article 50(1) is addressed to providers: the chatbot must be designed so people are told they are talking to an AI system, unless that is obvious. If you deploy a third-party chatbot, check that it shows such a notice, and add your own if it doesn't. If you put an AI system on the market under your own name, you may count as its provider.

Do the high-risk rules apply from August 2026 too?

No. Under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, the high-risk rules for Annex III systems apply from 2 December 2027 and for Annex I products from 2 August 2028. Article 50 transparency and Article 4 AI literacy are separate duties that already apply.
