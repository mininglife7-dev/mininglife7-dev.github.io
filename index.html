Questions doctors ask

Will it replace my receptionist?+

No. It's built as a safety net: your staff answer first, and HaanJi picks up only the calls they miss or after closing time (once your phone line is live).

Do I need a new phone number?+

No. You keep your current clinic number and set call forwarding for unanswered/busy calls to our line. We help you set it up. Honest status: our phone line is still being set up (business registration and telecom KYC in progress), so call forwarding isn't available yet — today you can try HaanJi in the browser demo.

Will it give medical advice?+

It's built not to. It shares only what you approve — timings, services, fees, directions — and takes appointment requests. It is instructed never to diagnose; emergencies and clinical questions are flagged for your doctor or staff.

What if a patient wants to talk to a human?+

It detects the request (in Hindi or English) and logs it as a handoff note for your staff to call back. Forwarding the live call to a number you choose will be available once the phone line is live; sending these notes to you on WhatsApp is coming soon.

Which languages does it speak?+

Hindi, English and Hinglish today — try Hindi and English in the live demo. More Indian languages are coming, but not available yet.

How does the money-back guarantee work?+

After a 10-minute demo call you pay the ₹15,000 setup and share your clinic details. Your phone line goes live within 7 days (our own phone line setup is in progress). The 14-day window starts on your go-live day, not the payment day: if within 14 days of going live you feel it isn't worth it, send us one message and we refund the full setup fee — no questions asked. And until you go live, you can ask for a full refund of the setup fee at any time. The ₹4,000/month starts on day 15 after go-live, monthly with no lock-in.

Is patient data safe?+

We collect only what's needed for the appointment request (name, phone, preferred date/time, reason), use it only for your clinic, and never sell it. We aim to follow India's DPDP Act principles and put these commitments in writing in our Terms and Privacy Policy, which you can read before you pay.

Can I cancel?+

Yes — monthly plan, no lock-in. Just switch off call forwarding and tell us.
