Rules to know

  • South Africa: POPIA (section 69 restricts unsolicited direct marketing by email)
  • Nigeria: Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
  • Kenya: Data Protection Act 2019
  • African Union Continental AI Strategy (2024, policy not law)
  • EU AI Act reach for African firms selling into the EU
