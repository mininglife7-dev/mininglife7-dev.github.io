सवाल-जवाब FAQ

क्या Asha अभी मेरे clinic का फ़ोन उठाएगी? / Will Asha answer my phone today?

Not yet. Phone answering on your number starts after our phone line setup (in progress). Today you can hear the demo and book a 10-min demo call. If go-live slips, you can take the full setup refund before go-live.

Does Asha book appointments?

Asha takes appointment requests (name, phone, day, time, reason) and saves them for your staff to confirm. It doesn't book, reschedule or cancel by itself.

क्या Asha medical advice देगी? / Will it give medical advice?

Asha is instructed never to diagnose or give medical advice. It only shares clinic information you approve, and it flags emergencies and "talk to a human" requests for your staff.

Which languages?

Hindi, English and Hinglish today. More Indian languages are coming, but they aren't available yet.

Can I keep my number?

Yes, by design: busy/unanswered calls forward to Asha's line. This works after our phone line setup (in progress).

WhatsApp confirmations?

Coming soon, not available yet. Today, requests are saved for your staff.
