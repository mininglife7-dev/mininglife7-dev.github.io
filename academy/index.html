What the completion record is, and is not

It is a completion record issued by Unadhi Academy, not an official accreditation. The EU AI Act does not require a certificate: Article 4 asks providers and deployers to take measures to support AI literacy, and the European Commission's Q&A says an internal record of trainings is a sensible approach.
