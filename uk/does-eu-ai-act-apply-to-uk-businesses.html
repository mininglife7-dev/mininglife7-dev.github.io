Does the EU AI Act apply to UK businesses? (2026 answer with dates)

Updated 13 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: products linked below are Unadhi products with free editions.

Short answer: Yes, in two common cases. (1) You place an AI system on the EU market, wherever you are established (Art 2(1)(a)). (2) You are a UK provider or deployer and the output of your AI system is used in the EU (Art 2(1)(c)). Brexit does not change this.

Which UK businesses are caught

  • A UK SaaS selling an AI feature to EU customers (provider).
  • A UK firm whose AI scores, screens, writes or generates content that EU clients or branches use (output used in the Union; Recital 22 explains the anti-circumvention aim).
  • A UK company with an EU subsidiary that uses AI (that subsidiary is an EU deployer).

First step (15 minutes)

  • List every AI tool you build or use.
  • Mark which outputs reach EU customers, staff or users.
  • For each one, note your role (provider or deployer) and whether it chats with people or generates content (Article 50).
