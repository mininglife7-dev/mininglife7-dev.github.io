UK B2B cold email rules in 2026: PECR, sole traders and the new £17.5m fines

Updated 13 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: products linked below are Unadhi products with free editions.

Short answer: PECR's email consent rule doesn't apply to corporate subscribers (limited companies, LLPs, Scottish partnerships, some government bodies). You must still say who you are, give an opt-out in every message and honour it. Sole traders and ordinary partnerships count as individuals: email them only with consent or a soft opt-in. Since 5 Feb 2026 the maximum PECR fine is £17.5m or 4% of worldwide turnover.

Checklist before a B2B campaign

  • Each recipient checked as a Ltd/LLP (Companies House or their own site); if unsure, treat as an individual
  • Prefer generic inboxes (info@, hello@). Named addresses are personal data, so you need a UK GDPR lawful basis (usually legitimate interests) and a privacy notice
  • Identity and a working opt-out in every email
  • Suppression list screened before every send
  • No bought-in lists of individuals
