AI CV screening in the UK after the Data (Use and Access) Act: what applies in 2026

Updated 13 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: products linked below are Unadhi products with free editions.

Short answer: Since 5 Feb 2026, UK GDPR generally allows significant solely-automated decisions (like auto-rejecting a candidate), with safeguards: tell people, let them make representations, offer human intervention, and let them contest the decision. Special-category data stays restricted. The ICO's updated ADM guidance is still in draft. If EU roles or clients use your output, EU Annex III high-risk duties for recruitment apply from 2 Dec 2027.

Safeguard checklist

  • Candidates told an automated decision is made
  • Route to a human review, with a named owner
  • Way to contest, with a response time
  • No special-category data in solely automated decisions without a lawful condition
  • DPIA for high-risk processing
  • Complaints process (DUAA duty since 19 Jun 2026)
