AI video, voice and image tools: the 2 December 2026 EU marking deadline for UK providers

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Dates checked against the Official Journal · Disclosure: products linked below are Unadhi products with free editions.

Short answer: If your UK tool generates synthetic audio, images, video or text used in the EU, its outputs must be marked in a machine-readable, detectable way (Art 50(2)). Systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026. Newer systems must comply now. Deepfake disclosure by deployers (Art 50(4)) gets no extra time.

What the law says (exact wording)

Article 50(2): “Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, shall ensure that the outputs of the AI system are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated.” It does not apply where the system only performs “an assistive function for standard editing” or does “not substantially alter the input data provided by the deployer or the semantics thereof”.

The extra time (Article 111(4), added by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, OJ L 24.7.2026): “Providers of AI systems, including general-purpose AI systems, generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, that have been placed on the market before 2 August 2026 shall take the necessary steps in order to comply with Article 50(2) by 2 December 2026.”

Who gets the extra time, and who does not

Your situationArticle 50(2) marking due
Generator placed on the EU market before 2 Aug 20262 Dec 2026
Generator placed on the EU market on or after 2 Aug 2026Now (since 2 Aug 2026)
Deployer publishing deepfakes (Art 50(4)), chatbot disclosure (Art 50(1))Now: Article 111(4) covers only Article 50(2) providers
Only standard editing, or no substantial change to the inputException in Art 50(2) may apply: document why

For context, the same Regulation moved high-risk duties for Annex III systems (for example recruitment and credit scoring) to 2 Dec 2027, and for AI in Annex I regulated products to 2 Aug 2028. It did not move Article 50.

What to do before 2 December

  • Inventory each generator (video, voice, image, text) and its launch date
  • Choose and document a marking method (metadata/watermark) and test it survives export
  • Tell EU customers how to label deepfakes they publish
  • Keep test evidence and a dated record

The Commission has published a voluntary Code of Practice on transparency of AI-generated content, with a public, growing list of signatories.
