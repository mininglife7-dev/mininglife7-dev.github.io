Asha by HaanJi: AI receptionist for dental & skin clinics

Asha is designed to help your front desk when the phone keeps ringing during OPD rush, lunch or late evenings. In the demo, she answers common questions and takes appointment requests for your staff to confirm. Phone answering starts after our phone line setup (in progress).

What Asha does in the demo today

  • Talks in Hindi and English (and Hinglish) in our browser voice demo, with male and female voices
  • Chats in Telugu, Hindi and English in the demo (Telugu wording under native review)
  • Answers your clinic's FAQs (timings, fees, address, doctors, payment) from your own clinic details
  • Takes appointment requests (name, phone, date, time, reason), saved for your staff to confirm
  • Directs emergencies to call 108 and flags them for staff
  • Is built not to give medical advice or diagnoses

The demo runs on sample clinic data. We can show it with your clinic's details where possible.
