About us

One mind. Many helpers.

UNADHI (say it oo-NAH-dhee) joins the Latin una, "one", and the Sanskrit dhī, "intellect": one mind. We build one shared AI platform and turn it into simple, focused helpers for real businesses: a receptionist for a clinic, an assistant for a coaching centre, a front desk for a property office.

Our mark

Our logo is a diya, the lamp lit at the start of something good. Its flame stands for "first light": a helper that's ready when your day begins.

Our mission

Make practical, honest AI help available to every small business in India, starting with dental and skin clinics in Telangana.

How we work

  • We say what works today and label what's coming soon.
  • Our helpers support your staff. They don't replace them.
  • We collect the minimum data, use it only for your business and never sell it.
  • We speak your language: Telugu, Hindi and English.
