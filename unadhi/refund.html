Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Draft.

In short (Asha by HaanJi): full refund of the ₹15,000 setup fee anytime before go-live; a 14-day no-questions money-back on the setup fee counted from your go-live day; the ₹4,000 monthly fee starts from day 15 after go-live, with no lock-in.

1. Setup fee (₹15,000)

  • Before go-live: cancel at any time and get a full refund of the setup fee.
  • After go-live: ask within 14 days of your go-live day and we refund the full setup fee. No reason needed. The 14 days are counted from go-live, not from the day you paid.
  • After the 14-day window, the setup fee is non-refundable, because the setup work has been done and accepted.

2. Monthly fee (₹4,000)

  • Starts from day 15 after go-live, paid in advance for each monthly cycle.
  • Cancel anytime: no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Cancellation takes effect from the next cycle; the service continues until the end of the cycle already paid for.
  • No partial refunds for a monthly cycle that has already started. If we end the service ourselves, we refund any fee paid for the period after the service ends.

3. Payment mistakes

If you pay twice or pay too much, we refund the extra amount in full.

4. How to ask

Send a WhatsApp message or call +91 91821 63868. A single message is enough. For the 14-day guarantee, the date you sent your request counts.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method (the same UPI ID or bank account you paid from) within 5–7 working days of approval.
