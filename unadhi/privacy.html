Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026. Draft.

This policy explains what personal data UNADHI handles, why, for how long, and how to contact us. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 in mind. "UNADHI", "we" and "us" mean the owner of the UNADHI business, Hima Bindu D [legal name, structure and address to be confirmed].

1. Our two roles

Whose dataOur roleWho decides how it is used
Patients and callers of a clinic that uses Asha by HaanJiData Processor: we process data only on the clinic's behalf and instructionsThe clinic (the Data Fiduciary)
Our customers (clinics and businesses), people who contact us, and website visitorsData FiduciaryUNADHI

2. Data we process for clinics (Asha by HaanJi)

  • Appointment requests: name, phone number, preferred date and time, and the reason for the visit as the caller states it.
  • Conversation data: once phone answering is live (after our phone line setup, in progress), call audio is converted to text to understand and reply; transcripts may be kept for the clinic to review.

We use this data only to provide the service to that clinic. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or contact patients for our own purposes. If cloud speech or AI providers are enabled for a clinic, audio or text is processed by those providers, and we will name them in that clinic's service terms before use. Retention periods (for example, deletion of transcripts and requests after a fixed period, and deletion within 30 days after the service ends) are set out in each clinic's service terms. [Confirm final retention periods.]

3. Data we collect as Data Fiduciary

  • When you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone: your name, number, business name and your message. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own privacy policy applies.
  • Customers: billing and contact details, and clinic information you give us for setup.
  • Website: this site does not use cookies or analytics at present. The contact form does not store anything; it opens WhatsApp with your message. If we add analytics later, we will update this section first.

4. Purpose and legal basis

We process personal data with your consent or for legitimate uses allowed under the DPDP Act (for example, to provide a service you asked for), only for the purposes stated here.

5. Security

We take reasonable security safeguards, including access controls and encryption where available, and limit access to people who need it. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected clinics and, where the law requires, the Data Protection Board of India and affected people.

6. Where data is processed

Our service providers and team may process data in or outside India (for example, the owner is based in the United Kingdom). Any transfer outside India will follow the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India. [Confirm cross-border arrangements.]

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask to access a summary of your personal data, correct or update it, erase it (where we no longer need it), withdraw consent, nominate another person to act for you, and raise a grievance. If you are a patient of a clinic, please contact the clinic first; we will help the clinic respond.
