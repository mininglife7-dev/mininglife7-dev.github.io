FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is UNADHI?

UNADHI builds practical AI helpers for small businesses in India. Our first product is Asha by HaanJi, an AI receptionist for dental and skin clinics in Telangana.

Does Asha answer my clinic's phone calls today?

Not yet. Phone answering starts after our phone line setup (in progress). Today you can try Asha in our browser voice demo and demo chat.

Which languages does Asha speak?

Asha talks in Hindi and English (and Hinglish) in our browser voice demo, and chats in Telugu, Hindi and English in the demo (Telugu wording under native review). More Indian languages are coming.

How does Asha handle appointments?

Asha takes appointment requests (name, phone, date, time and reason) and saves them for your staff to confirm. Your staff confirm every appointment.

What happens in an emergency?

Asha directs emergencies to call 108 and flags them for staff. Asha is built not to give medical advice or diagnoses. It isn't an emergency service.

How much does Asha cost?

₹15,000 one-time setup (paid upfront) and ₹4,000 per month from day 15 after go-live, with no lock-in. Final terms are on your written quote and receipt.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Full refund of the setup fee anytime before go-live, and a 14-day no-questions money-back on the setup fee counted from your go-live day. See the Refund & Cancellation policy.

What happens to my patients' data?

We collect only name, phone, date, time and reason, use it only for your clinic and never sell it. This is written into your service terms before you pay. Read our Privacy Policy.

Do you charge GST?

We are not registered under GST at present, so no GST is charged. If this changes, we will tell you in writing before your next billing cycle.

When will the digital kits be available?

The DPDP Starter Kit for clinics and the automation workflow packs are coming soon (not available yet). Message us on WhatsApp to be told when they launch.
