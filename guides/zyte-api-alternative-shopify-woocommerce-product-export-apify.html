Short answer: Zyte sells web scraping infrastructure for developers: Zyte API (proxies, browser rendering and AI extraction, including for product and e-commerce pages), Zyte Data and Scrapy Cloud. Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) prices Zyte API per 1,000 successful responses, in five website tiers from Simple to Advanced. Pay as you go: $0.13 to $1.27 per 1,000 HTTP responses and $1.01 to $16.08 per 1,000 browser-rendered responses. With a $100 monthly commitment the ranges drop to $0.10–$0.95 and $0.75–$12.00, with $200 to $0.08–$0.76 and $0.60–$9.60, and lower again at higher commitments. A trial gives $5 of free credit for 30 days with no commitment. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify needs no code: give it shop addresses and it returns product rows (titles, prices, compare-at prices, variants, stock, SKU/GTIN and images where the shop shows them) as a spreadsheet. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It is made for independent Shopify, WooCommerce and schema.org shops, not marketplaces such as Amazon. If you run your own crawlers at scale across many sites, Zyte is built for that. If you want competitors' Shopify or WooCommerce catalogues in a sheet, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“We crawl millions of pages a month and need ban handling and rendering priced per response.” That is an infrastructure job. “I want my three competitors' WooCommerce prices in a sheet every Monday” is a ready-made export job.

Who should pick Zyte instead

  • You are a developer or data team running your own crawlers across many sites.
  • You need ban handling and browser rendering at volume, priced per response.
  • You already use Scrapy and want hosting from the same company.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want no code and a spreadsheet of products and prices.
  • Your competitors run Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • You need occasional checks, not a crawling setup.
