The pain this page is about

Frequently asked questions

How much does trovevault's Shopify Products Scraper cost?

Is inventory enrichment free on trovevault's Actor?

Its pricingInfos reasonForChange text says inventory enrichment is free until 23 September 2026; afterward only enriched products are charged. Re-check the live Actor page.

Does the Unadhi Actor charge per product?

There is no developer Actor fee during launch (pricingInfos empty on 3 Nov 2026). You pay Apify platform usage for the run.

Can I use both?

Yes. Use a deep Shopify catalogue Actor when you need GTINs and metafields; use our tracker for mixed Shopify/WooCommerce price monitoring with no developer fee during launch.
