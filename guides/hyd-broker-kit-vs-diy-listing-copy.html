Short answer: You can write listings that follow TG-RERA's ad rule yourself, for free. TG-RERA's public notice (issued under Section 37 of the RERA Act, 2016) says advertisements of real estate projects in print, hoardings or any visual medium must show the competent authority's project permit number, the TG-RERA registration number and the TG-RERA website in the top right corner, in a font at least as large as the contact details; radio, electronic media and SMS ads must prominently mention the registration number and approval details. The rule, in the regulator's words

From TG-RERA's public notice “For the attention of promoters of real estate projects”: advertisements “shall mention the Competent Authorities PROJECT PERMIT NUMBER and REGISTRATION NUMBER along with TGRERA Website” in the top right corner, with a font size equal to or larger than the font used for the project contact details and address (TG-RERA public notice, PDF). Rentals and resales of completed homes are a different situation; the notice is about real estate projects. Not legal advice.

What must a Telangana real estate project advertisement show under TG-RERA?

TG-RERA's public notice, issued under Section 37 of the RERA Act 2016, says ads must show the competent authority's project permit number, the TG-RERA registration number and the TG-RERA website in the top right corner, in a font at least as large as the contact details. Radio, electronic and SMS ads must prominently mention registration and approval details.
