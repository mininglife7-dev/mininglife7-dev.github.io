Short answer: They do different jobs. Zoho Practice is practice-management software for accounting firms: client management, tasks and recurring tasks, timesheets and billing, documents, workpapers, workflow automation and WhatsApp integration, with a client self-service portal on Premium. Its India pricing page (checked 22 Oct 2026) shows Standard at ₹2,950 per organisation per month billed annually (₹3,540 billed monthly) and Premium at ₹8,950 (₹10,740), each for up to 5 users, extra users ₹400 per user per month billed annually, a 14-day free trial, and free access for Zoho Finance partners. Our White-Label Client Compliance Calendar is not software: it is four quarterly packs (DOCX, PDF, WhatsApp image, .ics, .xlsx) with your firm's name on them, for you to send to unlimited clients, at ₹999 a year. If you want to run your firm's work on one system, pick Zoho Practice. If you only want a branded due-date calendar for clients, start with the calendar; many firms could use both.

The pain this page is about

“Clients phone every month asking when GSTR-3B or TDS is due, and some still miss it.” Practice software organises your team's work on each client. A client calendar puts the dates in front of the client, under your firm's name, before the quarter starts.

Who should pick Zoho Practice instead

  • You want one system to run the firm: tasks per client, recurring compliance tasks, timesheets, billing, documents and workpapers.
  • Your clients use Zoho Books or another Zoho Finance app, or you want a client portal for document exchange.
  • You are, or plan to become, a Zoho Finance partner: the page says partners get free access.

Who the white-label calendar fits better

  • You already track work your own way and only want a branded quarterly calendar for every client.
  • You want something clients can add to their phone calendar in one step (.ics), with extension updates on a best-effort basis.
  • ₹999 a year matters more to you than workflow features.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Zoho Practice cost in India?

Its pricing page (checked 22 Oct 2026) shows Standard at ₹2,950 per organisation per month billed annually or ₹3,540 billed monthly, and Premium at ₹8,950 or ₹10,740, each for up to 5 users. Extra users are ₹400 per user per month billed annually.

Is Zoho Practice free?

For Zoho Finance partners, yes: the page says Authorized and Advanced partners get Standard features free and Premium partners get Premium. Others can take a 14-day free trial.

Does Zoho Practice send due-date calendars to clients?

Its plans list client collaboration, recurring tasks, WhatsApp integration and, on Premium, a client portal. The pricing page does not describe a ready-made, branded statutory due-date calendar for clients, so ask Zoho if that is what you need.

Is the ₹999 calendar a subscription?

It is a yearly licence sold as a Gumroad membership, so it renews yearly unless you cancel. Year one covers four quarterly packs, starting with your first pack.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many firms could track work in practice software and still send clients a branded quarterly calendar.
