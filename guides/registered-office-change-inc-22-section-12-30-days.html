Moving your company's registered office: which approval you need and filing INC-22 within 30 days

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Under section 12(4), a company files notice of every change of its registered office with the Registrar in Form INC-22 within 30 days of the change. Within the same city, town or village a board resolution is enough. Outside those local limits a special resolution is needed; moving to another ROC's jurisdiction in the same state also needs the Regional Director's confirmation (INC-23), and moving to another state means altering the MoA with Central Government (Regional Director) approval.

Which route

MoveApprovalFiling
Within the same city/town/villageBoard resolutionINC-22 in 30 days
Another city, same ROCSpecial resolution (MGT-14)INC-22 in 30 days
Another ROC, same stateSpecial resolution + Regional Director (INC-23)INC-22 after confirmation
Another stateSpecial resolution altering MoA + Central Government (RD) approvalApproval filings, then INC-22

Documents usually attached to INC-22

  • Proof of the new office (sale deed or rent agreement)
  • Owner's NOC
  • Utility bill not older than two months
  • Resolution copy

After the move

  • Update GST registration (REG-14, core field) within 15 days
  • Update PAN/TAN address, bank, letterheads, website and invoices
  • Paint or affix the name and address outside the new office (section 12(3))

Common mistakes

  • Moving first, filing months later. The 30-day clock runs from the change.
  • Board resolution only, for a move to another city. That needs a special resolution.
  • Forgetting GST. The GST principal place is a separate amendment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the time limit to file INC-22?

Within 30 days of the change of registered office (section 12(4), Companies Act 2013).

Is a special resolution needed to shift the registered office within the same city?

No. A board resolution is enough within the local limits of the same city, town or village.

When is Regional Director approval needed?

When the office moves to the jurisdiction of another Registrar within the same state (INC-23), or to another state.

Do I also need to change GST registration?

Yes. The principal place of business is a core field; file REG-14 within 15 days.
