The pain this page is about

“We use ChatGPT, a website chatbot and an AI feature in our CRM. What do we actually have to write down, and do we need a €2,000-a-year platform for it?” For many small deployers the first job is a list of AI systems and a record of the literacy measures taken. Bigger or regulated firms need more.

Why this matters now (official dates)

  • Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 to providers and deployers, whatever the risk level. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, it asks organisations to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff; it does not require them to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The European Commission's Q&A says there is no need for a certificate: an internal record of trainings or guidance is enough, and national authorities supervise and enforce from August 2026 (Commission AI literacy Q&A).
  • Article 50 transparency duties (for example, telling people they are talking to a chatbot) apply from 2 August 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
  • High-risk rules were moved by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III uses (such as recruitment and credit scoring) from 2 December 2027, and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SimpleAct cost?

Its pricing page (checked 23 Oct 2026) shows Starter at €199 a month or €159 a month billed annually, Professional at €349 or €279, and Enterprise on request, all excluding VAT.

Does SimpleAct have a free trial?

Yes, 7 days with the full Starter scope. Its page says a credit card is required and the Starter subscription continues unless you cancel.

Is the GDPR module in SimpleAct Starter?

No. Its pricing page says the GDPR module is included from Professional.

Does EURO AI Register make my business compliant?

No tool can. It is a record-keeping aid: an AI inventory, an Article 4 literacy record and dated notes. It is not legal advice, an audit or a conformity assessment.

When do the EU AI Act high-risk rules apply?

After Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027 and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028. Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025.
