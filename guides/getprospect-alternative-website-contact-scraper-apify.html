GetProspect or an Apify Actor? An email finder with credits vs the contacts a company publishes

Competitor facts below come only from their own public pages, read on 27 Oct 2026; each is linked in Sources. We did not log in, start trials or read paywalled material. Short answer: GetProspect is an email finder and verifier: you find work emails of people from LinkedIn, by name and domain, or by enriching a CSV, then verify and send them to your CRM. Its pricing page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows Free at $0 (50 valid emails and 100 verifications a month), Starter $49 a month (1,000 valid emails, 2,000 verifications, 5 phone numbers), Growth 5k $99 a month (5,000 emails, 10,000 verifications), Growth 20k $199 and Growth 50k $399; it says annual billing saves 30%. You pay only for valid emails found, and unused credits roll over up to one monthly allowance. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify is not an email finder: it reads each company's own website and returns the role emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers and social links that site publishes, with the page each came from. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need named people's emails from LinkedIn, pick GetProspect. If you have company websites and want what each business publishes, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I don't need the CEO's private work email. I need the number and email the dental clinic puts on its own contact page, for 300 clinics, in one sheet.” An email finder starts from a person. A website reader starts from the business.

Who should pick GetProspect instead

  • You prospect people on LinkedIn and want their work emails verified in one click.
  • You need a free tier with 50 valid emails a month to start.
  • You want built-in CRM sync and an email verifier in the same tool.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your targets are local businesses whose websites list a general email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • You want every contact with the source page next to it.
  • You'd rather pay for compute on Apify than a monthly credit plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GetProspect cost?

Its pricing page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows Free at $0 (50 valid emails and 100 verifications a month), Starter $49 a month (1,000 valid emails, 2,000 verifications, 5 phone numbers), Growth 5k $99 a month, Growth 20k $199 and Growth 50k $399. It says annual billing saves 30%.

Does GetProspect charge for emails it cannot find?

Its FAQ says you are charged only for valid emails found, and unused credits roll over up to one monthly allowance.

Does the Unadhi Actor find personal work emails?

No. It reads only what each company publishes on its own website, and shows the page each contact came from.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Can I use both?

Yes. Use our Actor for company-level contacts, then a people finder for the few named contacts you need.
