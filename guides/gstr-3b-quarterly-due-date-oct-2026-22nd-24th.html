GSTR-3B quarterly due date, Oct 2026: the 22nd or the 24th, depending on your state

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · India

Last checked: 11 Oct 2026 against GST portal: GSTR-3B user guide and GSTN QRMP advisory (state groups).

Short answer. If you file GST quarterly under the QRMP scheme, GSTR-3B for July–September 2026 is due on 22 October 2026 if your principal place of business is in a 22nd-group state (including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Maharashtra, Gujarat), or 24 October 2026 in a 24th-group state (including Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, West Bengal). Monthly filers file GSTR-3B by the 20th of the following month in every state (GST portal GSTR-3B guide; state groups in the GSTN QRMP advisory). As of 13 Oct 2026 we found no official notification extending these dates.

Don't let the date slip. A late GSTR-3B costs ₹50 a day (₹20 for a nil return) in late fee, plus 18% a year interest on tax paid in cash. Check yours with the free late fee calculator.

“For quarterly filers, due date for filing of Form GSTR-3B, as notified for different States/UTs, is 22nd and 24th day of the month following the quarter for which the return pertains.”

— GST portal, Form GSTR-3B user guide (checked 13 Oct 2026)

“if you opt for QRMP scheme, both Form GSTR-1 and Form GSTR-3B will be required to be filed at quarterly frequency.”

— GST portal, FAQs on QRMP profile (checked 13 Oct 2026)

Why two dates?

The split spreads the load on the GST portal so that the whole country does not file on one day. Your date depends on where your principal place of business is registered, not where you sell.

Common mistakes

  • Copying a date from a national calendar. Many lists show only one quarterly date. Check your state's group.
  • Believing rumours of an extension. Extensions come by notification and appear on gst.gov.in. Until you see it there, file on time.
  • Filing GSTR-3B before GSTR-1 is right. Mismatches between the two are a common reason for notices.
  • Skipping PMT-06 in months 1 and 2. QRMP is quarterly filing but monthly payment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GSTR-3B quarterly due date for Telangana?

The 22nd of the month after the quarter: 22 October 2026 for July–September 2026 and 22 January 2027 for October–December 2026.

What is the GSTR-3B quarterly due date for Delhi?

The 24th of the month after the quarter: 24 October 2026 for July–September 2026.

What is the monthly GSTR-3B due date?

The 20th of the following month in every state.

Has the October 2026 quarterly GSTR-3B date been extended?

As of 13 October 2026 we found no CBIC notification extending it. Check the GST portal before filing.
