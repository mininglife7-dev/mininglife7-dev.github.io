An Employees tab works out each person's EPF, EPS, EDLI and admin amounts from their PF wages, and flags staff in the ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 band who must be enrolled. Short answer (checked 27 Oct 2026): EPF for October 2026 wages is due within 15 days of the close of the month, so by Sunday 15 November 2026; aim to pay by Friday 13 November. From 17 September 2026 the statutory wage ceiling went up from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month (S.O. 5109(E)). EPFO's own FAQ says October is the first wage month to use the new ceiling for the full month, so a ₹20,000 employee now costs ₹2,400 employee share plus ₹1,666 EPS and ₹734 EPF from the employer, and anyone earning up to ₹25,000 in PF wages must be a member of EPF, EPS and EDLI. Late payment carries simple interest at 12% a year (S.O. 2698(E), 29 May 2026, deemed from 21 November 2025), and damages can be added on top.

What changed for October

  • Ceiling ₹25,000: contributions are worked on PF wages (basic, DA and retaining allowance, as defined in section 2(88) of the Code on Social Security, 2020), not gross salary, up to ₹25,000. EPFO's FAQ: a person with ₹50,000 gross but ₹25,000 PF wages must be covered; one with ₹30,000 PF wages need not be (they can opt in with employer consent).
  • New members: employees already working on 17 September 2026 with PF wages up to ₹25,000 who were "excluded" before must be enrolled in EPF, EPS and EDLI from 17 September. The employer has to do it; the employee does not apply.
  • EPS for the ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 band: an EPF member who was outside EPS and is in this band now joins EPS, so 8.33% of the employer share moves to EPS and only 3.67% stays in EPF.
  • Above ₹25,000: contributions may stay capped at ₹25,000, but staff already contributing on higher wages keep their arrangement. Do not cut them back to ₹25,000.
  • September carry-over: the September 2026 employee share that could not be deducted may be recovered from the next payroll (EPFO FAQ Q10 and Q11). That is a payroll adjustment only; it does not belong in the October ECR.
  • Admin charges: 0.5% of PF wages, with a minimum of ₹500 a month for an establishment with at least one contributing member (₹75 with none).

Damages: on top of interest, damages can be levied for late payment after a hearing. Since 14 June 2024 the EPF Scheme rate has been a monthly percentage of the arrears (paragraph 32A as amended by G.S.R. 329(E)), and secondary sources report a graded monthly table in the EPF Scheme, 2026. We could not open the 2026 gazette text, so the sheet does not estimate damages. Check the scheme before you quote a figure to a client.

Made-up example (not a real client)

Client "Sample Packaging" has 9 staff. Four earn ₹12,000 PF wages and were already members; each costs ₹1,440 employee share, ₹1,000 EPS, ₹440 EPF, ₹60 EDLI and ₹60 admin. Three earn ₹20,000 PF wages: two were members contributing on ₹15,000 and now contribute on ₹20,000; the third was "excluded" and had no UAN, so the Check column says ENROL: no UAN. Each costs ₹2,400, ₹1,666, ₹734, ₹100 and ₹100. Two managers have ₹40,000 PF wages and no higher-wage arrangement, so each is worked on ₹25,000: ₹3,000 employee share, ₹0 EPS (not EPS members), ₹3,000 EPF, ₹125 EDLI and ₹125 admin. Admin works out to ₹790, above the ₹500 minimum. Total challan: ₹39,500. Paid on Friday 13 November: OK. Paid on 25 November: 10 days late, interest = 39,500 × 12% × 10 / 365 = about ₹130, plus possible damages.
