December 2026 due-date chase list for CA firms: every client deadline from 7 to 31 December on one sheet

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 20 Oct 2026): December 2026 has eleven client deadlines on nine dates that a typical CA firm has to chase. Two of those dates fall on a Sunday and one on Christmas Day. The ones you can rely on: TDS/TCS for November deposited by Mon 7 Dec (rule 218, Income-tax Rules, 2026), GSTR-1 by Fri 11 Dec and GSTR-3B by Sun 20 Dec for monthly filers (GST portal), the third advance tax instalment (at least 75% cumulative) by Tue 15 Dec (e-filing portal FAQ), Form 141 challan-cum-statements for November deductions by Wed 30 Dec (rule 218(3)), and on Thu 31 Dec both the FY 2025-26 GST annual return (GSTR-9 FAQ) and the last date for a belated AY 2026-27 income-tax return (Income Tax Department, periods of limitation). This is a starting point, not tax advice. Confirm each date on the portal before you file.

About the Sunday and holiday dates: 13 and 20 December fall on a Sunday and 25 December is Christmas Day. Do not plan on a date moving because of the weekend or the holiday. Aim for the working day before, as in the last column, and treat any later relief as a bonus.

The 31 December pile-up, in one line for clients: the FY 2025-26 GST annual return and the last belated AY 2026-27 return share one date, in the same week as the year-end holidays. Split them: annual returns first (they depend on books and GSTR-1/3B data already filed), belated and revised income-tax returns next, both done by Tue 29 Dec. Any FY 2025-26 credit or correction work had to be done by 30 November (see the November chase list), so GSTR-9 in December should report, not fix.

The easy mistake this month: sending every advance-tax client the same 15 December reminder. Presumptive-scheme clients normally pay once, by 15 March, and clients whose tax after TDS/TCS is under ₹10,000 do not pay advance tax at all. Sort them out first, using the advance tax reminder plan. And if a company client held its AGM after 30 September (for example under an extension), count AOC-4 (30 days) and MGT-7 (60 days) from that actual AGM date; see the ROC chase list.

Collect list

  • TDS: November payment list with deductee PAN, section and amount; challan details once paid
  • Form 141 cases: buyer and seller PAN, property or rent details and payment dates for November deductions
  • GST monthly: November sales register, credit and debit notes, e-invoice and e-way bill exceptions
  • QRMP: B2B invoices for November (for IFF) and the chosen PMT-06 method (fixed sum or self-assessment)
  • Advance tax: April–November profit (or books to 30 Nov), capital gains since April, other income outside the books, TDS/TCS credit so far, and the 15 Jun and 15 Sep challans
  • Employers: November wage sheet, joiners and leavers, UAN and IP numbers
  • GSTR-9/9C: FY 2025-26 books, filed GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B summaries, HSN-wise summary, and the reconciliation for clients that need GSTR-9C
  • Belated or revised ITR: AIS/TIS and Form 26AS, the original acknowledgement (for revisions), the list of changes, and the person who will e-verify

WhatsApp / email script pack

Your December dates: "Hello. Your compliance dates for December: [list only theirs]. The year-end holidays fall in the last week, so please send [items] by [date in the first week]. We will confirm each filing as it is done."

Advance tax: "Based on your April–November figures, we estimate ₹[amount] for the 15 December advance tax instalment. Here is how we got it: [two lines]. Reply YES and we will guide the payment by Friday 11 December."

Property or rent TDS: "Tax you deducted in November on [rent / the property purchase] has to be paid with Form 141 by 30 December. Please send the payment date and the other party's PAN by [date], and we will prepare it for Monday 28 December."
