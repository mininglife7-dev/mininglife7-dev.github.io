Short answer (checked 22 Oct 2026): under the Income-tax Rules, 2026, the non-PAN declaration Form 60 is now Form 97 and the half-yearly statement Form 61 is now Form 98. A person covered by rule 160 who received any Form 97 declaration between 1 April and 30 September must file Form 98 online by 31 October (rule 160(3)(a)); declarations received by 31 March go by 30 April. For this half-year that means 31 October 2026, a Saturday. Covered persons are those in column 4 of the rule 159 table at Sl. Nos. 11 to 14, and at Sl. Nos. 15 and 16 only if their accounts must be audited (rule 160(2)). Form 97 itself must be kept for six years from the end of the financial year (rule 160(1)(b)). Sources: Rule 159 and Rule 160 (Income Tax Department) and the Directorate of Income Tax (Systems) Notification No. 3 of 2026 (15 September 2026). This is a starting point, not tax advice.

How filing works (Notification No. 3 of 2026)

  • Register once: log in to the e-filing portal with the client's own login, open "Pending Actions" → "Reporting Portal", enter form type, category, address and principal officer. An ITDREIN is generated and cannot be deactivated. Clients already registered for Form 61 keep their ITDREIN and principal officer; no new registration.
  • Prepare and upload: download the Form 98 schema, Report Generation and Validation Utility and Generic Submission Utility from the "Resources" tab of the Reporting Portal. The statement is digitally signed and uploaded by the principal officer (PAN login).
  • Fix errors: if the Data Quality Report shows "Reports Requiring Correction", file a correction statement until the count is zero.
  • Wrong report filed: file a "Deletion Statement" with the exact values filed earlier.

Nine-day plan (22–31 October 2026)

  • Thu 22 – Fri 23 Oct: fill column B and C for every client with hotel, restaurant, banquet, event, jewellery, high-value retail, co-operative bank, Nidhi or NBFC business. Send message 1.
  • Sat 24 – Mon 26 Oct: log every Form 97 in tab "declarations"; check column G so only needed declarations are reported.
  • Tue 27 Oct: register any client showing "Register on Reporting Portal first"; confirm the principal officer's DSC works (message 2).
  • Wed 28 – Fri 30 Oct: prepare, validate and upload Form 98; write the acknowledgement number in column G. 31 October is a Saturday, so aim to finish by Friday.
  • After upload: check the Data Quality Report and clear any RRC.

Notes

  • Rule 160 applies to a person who "has received any declaration in Form No. 97". We did not find a rule requiring a nil statement; confirm on the Reporting Portal before deciding not to file.
  • Penalties and late fees are not covered here; check them in the Act before advising a client who missed the date.
  • Declarations received in FY 2025-26 or earlier stay on Form 61 under the 1962 Rules (Notification No. 3 of 2026, point (f)).
  • The sheet holds customer names and amounts: share it only inside your firm.
