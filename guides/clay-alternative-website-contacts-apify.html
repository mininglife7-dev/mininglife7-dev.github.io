Frequently asked questions

What is a simple Clay alternative for company website contacts?

If all you need is the contact details a business publishes on its own website, Unadhi's Website Contact Scraper on Apify does that one job: role emails, phones normalised to E.164, WhatsApp numbers and social pages, with the source page recorded. Our Actor is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage, and Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month. Clay suits multi-provider, people-level enrichment.

How much does Clay cost in 2026?

Clay's pricing page, read on 14 Oct 2026, has a Free plan with 100 data credits and 500 actions a month and up to 200 rows per table. Its FAQ says Launch starts at $185 a month and Growth at $495 a month, though plan cards show other slider-based figures. Enterprise is custom; check the live page.

Does Clay's free plan include phone numbers?

No. According to Clay's plan table, phone enrichment is available on Launch and above, not on the Free plan. The Unadhi Actor returns phone and WhatsApp numbers only when the business has published them on its own website, and it normalises them to E.164 format.

Can I use the Apify contact scraper together with Clay?

Possibly, but Unadhi has not tested an integration and makes no claim about one. The simple path is to export the Actor's dataset as a CSV file and import it into your table. Check the rows by hand against a couple of websites before you run hundreds.

Does the Actor return personal email addresses?

Not by default. Only shared role mailboxes such as info@ or sales@ are returned; personal addresses are dropped and counted. You can switch them on, but only with a lawful basis under India's DPDP Act 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR, and you stay responsible for how you use the output.
