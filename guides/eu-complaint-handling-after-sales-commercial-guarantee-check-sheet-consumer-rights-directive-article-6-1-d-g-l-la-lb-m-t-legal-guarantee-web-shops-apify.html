Step 3: the five-minute checklist per shop

  1. Is a complaint-handling policy shown before the consumer is bound? (E / Article 6(1)(g) — separate from the delivery half of (g) covered in the delivery sheet.)
  2. Is the complaint address given, or clearly the same as the establishment address? (F / Article 6(1)(d).)
  3. For goods: is there a prominent reminder of the legal guarantee of conformity, including the minimum two-year duration under Directive (EU) 2019/771? (G / Article 6(1)(l).)
  4. If the producer offers a free commercial guarantee of durability >2 years and told the trader: is that disclosed with duration + legal-guarantee reminder? (H / Article 6(1)(la).) If none is offered, mark N/A (none offered).
  5. For digital content / digital services: is the legal-guarantee reminder present? (I / Article 6(1)(lb).)
  6. Where the shop offers after-sale assistance, after-sales services or commercial guarantees, are existence and conditions stated? (J / Article 6(1)(m).)
  7. Where the trader is subject to an out-of-court complaint and redress mechanism, is that stated with how to access it? (K / Article 6(1)(t).)
  8. Take dated screenshots of product, terms, guarantee and contact pages (Article 6(9) burden of proof on the trader).

Frequently asked questions

Must an EU distance web shop tell consumers about its complaint-handling policy before they order?

Article 6(1)(g) of Directive 2011/83/EU requires, before the consumer is bound by a distance or off-premises contract, information on the arrangements for payment, delivery, performance, the time by which the trader undertakes to deliver, and, where applicable, the trader’s complaint handling policy. The delivery half of (g) is in our delivery check sheet; this sheet focuses on the complaint-handling policy.

What must a shop say about the legal guarantee of conformity for goods?

Article 6(1)(l) requires a reminder of the existence of the legal guarantee of conformity for goods and its main elements, including its minimum duration of two years as provided in Directive (EU) 2019/771, in a prominent manner, using the harmonised notice referred to in Article 22a. SGD Article 10 sets the two-year liability period for lack of conformity that becomes apparent within two years of delivery.

What is the commercial guarantee of durability in Article 6(1)(la)?

Where the producer offers a commercial guarantee of durability at no additional cost, covering the entire good for more than two years, and makes that information available to the trader, Article 6(1)(la) requires the trader to disclose that benefit, its duration, and a reminder of the legal guarantee, using the harmonised label in Article 22a. SGD Article 17 covers commercial guarantee statements.

What does Article 6(1)(m) add beyond the legal guarantee?

Where applicable, information on the existence and the conditions of after sale customer assistance, after-sales services and commercial guarantees — separate from the mandatory legal guarantee reminder in (l)/(lb).

Do shops also have to mention out-of-court complaint bodies?

Article 6(1)(t) requires, where applicable, information on recourse to an out-of-court complaint and redress mechanism to which the trader is subject, and how to access it. If not subject, mark N/A (not subject) and keep evidence.

Is the complaint address the same as the contact details sheet?

Article 6(1)(d) covers the geographical address where the consumer can address complaints if different from the establishment address in 6(1)(c). The contact/hotline sheet covers phone, email and Art 21; this sheet only checks the complaint-address point.
