Advance tax due dates for FY 2026-27 (tax year 2026-27): 15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar

Last updated 14 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. If your tax liability for the year (after TDS/TCS) is ₹10,000 or more, you pay advance tax (Income Tax e-filing: Tax payments FAQ, ss.404–408 of the Income-tax Act, 2025). For tax year 2026-27 (FY 2026-27) the usual instalments fall on 15 June, 15 September, 15 December and 15 March. The first instalment for tax year 2026-27 is due 15 June 2026 and is governed by the Income-tax Act, 2025. Presumptive taxpayers (s.58) generally pay the whole advance tax by 15 March.

“Under Section 404 of the Income Tax Act, 2025, advance tax is payable if the amount of tax payable during the year, computed under the advance tax provisions, is Rs. 10,000 or more.”

— Income Tax e-filing portal, Tax payments FAQ Q17 (checked 14 Oct 2026)

When you pay on the portal, pick the correct tax year / assessment year so credit lands in the right year (FAQ Q22).

Presumptive businesses

If you opt for the presumptive scheme under section 58 of the new Act, you must discharge the entire advance tax in one instalment on or before 15 March of that financial year (FAQ Q19 / s.408(2)).

Interest if you defer

Interest for shortfall or deferment continues at the same rates as under the old Act: roughly s.424 (old 234B) and s.425 (old 234C) — 1% per month-type charges as explained in FAQ Q20. Plan instalments so you are not guessing in March.

Common mistakes

  • Selecting the wrong year on the challan during the 2026-27 transition.
  • Ignoring TDS already deducted when estimating advance tax.
  • Presumptive filers paying in four instalments when the rule is one shot by 15 March.
  • Waiting for a demand. Advance tax is self-computed.

Checklist

  • Estimate annual tax after TDS; if ≥ ₹10,000, diarise the four dates
  • 15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar on the calendar (or 15 Mar only if presumptive)
  • Correct tax year selected on the payment screen
  • Challan / CIN saved against books

Frequently asked questions

What are the advance tax dates for FY 2026-27?

15 June 2026, 15 September 2026, 15 December 2026 and 15 March 2027 for the normal four-instalment schedule.

What is the advance tax threshold?

₹10,000 of tax payable for the year under the advance-tax computation (s.404 of the Income-tax Act, 2025).

Which Act applies to the 15 June 2026 instalment?

The Income-tax Act, 2025, because it relates to income of tax year 2026-27.

I am on presumptive tax. When do I pay advance tax?

The entire advance tax by 15 March of that financial year (s.408(2) / portal FAQ Q19).
