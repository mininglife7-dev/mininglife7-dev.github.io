New client onboarding sheet for CA firms: 12 details that decide every due date

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer: a client's due dates are decided by a handful of facts: entity type, GST registration and filing frequency, state of registration, whether they deduct TDS, whether they are under tax audit, and who on their side answers messages. Collect those 12 details once, on the first day, and keep them in one row per client. Every GST, TDS, income-tax and ROC reminder can then be built from that row instead of from memory.

Turn the row into a reminder rhythm

  • Monthly GST filer: reminders before GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month.
  • QRMP filer: monthly tax-payment reminders plus quarterly return reminders, using the due-date group for the client's state.
  • TDS deductor: a challan reminder every month and a statement reminder every quarter.
  • Advance tax "yes": four instalment reminders, each about two weeks ahead.
  • Tax audit "yes": an early books-closing reminder well before the audit report date.

Write the actual dates from the portals, not from this list. Due dates move through extensions and notifications, so re-check each one before you message the client.

A one-line onboarding message you can send

"Welcome aboard. To set your filing reminders correctly, please send: PAN, GST registration copy (REG-06) for each state, your GST return frequency screen, TAN letter if you deduct TDS, and the name and WhatsApp number of the person who sends us documents. We will confirm your calendar within two working days."

  • All 12 details filled, or marked "not applicable" on purpose
  • GST frequency re-checked at the start of each quarter
  • Calendar sent to the client and acknowledged
  • Owner on your team named for the client
