Why this matters now (dates (source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689))

  • Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 to providers and deployers, whatever the risk level. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, it asks organisations to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff; it does not require them to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The European Commission's Q&A says there is no need for a certificate: an internal record of trainings or guidance is enough, and national authorities supervise and enforce from August 2026 (Commission AI literacy Q&A).
  • Article 50 transparency duties (for example, telling people they are talking to a chatbot) apply from 2 August 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
  • High-risk rules were moved by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III uses (such as recruitment and credit scoring) from 2 December 2027, and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028.

Frequently asked questions

EURO AI Register or Veritome: which should a small business choose?

A small deployer that needs an AI inventory, an Article 4 literacy record and the key dates can start with EURO AI Register. EURO AI Register is free for up to 10 AI systems, including the printable Article 4 literacy record; beyond that it is $19 a month or $190 a year flat, with unlimited systems. A high-risk provider, or a team needing a multi-user EU-hosted workspace with ISO/IEC 42001 programmes, should look at Veritome.

How much does Veritome cost?

Veritome's pricing page, read on 14 Oct 2026, lists Classify free for 3 AI systems, Starter at €79 a month or €66 billed annually, Govern at €199 a month and Manage at €599 a month, excluding VAT. Enterprise is by quote. A 30% founding rate applies to organisations created before 1 December 2026.

Which EU AI Act tool has the bigger free tier, Veritome or EURO AI Register?

On the public pages read on 14 Oct 2026, Veritome Classify covers 3 AI systems, with a view-only obligation list and a watermarked report. EURO AI Register's free edition covers 10 AI systems and 10 evidence records, including the printable Article 4 literacy record, for a single user.

Where is my data stored in Veritome and EURO AI Register?

Veritome's pricing page says data lives in the EEA, on Hetzner in Germany, with AI processing on Mistral in France. EURO AI Register keeps everything only in your browser on your device, with no account and no upload; teams share JSON backups.

Does Veritome or EURO AI Register certify us as compliant?

No. Veritome states it is not a certification body. EURO AI Register is a record-keeping tool, not proof of compliance, not an audit and not a conformity assessment. The AI Act has no organisation-wide seal that a tool can give you; for possible high-risk systems, consult a qualified lawyer.
