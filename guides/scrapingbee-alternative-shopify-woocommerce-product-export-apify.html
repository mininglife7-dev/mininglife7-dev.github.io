Short answer: ScrapingBee is a web scraping API: your code sends it a URL and gets back the page, with proxies, JavaScript rendering, screenshots and extraction rules handled for you, plus dedicated APIs for Google, Amazon, Walmart and others. Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists Hobby at $19 a month (75,000 API credits, 25 concurrent requests), Freelance $49 (250,000 credits), Startup $99 (1,000,000), Business $249 (3,000,000) and Business+ $599 (8,000,000), with Enterprise plans from $999. Credits last one month; a request costs 1 credit, 5 with JavaScript rendering and 10 or 25 with premium proxies. It offers 1,000 free API credits with no card. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does one job with no code: give it shop addresses and it returns product rows (titles, prices, compare-at prices, variants, stock, SKU/GTIN and images where the shop shows them) as a spreadsheet. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It is made for independent Shopify, WooCommerce and schema.org shops, not marketplaces such as Amazon. If you are a developer building your own scraper for many kinds of sites, ScrapingBee is built for that. If you want competitors' Shopify or WooCommerce catalogues in a sheet today, try ours.

Who should pick ScrapingBee instead

  • You are a developer and want to scrape many kinds of sites from your own code.
  • You need Amazon, Walmart or Google results, or screenshots.
  • You need high volumes with concurrency on a monthly plan.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want no code: shop addresses in, a spreadsheet out.
