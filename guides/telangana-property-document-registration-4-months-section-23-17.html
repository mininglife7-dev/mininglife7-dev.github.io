Registering a property document in Telangana: what must be registered, the 4-month deadline and the prohibited-property check

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Under section 17 of the Registration Act 1908, documents such as a sale deed, gift deed, mortgage and leases above one year must be registered. Under section 23 a document must be presented for registration within 4 months of its execution; section 25 allows a further 4 months only with a fine. An unregistered compulsorily-registrable document does not pass title (section 49). In Telangana, check the prohibited properties list (section 22-A) and the encumbrance details on the official portal, registration.telangana.gov.in, before paying.

Before you sign

  1. Check the prohibited properties list (government, endowment, assigned land etc.) on the Telangana registration portal.
  2. Search the encumbrance details for the survey/plot number over a long period.
  3. Match the seller's name with previous registered deeds and tax receipts.
  4. For apartments and layouts, check the project's TG-RERA registration.

Registration day

  • Book the slot and pay stamp duty and fees as calculated by the portal (rates are notified by the state; use the portal's calculator, not a broker's figure)
  • Seller and buyer appear with ID; witnesses as required
  • Biometrics and photographs are captured at the Sub-Registrar office
  • Collect the registered document and check every detail

Common mistakes

  • Signing an agreement and waiting. The 4-month clock starts at execution.
  • Skipping the 22-A check because "the layout is approved".
  • Paying a large advance on an unregistered agreement with no encumbrance check.
  • Typos in survey numbers or boundaries that need a rectification deed later.

Checklist

  • Prohibited property check done
  • Encumbrance and previous deeds verified
  • RERA check (projects)
  • Stamp duty from the official calculator
  • Presented within 4 months of execution

Frequently asked questions

What is the time limit to register a document after signing?

It must be presented within 4 months of execution (section 23, Registration Act 1908); a further 4 months is possible only with a fine (section 25).

Which property documents must be registered?

Those listed in section 17, including sale and gift deeds of immovable property, mortgages and leases for more than one year.

What is section 22-A?

A provision in the Registration Act as applied in Telangana (and Andhra Pradesh) that prohibits registration of notified properties such as government and endowment land.

Where do I check property registration details in Telangana?

On the official Registration and Stamps Department portal, registration.telangana.gov.in.
