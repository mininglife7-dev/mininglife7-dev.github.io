The pain this page is about

Visual scrapers can read almost anything, but every shop needs its own project, and projects break when a theme changes. Shopify and WooCommerce shops already publish their catalogue in a structured form; reading that is simpler.

Who should pick ParseHub instead

  • The sites you need are not Shopify or WooCommerce shops — directories, listings, custom catalogues.
  • You want a visual tool to pick any field on any page, or ParseHub's team to build and deliver the data for you.
  • You need more than product data, for example images and files saved to Dropbox or S3 (paid plans).

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your targets are independent Shopify or WooCommerce shops and you want every product with price, compare-at price, stock and SKU/GTIN.

Frequently asked questions

Is ParseHub free?

ParseHub has a free plan (read 20 Oct 2026): 200 pages per run, 5 public projects and 14-day data retention, no credit card. Paid plans are Standard at $189 a month and Professional at $599 a month.

Do I need to build a project for each shop with the Unadhi Actor?

No. You paste shop URLs. The Actor detects Shopify or WooCommerce and reads their structured product data; other shops are read from schema.org markup where available.

Can the Unadhi Actor scrape any website like ParseHub?

No. It is built only for online shops' product data and refuses marketplaces. For other kinds of sites, a general scraper such as ParseHub is the right tool.

What does a run of the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify platform usage; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month. Run three shops first and read the usage in Apify billing.

Not legal advice. Check each shop's terms before you collect its data; the Actor skips shops whose robots.txt or terms forbid it.
