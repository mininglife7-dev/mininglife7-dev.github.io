Short answer (checked 24 Oct 2026): from 1 April 2025, an office of a business that receives tax invoices for input services (including reverse-charge services) for or on behalf of its other GSTINs under the same PAN must register as an ISD and distribute that credit (section 20(1) CGST Act, as substituted by the Finance Act 2024). Credit available in a month is distributed in the same month and reported in Form GSTR-6, due by the 13th of the next month, so 13 November 2026 for October 2026. A nil GSTR-6 is still required when there is nothing to distribute.

What the rules say

  • Who must register, section 20(1). "Any office of the supplier ... which receives tax invoices towards the receipt of input services, including invoices in respect of services liable to tax under sub-section (3) or sub-section (4) of section 9 ... for or on behalf of distinct persons referred to in section 25, shall be required to be registered as Input Service Distributor under clause (viii) of section 24 and shall distribute the input tax credit in respect of such invoices." Section 12 of the Finance Act 2024 substituted this section with effect from 1 April 2025 (Notification 16/2024-Central Tax).
  • Central tax and integrated tax, section 20(3). Central tax is distributed as central tax or integrated tax, and integrated tax as integrated tax or central tax, through a document showing the credit distributed.
  • Same month, rule 39(1)(a)-(b). Credit available for distribution in a month is distributed in the same month and reported in GSTR-6. The amount distributed cannot exceed the credit available.
  • Who gets it, rule 39(1)(c)-(f). Credit for a service used by one unit goes only to that unit. Credit for a service used by some or all units is shared pro rata on each unit's turnover in its State or UT during the relevant period: C1 = (t1 / T) × C. Units that make exempt supplies or are not registered are counted in T too.
  • Relevant period (Explanation to rule 39). The previous financial year if the recipients had turnover in it, so FY 2025-26 for credit distributed in October 2026. If some or all recipients had no turnover in that year, use the last quarter for which turnover of all recipients is available before the month of distribution.
  • Eligible and ineligible, rule 39(1)(g)-(h). Ineligible credit (for example under section 17(5)) and eligible credit are distributed separately, and each tax head separately.
  • Which head the unit gets, rule 39(1)(i)-(j). IGST credit goes out as IGST to every unit. CGST and SGST/UTGST go out as CGST and SGST/UTGST to a unit in the same State as the ISD, and as IGST (equal to CGST + SGST) to a unit in another State.
  • Documents, rule 39(1)(k)-(n). The ISD issues an ISD invoice (rule 54(1)) marked as issued only for distribution of credit, and an ISD credit note when distributed credit is reduced. A supplier's credit note is apportioned in the same ratio as the original invoice. If the amount comes out negative, it is added to the unit's output tax liability.
  • Reverse-charge services, rule 39(1A). A unit with the same PAN and State code as the ISD that paid tax under reverse charge on common input services may issue an invoice or note under rule 54(1A) to pass that credit to the ISD, which then distributes it under rule 39(1). The GSTR-6 FAQ (Q10) adds that reverse charge liability does not apply to the ISD registration itself.
  • Return, section 39(4) and the portal. An ISD files a return for every calendar month or part of a month within thirteen days after the end of the month. The FAQ says GSTR-6 is mandatory every month (nil if nothing is distributed), can be filed even if suppliers have not filed GSTR-1, has no electronic credit ledger, and needs DSC for companies and LLPs. Table 3 is for invoices received, Table 6B for credit and debit notes, and Tables 5 and 8 for ISD invoices and ISD credit notes.
  • Late fee. Section 47(1) sets ₹100 a day up to ₹5,000 for a late section 39 return. The portal shows the amount in Table 10 after filing, and the FAQ (Q29) says it is for the current period only. We did not check for any waiver notification for GSTR-6, or for the State/UT part, so check before you quote a figure to a client.
