Short answer: They solve different problems. Anymail Finder finds and verifies email addresses: a named person's work email from a name and domain, or up to 20 company emails for one domain. Its pricing page (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows monthly plans of $29 (400 credits), $49 (1,000), $89 (2,000), $149 (5,000) and $199 (10,000), yearly plans billed upfront at about 33% off, prices excluding tax, and a 7-day trial with 100 credits that needs a card. It charges a credit only when a verified email is found, and 0.2 credits to verify an address from elsewhere. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does not guess or verify personal addresses: it reads each company's own website and returns the role emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers and social links published there, with the page each one came from. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need a named decision-maker's email, pick Anymail Finder. If you need the contacts a business itself publishes, including phones and WhatsApp, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I have 300 company websites. I need a way to reach each business, and my list is mostly small Indian firms that answer WhatsApp faster than email.” An email finder answers “what is this person's work address?”. A website contact scraper answers “what contact details does this business publish?”. Pick by the question you are asking.

Who should pick Anymail Finder instead

  • You need the work email of a named person (a founder, a buyer, a finance head), not the general inbox.
  • You already have emails from other sources and want them verified before a campaign.
  • Your team wants one shared credit pool, an API and a browser extension in the same account.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want only what the business itself publishes, with the source page of every detail, which is easier to explain under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR.
  • You need phones and WhatsApp numbers as well as email, for Indian SMEs, clinics, shops or dealers.
  • You already work in Apify and want to chain it after a Google Maps Actor and pay one platform bill.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Anymail Finder cost?

Its pricing page (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows monthly plans of $29 for 400 credits, $49 for 1,000, $89 for 2,000, $149 for 5,000 and $199 for 10,000, with larger plans from $299. Yearly plans are billed upfront at about 33% off. Prices exclude tax.

Does Anymail Finder charge when no email is found?

Its page says a search costs a credit only if a verified email is found. Verifying an address from another source costs 0.2 credits per check.

Is there an Anymail Finder free trial?

Monthly plans have a 7-day trial with 100 credits. Its page says your card is checked, not charged, and the plan starts after 7 days unless you cancel. Yearly plans have no trial.

Does the Unadhi Actor find a person's email from their name?

No. It does not guess or construct addresses. It reports the emails, phones and links a company's own website publishes, and keeps role inboxes only unless you turn personal addresses on.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
