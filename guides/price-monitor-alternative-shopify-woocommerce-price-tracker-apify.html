Short answer: Price Monitor (price-monitor.com) is competitive price monitoring and daily repricing for sellers on marketplaces; its FAQ says it supports the common marketplaces in Germany, with more on request, and that data is stored on DigitalOcean Frankfurt. Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €29 a month plus VAT (up to 250 products, 1 marketplace, 1 user, 90-day history, REST API 1,000 requests a day, no AI features), Growth at €149 a month (1,000 products, 2 marketplaces, 2 users, 2-year history, Smart Price Recommendations and AI Market Insights) and Pro at €349 a month (3,000 products, 2 marketplaces, 5 users, unlimited history, AI price forecast). Enterprise is priced on request. Every paid plan has a 14-day trial; a card is required but not charged during it. New prices reach your shop through a CSV or JSON feed or the API. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does a different job: give it competitor shop URLs (Shopify or WooCommerce sites, not marketplaces) and it returns their public product and price rows for your own sheet, plus a summary of skipped shops. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It does no repricing. If you sell on German marketplaces and want daily repricing, Price Monitor is built for that. If your competitors run their own Shopify or WooCommerce shops and you want their catalogues in a sheet, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Other sellers undercut us on the marketplace listing every morning.” That is a marketplace repricing job. “What do the three independent shops in our niche charge for their whole range?” is a shop-catalogue export job.

Who should pick Price Monitor instead

  • You sell on German marketplaces and other sellers share your listings.
  • You want daily repricing fed back to your shop system.
  • You want AI price recommendations and a long price history (Growth and up).

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your competitors are independent Shopify or WooCommerce shops, not marketplace sellers.
  • You want their whole public catalogue in a spreadsheet.
  • You want to start with no card and no monthly plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Price Monitor cost?

Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €29 a month, Growth at €149 and Pro at €349, plus VAT and billed monthly; Enterprise is priced on request.

Which marketplaces does Price Monitor support?

Its FAQ says the common marketplaces in Germany, with further marketplaces on request and custom adapters for Enterprise.

Does the Unadhi Actor read marketplace listings?

No. It reads public product and price data from Shopify and WooCommerce shop sites you list.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 17 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
