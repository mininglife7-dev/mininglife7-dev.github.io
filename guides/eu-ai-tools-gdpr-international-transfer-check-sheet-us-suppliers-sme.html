Your AI tool sends customer data to the US or elsewhere outside Europe: a GDPR international transfer check sheet for small EU businesses (Chapter V)

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the EURO AI Register mentioned below is an Unadhi product with a free edition. This guide is for information only, not legal advice.

What you get: a finished transfer check sheet with one row per AI tool: a "is it a transfer?" test in three questions, a tool table (supplier, where data goes, legal basis, date checked), a basis table saying exactly what to check and what proof to keep for adequacy, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, the privacy notice line and the records line to copy, and five questions to send the supplier.

The short answer

When personal data leaves the EEA for an AI supplier, the GDPR's transfer rules in Chapter V apply (Article 44). Each transfer needs a basis: an adequacy decision (Article 45), appropriate safeguards such as the Commission's standard contractual clauses (Article 46), or, only in specific situations, a derogation (Article 49). For US suppliers, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework counts as adequacy only if the supplier is on the official list. Tell people about transfers in your privacy notice (Article 13(1)(f)) and list them in your records (Article 30(1)(e)).

Step 1: is it a transfer? Three questions

QuestionYes / no
1. Is your business subject to the GDPR for this processing? (An EU business usually is.)[..]
2. Do you send personal data to the AI supplier, or make it available to it, for example customer chats, emails, call recordings, CVs?[..]
3. Is the supplier, or anyone it uses (sub-processor, support team with remote access), in a country outside the EEA?[..]

Three yes answers mean a transfer under Chapter V (EDPB Guidelines 05/2021, version 2.0, adopted 14 February 2023). If the supplier keeps everything in an EEA data region and no one outside the EEA can access it, there may be no transfer, but you still need the normal GDPR safeguards. Under Article 44 the rules also cover onward transfers, so ask about the supplier's own AI model providers.

Take the facts from the supplier's data processing agreement and its published sub-processor list. A processor needs your written authorisation before adding another processor and, under a general authorisation, must tell you about changes so you can object (Article 28(2)). Its contract must say it follows your documented instructions, including on transfers (Article 28(3)(a)).

Data Privacy Framework status on 21 October 2026: the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023) is in force. On 3 September 2025 the General Court dismissed an action for its annulment (Case T-553/23, Latombe v Commission). The applicant appealed to the Court of Justice on 31 October 2025 (Case C-703/25 P); when we checked, no judgment or Advocate General opinion had been published. If the decision were ever annulled, transfers relying on it would need another basis, so note in your sheet which suppliers also offer standard contractual clauses.

Step 4: two lines to copy

Privacy notice (Article 13(1)(f)):

We use [AI tool] provided by [supplier] to [purpose]. This involves transferring your [data types] to [country]. The transfer relies on [the European Commission's adequacy decision for [country] / the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in which [supplier] participates / the European Commission's standard contractual clauses]. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at [contact].

Records of processing (Article 30(1)(e)): "Transfers: [country], [supplier], basis [..], checked [date]." Add it to your Article 30 records sheet.

Step 5: five questions to send the supplier

  • In which countries is our data stored and processed, including backups, logs and support access?
  • Which sub-processors, including AI model providers, receive our data, and where are they?
  • What is your transfer basis for each: adequacy, Data Privacy Framework (exact legal entity listed) or standard contractual clauses (which module)?
  • Can you share your transfer impact assessment and the security measures in annex II?
  • Can we choose an EEA data region, and does that cover prompts, outputs and logs?
