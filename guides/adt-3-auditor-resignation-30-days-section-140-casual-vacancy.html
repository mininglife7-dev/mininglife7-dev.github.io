When a statutory auditor resigns: ADT-3 within 30 days and how the casual vacancy is filled

Last updated 23 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. An auditor who resigns must file a statement in Form ADT-3 with the company and the Registrar within 30 days of resignation, giving the reasons (section 140(2) of the Companies Act 2013). Failing to do so draws a penalty under section 140(3). The company's board fills the casual vacancy within 30 days; where the vacancy is caused by resignation, the appointment must also be approved by members at a general meeting within 3 months of the board's recommendation (section 139(8)). The new auditor's appointment is then reported in ADT-1.

The sequence

  1. Auditor sends the resignation letter to the company.
  2. Auditor files ADT-3 within 30 days, with reasons.
  3. Board meets, recommends a new auditor within 30 days of the vacancy.
  4. Members approve at an EGM or postal ballot within 3 months (vacancy by resignation).
  5. Company files ADT-1 for the new auditor; the new auditor holds office until the next AGM.

Common mistakes

  • Vague reasons such as "personal reasons" without detail; the ADT-3 reasons are read by the ROC and, for some companies, by regulators.
  • Board appoints, members never approve. For a resignation vacancy the members' approval within 3 months is not optional.
  • Appointing someone who is not eligible under s.141 (for example, holding securities of the company or indebted beyond the limit).
  • Waiting for the AGM. If accounts are due, the gap delays the audit and AOC-4.

Checklist

  • Resignation letter with reasons
  • ADT-3 by the outgoing auditor within 30 days
  • New auditor's consent and eligibility certificate (s.139(1) proviso)
  • Board resolution within 30 days; members' approval within 3 months; ADT-1

Frequently asked questions

Who files ADT-3, the company or the auditor?

The resigning auditor files ADT-3 with the company and the Registrar within 30 days of resignation (section 140(2)).

How is a casual vacancy of an auditor filled?

By the board within 30 days. If it arose from resignation, members must approve the appointment at a general meeting within 3 months of the board's recommendation (section 139(8)).

How long does the auditor appointed in a casual vacancy hold office?

Until the conclusion of the next annual general meeting.
