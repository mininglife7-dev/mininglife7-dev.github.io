GST registration threshold: ₹40 lakh, ₹20 lakh or ₹10 lakh, and who must register at any turnover

Last updated 20 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. The basic limit in section 22 of the CGST Act is ₹20 lakh aggregate turnover in a year (₹10 lakh in some special-category states). Since April 2019 a ₹40 lakh limit applies to businesses that supply only goods, in states that adopted it. Service providers stay at ₹20 lakh. Some businesses must register at any turnover, for example those making inter-state supplies of goods or selling through e-commerce platforms that collect TCS. Apply within 30 days of becoming liable.

Which limit is yours

You supplyUsual limit
Only goods (state adopted the higher limit)₹40 lakh
Services, or goods and services₹20 lakh
Special-category states (as notified)₹10 lakh / ₹20 lakh

Telangana: when the ₹40 lakh goods limit came in (2019), Telangana was among the states that kept ₹20 lakh for goods. Confirm the current position on the GST portal or with your CA before relying on either figure.

What counts in aggregate turnover

  • Taxable and exempt supplies, exports and inter-state supplies
  • All GSTINs on the same PAN across India
  • Not counted: the GST itself, and inward supplies on which you pay tax under reverse charge

Creators and freelancers: income from foreign clients or platforms that qualifies as export of services is zero-rated, but it still counts towards aggregate turnover.

Registration needed at any turnover (section 24, main cases)

  • Inter-state taxable supply of goods (inter-state services up to the threshold are exempt from registration)
  • Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons
  • Persons liable to pay tax under reverse charge
  • E-commerce operators, and sellers whose platform collects TCS (some relaxations are notified, so check yours)
  • Input Service Distributors and persons required to deduct TDS under GST

Common mistakes

  • Counting only this month's sales. The test is aggregate turnover in the financial year; once you cross it, the 30-day clock starts.
  • Leaving out exempt sales. Exempt supplies count towards the threshold.
  • Using the ₹40 lakh limit when you also sell a service. Even small service income moves you to ₹20 lakh.
  • Selling one order to another state without registering. Inter-state goods supply needs registration from the first sale.

Checklist

  • Goods only, services, or both?
  • Your state's limit confirmed
  • Turnover across all PAN branches added, including exports and exempt sales
  • Section 24 compulsory cases ruled out
  • REG-01 filed within 30 days of crossing

Frequently asked questions

What is the GST registration limit for services?

₹20 lakh aggregate turnover in a financial year (₹10 lakh in some special-category states).

Who gets the ₹40 lakh GST limit?

Businesses that supply only goods, in states that adopted the higher limit notified in 2019. Certain goods are excluded and compulsory-registration cases still apply.

Does export income count towards the GST threshold?

Yes. Exports are zero-rated but are included in aggregate turnover.

How soon must I apply after crossing the limit?

Within 30 days of becoming liable, in Form GST REG-01 on the GST portal.
