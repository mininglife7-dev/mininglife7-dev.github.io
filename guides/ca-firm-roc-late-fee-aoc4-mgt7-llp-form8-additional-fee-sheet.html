Missed 30 October? A late-fee sheet for CA firms: AOC-4 and MGT-7 at ₹100 a day per form, LLP Form 8 slabs, and what each week of delay costs

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 21 Oct 2026): if a company's AGM was on 30 September 2026, AOC-4 is due on Friday 30 October 2026 and MGT-7 / MGT-7A on Sunday 29 November 2026. LLP Form 8 is due on 30 October 2026 for every LLP. Miss the date and MCA charges an additional fee on top of the normal filing fee. For AOC-4 and MGT-7 / MGT-7A it is ₹100 for every day of delay, per form, with no upper limit (Companies (Registration Offices and Fees) Rules, 2014, Table of Fees, for sections 92 and 137 where the due date falls after 30 June 2018). For LLP Form 8 and Form 11 it is a multiple of the normal fee that steps up with the delay, lower for small LLPs (LLP Rules, 2009, Annexure A, as substituted from 1 April 2022). As of 21 Oct 2026 we found no MCA circular extending the FY 2025-26 dates or waiving the fee. ICSI has only asked for an extension. This is a starting point, not legal advice. Check the MCA portal before you quote a number to a client.

Read it this way for a company client: a company that files neither AOC-4 nor MGT-7 runs up ₹100 a day on each form once each date passes. Every week of delay on one form is ₹700, and the meter does not stop. For MGT-7 / MGT-7A, count from 29 November 2026 if the AGM was on 30 September (29 Nov is a Sunday, so aim to file by Friday 27 Nov).

For an LLP client: the fee steps up at fixed points: after 14 November (15 days), 29 November (30 days), 29 December (60 days), 28 January 2027 (90 days), 28 April 2027 (180 days) and 25 October 2027 (360 days). If Form 8 cannot go on 30 October, filing before 14 November keeps it at the lowest step.

When the clock stops: MCA's Form 8 page says the additional (delay) fee "will be levied till the date payment is confirmed and acknowledgment is generated". So a form that is signed but not paid on the due date is still late. Leave time for DSC and payment problems, and do not plan a midnight upload on 30 October.

Small LLP or not? Under section 2(1)(ta) of the LLP Act, a small LLP has contribution of not more than ₹25 lakh and turnover in the previous year of not more than ₹40 lakh, unless the Government has notified higher limits (the Act allows up to ₹5 crore and ₹50 crore). Check the official source for the limits in force before you mark an LLP "small" in the sheet.

Fee is not the only cost: the additional fee lets the form be filed late. It does not settle the default. The Companies Act also has separate penalties for not filing on time (sections 92(5) and 137(3)), and failing to file financial statements or annual returns for three continuing financial years can disqualify the directors (section 164(2)). The LLP Act has its own penalty provisions. Check the official source for the current amounts. The 50% lower penalty for small companies and OPCs in section 446B applies to penalties, not to this additional fee.

Copy-paste formulas (Google Sheets, row 2):

G2 (days late): =MAX(0, IF(F2="", TODAY(), F2) - E2)

H2 (additional fee): =IF(G2=0, 0, IF(LEFT(B2,3)="LLP", D2*IF(G2<=15,1,IF(G2<=30,IF(C2="Y",2,4),IF(G2<=60,IF(C2="Y",4,8),IF(G2<=90,IF(C2="Y",6,12),IF(G2<=180,IF(C2="Y",10,20),IF(C2="Y",15,30)))))) + IF(G2>360,(G2-360)*IF(C2="Y",10,20),0), 100*G2))

I2 (total): =D2+H2. Optional "one more week" column for companies: =IF(LEFT(B2,3)="LLP","see slab",700).

Type the form exactly as "AOC-4", "MGT-7", "MGT-7A", "LLP Form 8" or "LLP Form 11" so the formula picks the right rule. The H2 formula is for AOC-4, MGT-7 / MGT-7A and LLP Form 8 / 11 only. Other company forms (ADT-1, DIR-12, MGT-14 and so on) use a different multiple-of-fee table, so do not use this formula for them. Put "n/a" where something does not apply, so an empty cell always means "still open". Sort by column E every Monday until 30 November.

Three client scripts

"Can we file next month?" (company): "We can, but MCA charges an extra ₹100 for every day AOC-4 is late, and the same again for MGT-7 after its date. One week late is ₹700 per form, one month is about ₹3,000 per form, and there is no upper limit. There is also a separate penalty for late filing. To file by 30 October we need [items] by [date]."

"Can we file next month?" (LLP): "Form 8 for your LLP is due on 30 October. If it slips, MCA adds a late fee that steps up after 15, 30, 60 and 90 days. Filing by 14 November keeps it at the lowest step. To file on time we need the figures and the designated partners' DSCs by [date]."

"I heard the date is extended": "As of today MCA has not extended the FY 2025-26 dates. ICSI has asked for an extension to 31 December, but that is a request, not an order. Until MCA issues a circular, we are filing to 30 October. If an extension comes, we will tell you the same day."
