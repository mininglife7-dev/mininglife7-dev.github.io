Appointing or removing a director: DIR-2 consent, DIR-12 within 30 days and when DIR-11 is filed

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. A new director gives written consent in DIR-2 and must hold a DIN. The company informs the Registrar of the appointment, or of a resignation, in Form DIR-12 within 30 days (sections 170(2) and 168(1)). A resigning director may also send a copy of the resignation with reasons to the Registrar in DIR-11 within 30 days. A resignation takes effect from the date the company receives the notice or the date the director specifies, whichever is later.

Board vs members

  • Additional directors appointed by the board hold office until the next AGM, where members regularise them.
  • A director appointed at a general meeting is approved by the members' resolution.

Common mistakes

  • Resigning the last director(s). A private company needs at least two directors (one for an OPC); resignations that leave fewer are not processed.
  • DIR-12 late. Additional fees apply on MCA21.
  • DIN not KYC-compliant. A deactivated DIN blocks the filing; complete DIR-3 KYC first.
  • Resignation letter with no date of receipt. Keep proof of when the company received it.

Checklist

  • DIN active and KYC done
  • DIR-2 and DIR-8 on file
  • Board or members' resolution passed
  • DIR-12 filed within 30 days
  • Bank mandates and GST authorised signatories updated

Frequently asked questions

What is the time limit for DIR-12?

Within 30 days of the appointment or resignation of a director.

Does a resigning director have to file DIR-11?

It is optional: the director may forward a copy of the resignation with reasons to the Registrar in DIR-11 within 30 days (section 168(1)).

From when is a director's resignation effective?

From the date the company receives the notice or the date specified by the director, whichever is later.

What is DIR-2?

The director's written consent to act, filed by the company with the appointment.
