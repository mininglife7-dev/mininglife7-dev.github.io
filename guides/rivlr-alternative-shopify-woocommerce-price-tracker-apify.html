Rivlr or Unadhi's price tracker? Hosted Shopify price-and-stock alerts vs a pay-per-use price sheet

Competitor facts below come only from their own public pages, read on 9 Nov 2026; each is linked in Sources. We did not log in, start trials or read paywalled material. No ratings, no reviews, no scores of our own.

Short answer: Rivlr is a hosted tracker that watches competitor prices and stock on Shopify stores and emails you when something changes. Its site (checked 9 Nov 2026) lists Free at £0 for 5 products and 1 competitor shop with daily checks; Starter at £19 a month for 50 products, up to 3 competitor shops, daily checks, a weekly summary and spreadsheet export; Growth at £29 a month for 100 products, up to 10 competitor shops and checks every 6 hours; and Scale from £49 a month for 250 products, up to 25 competitor shops, with extra 100-product packs at £10 a month up to 2,500. Billing is monthly through Stripe and you can cancel in the app. Rivlr says it tracks Shopify stores only, with other platforms on its roadmap. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify returns competitor product and price rows for Shopify and WooCommerce shops, with a summary of skipped shops, but it sends no alerts and keeps no history for you. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you want hands-off email alerts on a watchlist of Shopify products, Rivlr is built for that and starts free. If you need whole catalogues or WooCommerce shops in a sheet, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Tell me the moment my two main rivals drop the price on our ten best sellers.” That is an alert job. “Give me every product and price from these six Shopify and WooCommerce shops in one sheet” is an export job.

Who should pick Rivlr instead

  • You want email alerts when a Shopify competitor changes price or stock, with no setup.
  • Your watchlist is small: the free plan covers 5 products, and £19 a month covers 50.
  • You want price history kept for you and checks every 6 hours from Growth.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Some of your competitors run WooCommerce.
  • You want whole catalogues in a sheet now and then, not a capped product watchlist.
  • You are comfortable running a job on Apify and comparing outputs yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Rivlr cost?

Its site (checked 9 Nov 2026) lists Free at £0 (5 products), Starter £19 a month (50 products), Growth £29 a month (100 products) and Scale from £49 a month (250 products, then £10 per extra 100 up to 2,500).

Does Rivlr track WooCommerce shops?

Its FAQ says it tracks Shopify stores, with other platforms on the roadmap.

Does the Unadhi Actor send price-drop alerts?

No. It returns product and price rows for each run; alerts and history are up to you (for example, a scheduled run into a spreadsheet).

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 9 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
