GSTR-9 and 9C chase list for CA firms: FY 2025-26 (close 30 Nov, file by 31 Dec 2026)

Updated 19 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 19 Oct 2026): For CA firms, FY 2025-26 GST annual returns are due on 31 December 2026 (CGST Act section 44, CGST Rules rule 80). GSTR-9 is optional for clients with aggregate turnover up to ₹2 crore (Notification 15/2025-Central Tax) and must be filed above that. GSTR-9C is also needed above ₹5 crore. The date that matters first is 30 November 2026: after it, or after the client files the FY 2025-26 annual return if that is earlier, the client can no longer claim missed FY 2025-26 ITC (section 16(4)), declare FY 2025-26 credit notes (section 34(2)) or correct FY 2025-26 GSTR-1/GSTR-3B details (sections 37(3), 39(9)). So chase in two rounds: missed credit and corrections first, then the annual return. Sources: CBIC's rule 80 and section 16 pages and the GSTN GSTR-9 FAQ. This is a starting point, not GST advice. Confirm on the GST portal before you file.

What you get from this guide: a finished two-round chase list for FY 2025-26 GST annual returns. Sort each client into one of three bands by aggregate turnover: up to ₹2 crore (GSTR-9 optional), ₹2–5 crore (GSTR-9), above ₹5 crore (GSTR-9 + 9C). Round 1 (now to 30 Nov): close missed ITC, credit notes and corrections. Round 2 (1–24 Dec): collect books and audited accounts, draft, get sign-off and file before the 31 Dec rush. What decides the date is when a return is actually filed, not its due date. For a monthly filer, the October 2026 GSTR-1 (due 11 Nov) and GSTR-3B (due 20 Nov) are the last returns that can carry FY 2025-26 items, because the November returns can only be filed after November ends. A credit note has to go in the return for the month it is issued, so issue FY 2025-26 credit notes by 31 Oct 2026. QRMP clients have fewer returns left than monthly filers. Their October–December GSTR-3B cannot be filed before January, so check today which returns they can still use (check the portal for each client).

Late fee: the late fee runs until the complete annual return is filed, which means GSTR-9 plus GSTR-9C where 9C is required (CBIC Circular 246/03/2025). Notification 07/2023-CT caps the fee by turnover band. Let the portal compute it, and check the notification before you quote a number to a client.

Collect list for each GSTR-9 / 9C client

  • Client name, GSTIN(s), state, monthly or QRMP, FY 2025-26 aggregate turnover and band (optional / 9 / 9 + 9C)
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B filed for every period Apr 2025–Mar 2026 (Y/N), plus FY 2025-26 items reported in Apr–Nov 2026 returns (these feed Tables 10–14)
  • Sales register with taxable, exempt, nil and non-GST supplies split, plus advances, and credit and debit notes
  • Purchase register vs GSTR-2B / Table 8A download: matched, missing and excess
  • ITC reversals during the year (rule 37 and 37A, rules 42/43, section 17(5) blocked credit) and any reclaims
  • FY 2024-25 credit reclaimed in FY 2025-26. Per the GSTN FAQ it is reported in Table 6A1 (reclaims other than rule 37/37A) or Table 6H (rule 37/37A reclaims). Check the GSTN FY 2025-26 advisory of 13 Oct 2026 for the current table rules.
  • RCM tax paid and the related ITC, import of goods/services, ISD credit
  • HSN-wise summary of outward and inward supplies
  • Demands, refunds and late fees paid for FY 2025-26
  • For 9C: audited financial statements, and a turnover reconciliation (unbilled revenue, unadjusted advances, credit notes after year end, deemed supplies)
  • Owner on your team, draft sent date, client sign-off date, ARN

WhatsApp / email script pack

Turnover check: "Hello. We are preparing your GST annual return for 2025-26, which is due on 31 December 2026. Please confirm your total turnover for April 2025 to March 2026 across all your GST numbers by [date]. That tells us whether GSTR-9 and 9C apply."

Purchase register: "To make sure you get every input credit for 2025-26, please send your purchase register for April 2025 to March 2026 in Excel by [date]. Any credit not claimed by 30 November 2026 is lost."

Credit notes: "If you owe any customer a discount, return or price correction on a 2025-26 sale, please issue the credit note by 31 October 2026, so we can report it in your October return."

Vendor gap: "These [n] purchase bills from 2025-26 are not showing in your GST credit statement. Please ask these vendors to report them in their October GSTR-1 by 11 November. List attached: GSTIN, bill number, amount."

Sign-off: "Your 2025-26 GST annual return is ready. Summary attached. Please reply 'OK to file' by [date], and we will file it before 31 December."
