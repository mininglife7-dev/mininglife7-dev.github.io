Short answer (checked 23 Oct 2026): a client who files GSTR-3B quarterly under the QRMP scheme must deposit the tax for October 2026 in Form GST PMT-06 by 25 November 2026, and for November 2026 by 25 December 2026, choosing "Monthly payment for quarterly taxpayer" as the reason. It can pay a portal-calculated fixed sum (35% of the tax paid in cash in July to September 2026, if that quarter was filed quarterly) or its actual net liability. No deposit is needed if the cash and credit ledger balance already covers the tax due, or the liability is nil. The quarterly GSTR-3B for October to December follows on 22 or 24 January 2027.

What the rules say

All points below are from CBIC Circular 143/13/2020-GST (10 November 2020), which explains the QRMP scheme.

  • Para 6.1. Tax due for each of the first two months of a quarter is deposited in Form GST PMT-06 "by the twenty fifth day of the month succeeding such month".
  • Fixed sum method, para 6.1(a). The portal pre-fills a challan for 35% of the tax paid in cash in the preceding quarter where that return was quarterly, or 100% of the tax paid in cash in the last month of that quarter where the return was monthly. It is not available to a person who has not filed the return for a complete preceding tax period.
  • Self-assessment method, para 6.1(b). Pay the actual liability on outward and inward supplies, net of input tax credit available (use the month's GSTR-2B). Para 6.2: either method may be used in either month.
  • Para 6.3: when nothing needs paying. No deposit for month 1 if the electronic cash and/or credit ledger balance is adequate for that month's tax, or liability is nil. For month 2 the balance must cover the cumulative tax of months 1 and 2.
  • Para 6.4. The deposit can only be refunded or used elsewhere after the quarter's GSTR-3B is filed.
  • Interest, para 8. Fixed sum: no interest if the system-calculated amount is paid by the 25th and the full quarter is settled in GSTR-3B on time, even if the real liability was higher; if the fixed sum is paid late, interest runs from the 25th to the payment date. Self-assessment: interest under section 50 of the CGST Act on tax unpaid or paid late. Interest is paid through GSTR-3B (para 8.3). See our section 50 interest guide.
  • Late fee, para 9. No late fee for delay in the monthly payment; late fee applies only to the quarterly return.

Self-assessment row J pays the shortfall over the ledger balance. That is a cautious working figure, not a portal rule: under para 6.3 a client whose balance covers the tax pays nothing, and one whose balance does not can pay the full self-assessed tax if you prefer.

Made-up example

Client X filed Jul–Sep 2026 quarterly and paid ₹60,000 in cash that quarter. Fixed sum F = ₹21,000. Its October liability net of ITC is ₹28,000 and the ledger holds ₹2,000. On the fixed method it deposits ₹21,000 by 25 November and settles the rest in the January GSTR-3B with no interest, if that return is on time. On self-assessment it would deposit ₹26,000.
