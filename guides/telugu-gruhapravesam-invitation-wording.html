Telugu Gruhapravesam invitation wording (గృహప్రవేశ ఆహ్వానం), with English meanings

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · Telugu speakers everywhere

Short answer. A Telugu Gruhapravesam invitation usually opens with a blessing (శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తు), names the occasion (గృహప్రవేశ మహోత్సవం), gives the ముహూర్తం (auspicious time), date and full address of the new home, invites guests సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా (with their whole family), and closes with the hosts' names. Wording differs between families and regions, so please have an elder in the family check it before you print or send it.

Why the wording matters

A Gruhapravesam is the family's first formal welcome into a new home. Elders read the card for the blessing line, the muhurtham and how the family is named, and relatives who live far away often decide from the card whether to travel or join by video. A clear, respectful card in Telugu, with an English line for those who need it, does both jobs.

A simple template (fill the brackets)

శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తు
శ్రీ [ఇష్టదైవం] కృపతో
గృహప్రవేశ మహోత్సవ ఆహ్వానం
మా నూతన గృహప్రవేశ మహోత్సవం [తేదీ] న, [సమయం] శుభ ముహూర్తమున జరుగును. ఈ శుభ సందర్భమునకు మీరు సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా విచ్చేసి, మమ్ములను ఆశీర్వదించవలసిందిగా కోరుచున్నాము.
చిరునామా: [పూర్తి చిరునామా, గుర్తు]
విందు: [సమయం]
ఆహ్వానించువారు: [పేర్లు]

English meaning: With the blessings of [family deity], we invite you and your family to the housewarming (Gruhapravesam) of our new home on [date], at the auspicious time of [time]. Please come and bless us. Address: [full address, landmark]. Lunch/dinner: [time]. With warm regards, [hosts' names].

A short WhatsApp version for relatives abroad

నమస్కారం! మా నూతన గృహప్రవేశం [తేదీ] న [సమయం] (భారత కాలమానం) కు జరుగుతోంది. మీరు రాలేకపోతే, ఈ లింక్ ద్వారా చూసి ఆశీర్వదించండి: [లింక్]

English: Namaskaram! Our housewarming is on [date] at [time] IST. If you can't travel, please watch here and send your blessings: [link]. Tip: add the time in their zone too, for example "[time] in [city]".

Common mistakes

  • Muhurtham without the full date and time. Write the weekday, date and exact time, as fixed by your family priest.
  • An address guests can't find. New colonies are hard to locate; add a landmark and a map link.
  • Broken Telugu letters. Some design apps don't support Telugu. Use a Telugu font such as Noto Sans Telugu, export to PDF or image, and check every word on a phone.
  • Copying a wedding card's honorifics. చి॥ and చి॥ల॥సౌ॥ belong on wedding cards; a Gruhapravesam card names the hosts.
  • Skipping the elder's check. Families differ on the opening line, the deity named and how relatives are listed.

Checklist before sending

  • Muhurtham date and time confirmed with the priest
  • Full address, landmark and map link
  • Meal time, if you are serving a meal
  • Hosts' names spelled as the family prefers
  • Telugu text read by an elder or fluent reader
  • English line or livestream link for relatives abroad
  • Tested on an Android phone and an iPhone

తెలుగులో సంక్షిప్తంగా

గృహప్రవేశ ఆహ్వానంలో ఆశీర్వచనం, ముహూర్తం, తేదీ, పూర్తి చిరునామా, ఆహ్వానించువారి పేర్లు ఉండాలి. విదేశాల్లో ఉన్న బంధువుల కోసం ఇంగ్లీష్ వాక్యం, లైవ్ లింక్ ఇవ్వడం మంచిది. పంపే ముందు ఇంట్లో పెద్దలతో ఒకసారి సరిచూసుకోండి.

Frequently asked questions

What is a housewarming called in Telugu?

Gruhapravesam (గృహప్రవేశం), literally entering the home. The invitation is often titled గృహప్రవేశ మహోత్సవ ఆహ్వానం.

What is the opening line of a Telugu Gruhapravesam invitation?

Many families begin with శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తు, followed by the family deity's name, but the line varies by family.

How do I invite relatives abroad to a Gruhapravesam?

Send a short Telugu and English WhatsApp message with the time in IST and in their time zone, plus a livestream link.

Should I check the Telugu wording with someone?

Yes. Ask an elder or a fluent reader to check names, the opening line and the muhurtham before you print or send.
