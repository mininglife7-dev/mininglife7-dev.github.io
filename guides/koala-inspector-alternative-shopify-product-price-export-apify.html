Short answer: Koala Inspector is a Chrome extension for researching Shopify stores: open a shop and it shows best sellers, installed apps, the theme, and estimated sales and traffic, with CSV or Excel export. Its pricing page (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan with 15 tokens a month and Premium at $22 a month with 220 tokens, tracking for up to 50 shops and priority support; token packs cost $3.99 for 10, $6.99 for 25 and $11.99 for 100. Shop tracking costs 15 tokens per shop per month and a CSV export 1 token. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does one narrower job: it reads the public product catalogue of Shopify and WooCommerce shops you list and returns every product and variant with price, compare-at price and stock flag, on a schedule if you want. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you want spy-style research (apps, ads, traffic), use Koala. If you want clean, repeatable price data across many shops, including WooCommerce, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Every Monday I open five competitor shops and copy their prices into a sheet.” A browser extension helps while you are looking at a shop. A scheduled Actor does the same reading without you, across many shops, and gives you one file to compare week by week.

Who should pick Koala Inspector instead

  • You are a dropshipper or product researcher who wants to see a shop's apps, theme, ads and estimated traffic while browsing.
  • You look at a few shops by hand and like a point-and-click panel more than a data file.
  • You want supplier search and trend views in the same tool.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want exact published prices and stock flags for every variant, across many shops, in one file.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Koala Inspector cost?

Its pricing page (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan with 15 tokens a month and Premium at $22 a month with 220 renewing tokens, tracking for up to 50 shops and priority support. Token packs are $3.99 for 10, $6.99 for 25 and $11.99 for 100.

What do Koala Inspector tokens pay for?

Per its FAQ: traffic analysis 3 tokens, ad campaigns 2, supplier search 2, product trends 2, CSV exports and AI shop chat 1 each, and shop tracking 15 tokens per shop per month. Shop structure, app and theme detection, product browsing and best sellers need no tokens.

Are Koala Inspector sales figures exact?

Its own product page describes sales and traffic as estimates. Our Actor does not estimate sales at all; it reads published prices and stock flags.

Does the Unadhi Actor work on WooCommerce?

Yes. It reads public Shopify and WooCommerce catalogues. Shops that block access or return nothing are skipped and listed in the summary record.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
