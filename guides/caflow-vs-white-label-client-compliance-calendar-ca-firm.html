Short answer: They do different jobs. CAflow is GST practice software for Indian CAs, run largely through WhatsApp: AI extraction of invoice data, GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B preparation and filing, GSTR-2B purchase reconciliation, document chasing and WhatsApp deadline reminders to clients 10, 5 and 2 days before GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, TDS and professional tax dates. Its site and terms (checked 27 Oct 2026) show a free tier for up to 10 GSTINs and 1 user, Starter ₹4,999 a year (50 GSTINs), Professional ₹9,999 (200 GSTINs, 2 users) and Enterprise ₹14,999 (500 GSTINs, 3 users), extra users ₹1,999 a year, a 30-day free trial, and no refunds for partly used annual plans. Our White-Label Client Compliance Calendar is not software: four quarterly packs (DOCX, PDF, WhatsApp image, .ics, .xlsx) with your firm's name, for unlimited clients, at ₹999 a year, with a 14-day refund. If you want GST filing and per-client WhatsApp reminders in one system, CAflow does far more, and its free tier may be enough for a small office. If you only want a branded calendar every client can keep, start with ours.

The pain this page is about

“I want my firm's name in front of all 150 clients every quarter, including the ones who are not GST clients, with one clean list of dates.” Filing software reminds clients about the returns it files for them. A branded calendar is one page your whole client list can see and share.

Frequently asked questions

How much does CAflow cost?

Its site and terms (checked 27 Oct 2026) show a free tier for up to 10 GSTINs and 1 user, Starter ₹4,999 a year (50 GSTINs, 1 user), Professional ₹9,999 a year (200 GSTINs, 2 users) and Enterprise ₹14,999 a year (500 GSTINs, 3 users). Extra users are ₹1,999 per user per year.

Does CAflow have a free trial or refunds?

Its terms give new subscribers a 30-day free trial with full access. You can cancel any time and service runs to the end of the annual period, but there are no refunds for partly used annual subscriptions.

Does CAflow remind clients about deadlines?

Yes. Its site says it sends WhatsApp reminders 10, 5 and 2 days before GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, TDS and professional tax deadlines.

Is the ₹999 calendar a subscription?

Yes, a yearly Gumroad membership that renews unless cancelled. Year one gives four quarterly packs with your firm's name. 14-day refund from when you receive the first pack.

Can I use both?

Yes. Filing software handles returns and per-client reminders; the branded calendar is a simple page every client can keep.
