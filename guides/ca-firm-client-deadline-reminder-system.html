“No SMS came, so I stopped filing”: a simple client deadline reminder system for CA firms

Last updated 13 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Clients rarely miss dates because they don't care; they miss them because nobody reminded them. One taxpayer wrote that he filed nil returns while reminder messages came and stopped when they stopped. A simple fix for a CA firm: keep one calendar of the dates that drive most client work (GSTR-1 on the 11th or 13th, GSTR-3B on the 20th or 22nd/24th, TDS deposit within 7 days of month-end, quarterly TDS statements, e.g. 31 Oct 2026 for Q2) and send three touches per date: 7 days before, 2 days before, and on the day.

Why reminders beat follow-ups

A reminder before the date costs one message. A missed date costs a late fee, interest and a client who blames the firm. Firms that send a short, predictable reminder every cycle also get documents earlier, which spreads work away from the last two days.

The 3-touch rhythm

  1. 7 days before: "Your GSTR-1 for September is due on 11 Oct. Please send sales invoices by 8 Oct."
  2. 2 days before: "Two days left for GSTR-1. We still need: purchase register / bank statement."
  3. On the day (if still pending): "GSTR-1 is due today. Reply 'nil' if there were no sales this month and we will file a nil return."

Keep each message to two lines, name the form and the date, and say exactly what you need from the client. Use the same day and format every month so clients learn the pattern.

What to track per client

  • GST frequency (monthly or QRMP) and state (22nd or 24th group)
  • TDS deductor? (deposit by the 7th, quarterly statements)
  • Preferred channel (WhatsApp, e-mail, SMS) and contact person
  • Documents received date per cycle
  • Filed date and acknowledgement saved

Common mistakes

  • One reminder only, on the due date. Too late to collect documents.
  • National dates for every client. Quarterly GSTR-3B depends on the client's state.
  • Long newsletters. Clients read two lines with their form name; they skip a page of updates.
  • No "nil" option. Make it easy for a client with no transactions to say so, so a nil return goes in on time.

Frequently asked questions

When should a CA firm send GST reminders to clients?

A simple rhythm is three touches: 7 days before the due date (ask for documents), 2 days before (list what is still missing) and on the due date if still pending.

What is the quarterly GSTR-3B due date for Telangana clients?

The 22nd of the month after the quarter, for example 22 October 2026 for July to September 2026.

When are Q2 2026-27 TDS statements due?

31 October 2026 for Forms 138, 140, 144 and the TCS statement in Form 143.

By when must TDS be deposited?

For most deductors, within seven days from the end of the month of deduction under Rule 218 of the Income-tax Rules 2026; for March the date is 30 April, and some payments (such as certain rent or property payments) follow a 30-day rule.
