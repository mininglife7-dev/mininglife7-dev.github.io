Short answer (checked 22 Oct 2026): from tax year 2026-27 the quarterly TCS statement is Form No. 143 (earlier Form 27EQ), filed under Rule 219 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (section 397(3)(b) of the Income-tax Act, 2025). For July–September it is due 31 October. The TCS certificate is Form No. 133 (earlier Form 27D, section 395(4)(a)), due within 15 days of the statement due date, so 15 November for this quarter. Form 133 can only be downloaded from TRACES after the Form 143 statement is processed; a certificate made any other way is not valid. Sources: the Income Tax Department's note on Form 143, Form 143 FAQs and Form 133 FAQs. 31 October 2026 is a Saturday and 15 November 2026 a Sunday; no extension had been announced when we checked, so confirm on incometax.gov.in and the TRACES portal before you act. This is a starting point, not tax advice.

Who on your client list is affected

Every client who is a collector: the department's note lists sellers of scrap, liquor, tendu leaves, forest produce and motor vehicles, parking lot, toll plaza and mining or quarrying contracts, and authorised dealers collecting on overseas remittances and foreign tour packages. Which transactions and rates apply in tax year 2026-27 depends on the client's facts; check each one against the Act before you file.

Three client messages

1. Data request: "Your July–September TCS statement (now Form 143, earlier 27EQ) is due on 31 October, which is a Saturday. Please send the TCS collection register and all challans for the quarter by Saturday 24 October so we can file on time."

2. Missing PAN: "The TCS statement is filed buyer by buyer, by PAN. We are missing the PAN for: [names]. Please get them this week; until we have them the statement cannot be completed correctly."

3. Certificates ready: "Your TCS certificates for July–September (Form 133, earlier 27D) are downloaded from TRACES. Please sign and send each buyer their certificate by 15 November, or tell us to send them for you. Buyers will also see the credit in their AIS."

Notes that save a correction later

  • Corrections to a certificate are made by filing a revised Form 143; after it is processed, download and issue the corrected Form 133 (Form 133 FAQ 7).
  • Form 133 is not attached to the buyer's return, but must be kept for records (FAQ 8). A duplicate can be issued if marked "duplicate" (FAQ 11).
  • Collected tax must be paid to the Government within the time limits in Rule 218 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026; confirm the exact dates on the portal.
  • A statement filed late attracts a late fee; see our TDS late fee guide and confirm on the portal how it applies to TCS.
