Google Shopping listing check before Black Friday: one sheet that flags every product Google may reject or hide in your Shopify or WooCommerce shop

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the product tracker linked below is an Unadhi product on the Apify Store. This guide is free, and the sheet also works with your shop admin's own product export. Current Apify pricing is only on the Store page. We are not affiliated with Google.

What you get: one Google Sheet with one row per product in your shop and a plain-English flag on every product Google Merchant Center is likely to reject or show less: no image, no description, price is 0 or missing, title over 150 characters, title in capital letters, promotional text in the title, no brand, barcode (GTIN) check digit is wrong, barcode in a restricted or coupon range, compare-at price below the price and out of stock. A summary tab counts each flag, so you know before Black Friday (Friday 27 November 2026) how many products need a fix and which ones.

Who this is for

  • Shopify and WooCommerce shop owners who show products on Google Shopping (ads or free listings) and want a clean catalogue before the November sales.
  • Agencies and freelancers who run Google Shopping for client shops and want a quick catalogue check before they take on, or report on, a client.

This sheet checks the product details your shop itself shows. It does not replace the diagnostics in your Merchant Center account, which show Google's actual verdict per product. Use it to find and fix problems in bulk, then confirm in Merchant Center.

Step 1: get your products into a tab named scrape

With the product tracker

Open the E-commerce Product Data Scraper (Shopify, WooCommerce, any shop) on the Apify Store and use this input with your own shop address:

{
  "startUrls": [{ "url": "https://your-shop.example" }],
  "maxProductsPerShop": 2000,
  "includeDescription": true
}
  • maxProductsPerShop: set it above your number of products (the default is 100). You pay only for products delivered.
  • includeDescription: keep it on (the default); the sheet needs it to flag empty descriptions. The tracker keeps up to 5,000 characters, which is also Google's limit, so it cannot flag descriptions that are too long.
  • The tracker follows robots.txt and reads your shop's terms page. If your own terms forbid automated access (Shopify's default terms do), it skips the shop and charges nothing. Check the run's Storage → Key-value store → SHOPS_SUMMARY: your shop should show ok. If it shows blocked-by-terms, use your admin export below.

In the run, export the dataset as CSV. In your Google Sheet use File → Import → Upload, "Insert new sheet", and name the tab scrape. Do not rename headers. You get one row per product with columns such as title, url, source, price, compareAtPrice, availability, gtin, brand, description and images/0. For Shopify shops, price, compareAtPrice and gtin come from the cheapest variant, and brand is the product's vendor.

Free, from your own shop admin

  • Shopify: Products → Export gives one row per variant with the columns Title, Body (HTML), Vendor, Variant Price, Variant Compare At Price, Variant Barcode and Image Src. Extra variant rows have an empty Title, so the sheet checks the first variant of each product.
  • WooCommerce: the built-in product CSV exporter (Products → Export) gives the name, description, price, stock and image columns.

The formulas below look columns up by header name, so with an admin export you only change the header names in quotes (for Shopify: "Title", "Variant Price", "Variant Compare At Price", "Variant Barcode", "Vendor", "Image Src", "Body (HTML)").

Step 2: build the check tab

Add a tab named check. Row 1 holds these headers; row 2 holds the formulas, which you copy down one row per row of scrape.

A2 to I2 all use the same pattern; only the header name in quotes changes. A2:

=IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("title", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), "")

J2:

=LEN(IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("description", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), ""))

K2, the flag. It also does the GS1 check-digit sum on the barcode (pad to 14 digits, weights 3 and 1 on the first 13 digits):

=IF(A2="", "", LET(
  g, REGEXREPLACE(G2&"", "[^0-9]", ""),
  p, RIGHT("00000000000000"&g, 14),
  lenok, OR(LEN(g)=8, LEN(g)=12, LEN(g)=13, LEN(g)=14),
  chk, IF(lenok, MOD(10 - MOD(SUMPRODUCT(VALUE(MID(p, SEQUENCE(13), 1)),
        {3;1;3;1;3;1;3;1;3;1;3;1;3}), 10), 10) = VALUE(RIGHT(p, 1)), FALSE),
  woo, C2="woocommerce-store-api",
  f, TEXTJOIN(" · ", TRUE,
   IF(LEN(A2)>150, "Title over 150 characters", ""),
   IF(AND(EXACT(A2, UPPER(A2)), REGEXMATCH(A2, "[A-Z]{3}")), "Title in capital letters", ""),
   IF(REGEXMATCH(LOWER(A2), "free (shipping|delivery)|% off|\bsale\b|best price|buy now"), "Promotional text in title", ""),
   IF(N(D2)<=0, "Price is 0 or missing", ""),
   IF(AND(N(E2)>0, N(E2)<N(D2)), "Compare-at price is below the price", ""),
   IF(I2="", "No image", ""),
   IF(J2=0, "No description", ""),
   IF(AND(NOT(woo), H2=""), "No brand", ""),
   IF(AND(g<>"", NOT(lenok)), "Barcode is not 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits", ""),
   IF(AND(lenok, NOT(chk)), "Barcode check digit is wrong", ""),
   IF(AND(LEN(g)>=12, lenok, OR(MID(p,2,1)="2", MID(p,2,2)="02", MID(p,2,2)="04",
      MID(p,2,2)="98", MID(p,2,2)="99")), "Barcode in a restricted or coupon range", ""),
   IF(AND(NOT(woo), g=""), "No barcode", ""),
   IF(F2="OutOfStock", "Out of stock", "")),
  IF(f="", "OK", f) & IF(woo, " · Brand and barcode: check in Merchant Center", "")))

WooCommerce's public store data does not include brand or barcode, so for WooCommerce rows (source = woocommerce-store-api) the sheet skips those two checks and tells you to look in Merchant Center instead. If your barcodes import as numbers, leading zeros are lost; the padding to 14 digits puts them back for the check.

In the summary tab, list the flag names in column A from A2 down and put the COUNTIF formula shown above in B2, copied down. Add a filter to the check tab to see the products behind each count.

What to do with each flag

FlagWhy it matters (Google's product data specification)What to do
Price is 0 or missingPrice is required, and a price of 0 is not allowed (except mobile devices on contract and physical goods sold with a subscription).Set the real price. For gift cards and free samples, decide whether they belong in your Google feed at all.
No imageThe main image link is required. Google is also introducing a minimum of 500 × 500 pixels for all product images, enforced from 31 January 2027.Add a real product photo, not a placeholder, logo or image with promotional text. Check sizes now while you are adding images.
No descriptionDescription is required (plain text, up to 5,000 characters) and should match your product page.Write what the product is, its material, size and use. No links, shipping offers or competitor names.
Title over 150 charactersThe title limit is 150 characters.Keep brand, product type and the distinguishing feature (colour, size) and cut the rest.
Title in capital letters / Promotional text in titleGoogle asks for no promotional text such as "free shipping" and no all-capital titles.Rewrite in normal case and move offers to your promotions, not the title.
No brandBrand is required for new products (except movies, books and music). For truly unbranded products, leave it empty; do not write "N/A" or "Generic".Fill in the maker's brand, or your own brand for products you make.
Barcode check digit is wrong / not 8, 12, 13 or 14 digitsGoogle disapproves products with an incorrect GTIN.Copy the barcode from the packaging or the supplier again. If you are not sure it is correct, leave it out rather than guess.
Barcode in a restricted or coupon rangeGoogle does not accept GS1 restricted prefixes (02, 04, 2) or coupon prefixes (98, 99). These are often in-store codes, not the maker's GTIN.Replace with the manufacturer's GTIN, or remove it and use brand plus MPN where Google asks for them.
No barcodeGTIN is "strongly recommended if available"; products that have one but are submitted without it may be shown less. Products with no GTIN need the identifier rules (brand plus MPN, or identifier exists set to no).Add the maker's GTIN where one exists. For your own handmade or private-label products without a GTIN, this flag is expected.
Compare-at price is below the priceA struck-through price lower than the selling price is not a reduction, and it will look wrong next to your price.Correct or remove the compare-at price. For an EU sale, see the 30-day lowest price log linked below.
Out of stockIf an item is out of stock, its price must still be visible on the page. Google says not to set out_of_stock to pause products; use the pause attribute (up to 14 days) or excluded destination.Restock, or keep it out of your Black Friday ads; do not fake stock status to switch ads on or off.

Before your sale

  • This week: run the check, fix every Price, No image and No description flag.
  • Next week: fix barcode flags with your suppliers, then titles.
  • Week of 16 November: run it again and confirm in Merchant Center's diagnostics that the products you will promote are approved.
  • Keep the sale price on your product page, your feed and checkout the same: Google asks for the price to match on all three.
