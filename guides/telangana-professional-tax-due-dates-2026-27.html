Telangana professional tax due dates for 2026-27: the 10th of every month

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · Telangana

Short answer. If you are an employer registered under the Telangana Profession Tax Act, you must file a Form V return and pay the professional tax deducted from salaries on or before the 10th of the following month, online or with the assessing authority. For example, September 2026 salaries: 10 Oct 2026; October: 10 Nov 2026; February 2027: 10 Mar 2027.

Every date here has a late fee or interest behind it. The hard part is not knowing the date, it is remembering it on the day.

Why it exists

Professional tax is a state tax on people engaged in a profession, trade, calling or employment. Employers deduct it from salaries and pay it to the state each month; businesses, partners and directors can also owe it in their own right, at the rate in the Act's schedule.

Common mistakes

  • Old forum answers. Some threads give other dates; the Commercial Taxes Department help page says the 10th of the following month.
  • Confusing your own PT with employees'. The monthly Form V covers salaries paid; a business's own PT is separate.
  • Weekends. Dates don't move by themselves; pay before a weekend or holiday.
  • Several places of work. Tax is paid at the principal place of work you name.

Monthly checklist

  • Salary register for the month final
  • PT deducted per the schedule
  • Form V filed and tax paid by the 10th
  • Challan or e-payment receipt saved

Also for Telangana: quarterly GSTR-3B is due on the 22nd; see GSTR-3B quarterly due dates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the professional tax due date in Telangana?

The 10th of the month following the salary month, for the Form V return and payment by registered employers.

Which form is used for Telangana professional tax returns?

Form V, filed monthly by employers registered under the Act.

Is the Hyderabad professional tax date different?

No. Hyderabad follows the Telangana Profession Tax Act: the 10th of the following month.
