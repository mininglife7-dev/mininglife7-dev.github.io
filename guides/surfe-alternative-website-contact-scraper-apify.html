Short answer: Surfe is a prospecting and CRM enrichment product. Its pricing page (checked 3 Nov 2026) shows Free at $0 per user per month (20 email-find credits and 5 mobile-find credits a month on monthly billing). Essential is $49 per user per month billed monthly (150 email-find and 50 mobile-find credits a month) or $39 per user per month billed annually. Pro is $89 per user per month billed monthly (1,000 email-find and 100 mobile-find credits a month) or $79 per user per month billed annually. Enterprise is custom. The same page lists an Enrichment module that can be purchased for $29 per month. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify visits each website in your list and returns published emails, phones and WhatsApp links with the source page. No Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If your team lives in a CRM and needs credit-based email and mobile finds while prospecting, Surfe is built for that. If you already have company websites and want their published contacts, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“Our SDRs work in the CRM and need emails while they browse LinkedIn.” That is a Surfe-type job. “Here are 180 supplier websites from a trade fair PDF” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick Surfe instead

  • Your team needs email and mobile finds inside daily prospecting, with per-user plans and a CRM connector.
  • You want a free tier to try credit-based finds before Essential or Pro.
  • You may add the separate Enrichment module at $29/month listed on its pricing page.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have a list of websites and want each firm's published inbox, phone and WhatsApp.
  • You prefer role mailboxes by default and a recorded source page per contact.
  • You do not want per-user SaaS seats for a batch enrichment of sites you already collected.
