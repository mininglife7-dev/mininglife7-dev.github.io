E-way bill 180-day document limit and 360-day extension cap: a check sheet for CA firms and their clients

Updated 26 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year). This guide is for information only, not professional advice.

What you get: a ready-to-copy Google Sheet layout that catches two e-way bill portal blocks before a client's truck is loaded. Tab 1 lists documents waiting to move (invoice, delivery challan, bill of supply) and flags any dated more than 180 days ago, so it can no longer get a new e-way bill. Tab 2 tracks long-running e-way bills (job work, exhibition goods, stuck consignments) and shows the last date each can still be extended under the 360-day cap. Tab 3 filters what needs action this week. You can use it without buying anything.

Short answer. Since 1 January 2025 the e-way bill portal only accepts a base document dated within 180 days of the day you generate the e-way bill, and an e-way bill can be extended only up to 360 days from when it was first generated (GSTN advisory, 17 December 2024). On 26 October 2026, an invoice dated before 29 April 2026 is already too old.

Why 358 and not 360? The GSTN advisory's own example says an e-way bill generated on 1 January 2025 can be extended only up to 25 December 2025, which is 358 days later. We use that so the sheet never shows a later date than the portal's example. The 30-day warning is our working rule.

Tab 3: This week

Keep two small filters on this tab. Docs: =FILTER(Docs!A2:J,Docs!I2:I<>"OK",Docs!I2:I<>""). EWB: =FILTER(EWB!A2:I,EWB!G2:G<>"OK",EWB!G2:G<>""). Sort so BLOCKED and CAP PASSED rows come first.

What to do for each status

  • BLOCKED: the portal will not take that document date. Do not let the client move the goods on the old document; decide with the client's CA what the correct document for this movement is before the truck leaves.
  • HURRY: generate the e-way bill before the last day in column H, or move the goods earlier.
  • PLAN DELIVERY / LAST EXTENSION: the goods must reach before the cap date; after it the portal will not extend again.
  • CAP PASSED: no further extension is possible on that e-way bill. Take advice on the next step before the goods move again.
