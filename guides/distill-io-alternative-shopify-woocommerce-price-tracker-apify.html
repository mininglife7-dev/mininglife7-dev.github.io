Short answer: Distill.io watches web pages and alerts you when something on them changes, by email, push, SMS or webhook. You set it up per page (a monitor), in its browser extension, desktop app or cloud. Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Free with 25 monitors (5 in the cloud, checked at most every 6 hours, 1,000 cloud checks a month), Starter $15 a month (50 monitors, every 10 minutes, 30,000 checks), Professional $35 (150 monitors, every 5 minutes, 100,000 checks) and Flexi from $80 (500+ monitors, every 2 minutes). Browser-extension checks are unlimited. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does not send alerts; it reads the whole public catalogue of each shop you list and returns one row per product, with variants, price, compare-at price and stock as one file. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you want an alert the minute one product page changes, pick Distill. If you want a complete price sheet for whole shops, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“My competitor has 300 products. I don't want 300 alerts; I want one sheet every Monday that shows what each product costs and what is out of stock.” A page monitor is built for a few pages you care about a lot. A catalogue reader is built for every product at once.

Who should pick Distill.io instead

  • You watch a handful of specific pages (one product, a price list PDF page, a tender page) and want an alert fast.
  • The sites you watch are not Shopify or WooCommerce, or need clicks or a login before the price shows.
  • You want SMS or push notifications, not a data file.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want every product, with its variants, of a competitor in one CSV, not one alert per page.
  • You compare several shops side by side, Shopify and WooCommerce mixed.
  • You want a weekly file you can sort, filter and chart in Google Sheets or Excel.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Distill.io cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Free at $0 (25 monitors, 5 in the cloud, 1,000 cloud checks a month, 6-hour minimum cloud interval), Starter $15 a month (50 monitors, 10-minute interval, 30,000 checks), Professional $35 a month (150 monitors, 5-minute interval, 100,000 checks) and Flexi from $80 a month (500+ monitors, 2-minute interval, 200,000+ checks).

Are Distill.io browser checks limited?

Its FAQ says browser-extension checks are unlimited while cloud checks are limited by plan, counted in units of 10 seconds. Unused resources expire at the end of each billing period.

Can Distill.io track prices on any website?

It monitors any web page for changes, so it can watch a price on a page you choose. It sends an alert when that part changes; it does not export a shop's full catalogue as a table.

Does the Unadhi Actor send price-change alerts?

No. It exports the current catalogue each run. Schedule it weekly in Apify and compare the files, or connect an Apify webhook to your own alert.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
