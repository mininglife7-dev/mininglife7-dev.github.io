ROC annual filing chase list for CA firms: AOC-4 by 30 Oct, MGT-7 by 29 Nov 2026

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

What you get from this guide: a finished chase list for FY 2025-26 ROC annual filings. Put each company client's AGM date in one column. AOC-4 is due 30 days after it and MGT-7/MGT-7A 60 days after it. LLP clients go on the same sheet for Form 8 (30 Oct). Collect documents in the order below, and use the short scripts to ask each client for one thing at a time. As of 14 Oct 2026 MCA has not extended these dates, so plan to the statutory deadlines.

The due dates you can rely on (FY 2025-26)

FormWhoRuleIf AGM on 30 Sep 2026
AOC-4 / AOC-4 CFS / AOC-4 XBRLCompanies (financial statements)Within 30 days of the AGM (s.137)30 Oct 2026
MGT-7 / MGT-7A (small companies and OPCs)Companies (annual return)Within 60 days of the AGM (s.92(4))29 Nov 2026
AOC-4 (OPC)One Person CompaniesWithin 180 days of FY end (s.137 proviso)27 Sep 2026 (already passed, so chase any OPC still pending now)
ADT-1Companies appointing or reappointing an auditor at the AGMWithin 15 days of the AGM15 Oct 2026
LLP Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency)LLPsWithin 30 days from the end of six months of the FY (LLP Rules, r.24)30 Oct 2026 (fixed, no AGM link)

Earlier AGM, earlier dates. AGM on 15 Sep 2026 → AOC-4 15 Oct, MGT-7 14 Nov. AGM on 20 Aug 2026 → AOC-4 19 Sep, MGT-7 19 Oct. If the AGM was not held, count from 30 Sep 2026, the last date it should have been held. If the RoC extended the AGM, count from the AGM actually held.

Late filing: MCA charges an additional fee for each day of delay on AOC-4 and MGT-7. Check the current fee in the MCA fee rules before you quote a number to a client.

What belongs on the ROC chase sheet

  • Client name, CIN or LLPIN, company type (private / small / OPC / LLP)
  • AGM date (or "not held"), then the AOC-4 due date (+30 days) and MGT-7 due date (+60 days), calculated in the sheet
  • ADT-1 needed (Y/N) and its date
  • Signed financials received (Y/N), XBRL applicable (Y/N)
  • DSC valid and linked for every signer (Y/N), V3 login working (Y/N)
  • Filed date, SRN, and the extension note if MCA issues one
  • Owner on your team for that client

WhatsApp / email script pack

Now: "Hello. Your company's annual ROC filings for FY 2025-26 are due soon. AOC-4 is due within 30 days of your AGM and MGT-7 within 60 days. Please confirm your AGM date and send the signed accounts, board's report and auditor's report by [date]."

DSC check: "Before we file AOC-4 we need each director's DSC to be valid and linked on the MCA portal. Please confirm by [date] whether any DSC has expired, so we can renew it in time."

LLP: "Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) for your LLP is due on 30 October 2026. Please send the figures and confirm which designated partners will sign by [date]."

Sign today: "Your AOC-4 is ready to file. Please sign it with your DSC today between [time] and [time]. It takes about five minutes."

If you mention the ICSI extension request, say clearly that it is a request and not an extension.
