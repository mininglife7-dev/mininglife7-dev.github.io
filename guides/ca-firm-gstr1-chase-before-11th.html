GSTR-1 client chase list for CA firms: the 5 days before the 11th

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 20 Oct 2026): The GST portal's Form GSTR-1 FAQs say monthly filers must file GSTR-1 by the 11th of the following month and quarterly (QRMP) filers by the 13th of the month after the quarter ends, unless the Government extends the date by notification. So the October 2026 return of a monthly filer is due on 11 November 2026, and the October–December 2026 quarterly return is due on 13 January 2027. For monthly clients, the five-day chase below therefore runs from 6 to 10 November 2026. This guide is for CA firms, accountants and GST practitioners who file GSTR-1 for many clients; general information, not tax advice.

What you get from this guide: a finished day-by-day chase list for monthly GSTR-1 (usually due on the 11th), plus a short WhatsApp / email script pack your staff can copy. Split monthly vs QRMP/IFF clients, ask for one concrete invoice pack per message, and keep a shared sheet so no one double-pings the same client. Re-check gst.gov.in every morning in case an extension lands.

Confirm the due date first

  • Monthly filers: GSTR-1 for the previous month is commonly due on the 11th of the current month (example for Oct 2026: Sep 2026 return → typically 11 Oct 2026).
  • QRMP: full GSTR-1 is quarterly (often by the 13th of the month after the quarter ends). In months 1 and 2 of the quarter, clients may upload B2B invoices through the Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) instead of a full monthly GSTR-1. Do not put QRMP clients on the same "before the 11th" sheet without checking their frequency.
  • If CBIC publishes an extension, rewrite the due line on your sheet and tell clients once, with the circular or portal notice named.

QRMP / IFF: keep a separate rhythm

  • Month 1 and 2 of the quarter: if the client uses IFF, chase B2B invoices on the IFF cut-off (commonly the 13th), not the monthly GSTR-1 11th.
  • Month after quarter end: chase the full quarterly GSTR-1 on its own due date (often the 13th).
  • Mark each client row as monthly, QRMP+IFF, or QRMP quarterly only so junior staff do not send the wrong script.

What belongs on the chase sheet

  • Client name, GSTIN, monthly vs QRMP (and IFF yes/no)
  • Last contact date and channel (email / WhatsApp / call)
  • Invoice pack received (Y/N), e-invoice gaps (Y/N), "OK to file" (Y/N/date)
  • Portal due date you are working to (and extension note if any)
  • Owner on your team for that client

WhatsApp / email script pack

Day −5: "Hello — GSTR-1 for [month] is due on [date] for monthly filers. Please send your B2B and B2C invoice list (or the GSTR-1 export from your books) by [Day −3]. Reply to this thread if anything is delayed."

Day −3 follow-up: "Reminder: we still need the [month] invoice pack for GSTR-1 by noon [Day −2]. If data will be late, reply with the expected time so we can plan filing."

Day −1: "Quick confirm: may we file GSTR-1 for [month] with the invoices we already have? Reply YES or send the correction today."

QRMP / IFF (month 1–2): "For QRMP, please send B2B invoices for IFF upload for [month] by [IFF cut-off]. Full GSTR-1 for the quarter will be chased separately."

Do not quote a late-fee amount unless you have calculated it for that client from the portal rules.
