Tax audit report due date: 21 October 2026 (CBDT Circular 7/2026)

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. For FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), the tax-audit report (Form 3CA/3CB with 3CD under s.44AB of the 1961 Act) is due by 21 October 2026, extended from 30 September by CBDT Circular No. 7/2026 dated 28 Sep 2026. The income-tax return for these cases moves to 21 November 2026. Transfer-pricing (s.92E) cases are not covered: their report stays due 31 Oct 2026 and their return 30 Nov 2026.

Who this applies to

The circular covers persons in the audit category of the return due-date table who are not in transfer-pricing cases: companies, other assessees whose accounts must be audited under the Income-tax Act or any other law, and partners of such firms (or a spouse where s.5A applies). Other reports whose due date is tied to the s.44AB date move with it.

Why it matters even if your CA files it

The CA uploads the audit report, but the taxpayer must accept it on the e-filing portal. Reports often wait for that last click. The return itself also needs the audit details, so a late report delays the ITR.

Common mistakes

  • Thinking every ITR moved. If you are not under tax audit, your return date did not change.
  • Treating TP cases the same. s.92E cases keep 31 Oct (report) and 30 Nov (ITR).
  • Forgetting to approve the CA's upload. Log in and accept the report as soon as your CA tells you it is uploaded.
  • Waiting for another extension. Plan for 21 Oct; treat any later extension as a bonus.

Checklist

  • Books closed and shared with your CA
  • CA confirms the upload date
  • You accepted the report on incometax.gov.in
  • ITR planned before 21 Nov 2026

Frequently asked questions

What is the tax audit report due date for AY 2026-27?

21 October 2026, extended from 30 September 2026 by CBDT Circular No. 7/2026 dated 28 September 2026.

What is the ITR due date for tax audit cases for AY 2026-27?

21 November 2026 for non-transfer-pricing audit cases.

Does the extension apply to transfer pricing cases?

No. For s.92E cases the report is still due 31 October 2026 and the return 30 November 2026.

I am salaried. Does this affect me?

No. The extension is only for the audit category. The belated or revised return dates for others are different.
