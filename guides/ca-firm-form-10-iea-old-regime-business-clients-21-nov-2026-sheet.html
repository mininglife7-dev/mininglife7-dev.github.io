Short answer (checked 22 Oct 2026): the new tax regime is the default. An individual, HUF, AOP (other than a co-operative society), BOI or artificial juridical person with business or profession income who wants the old regime must file Form 10-IEA on or before the due date under section 139(1) of the Income-tax Act, 1961; people without business income just choose in the ITR (Rule 21AGA, Income-tax Rules, 1962; the department's old vs new regime FAQs and Form 10-IEA user manual). For AY 2026-27, CBDT Circular No. 07/2026 (28 Sep 2026) moved the section 139(1) due date for audit cases (Sl. No. 2 of the due-date table: audited non-company assessees and partners of audited firms, where section 92E does not apply) from 31 October to 21 November 2026, a Saturday. The circular does not name Form 10-IEA, so file well before and check which due date the portal offers you in the form (step 8 of the user manual). This is a starting point, not tax advice.

The rules the formula follows

  • Business income: old regime only through Form 10-IEA, filed on or before the section 139(1) due date (Rule 21AGA(1)(a); portal FAQs). Without business income, the choice is made in the return filed under section 139(1) (Rule 21AGA(1)(b)).
  • Two forms in a lifetime: one to opt out of the new regime and one to re-enter it (Form 10-IEA user manual, 3.1). Once a business client switches back to the new regime, they cannot choose the old regime again (portal FAQ 11).
  • No take-backs in the same year: a Form 10-IEA filed for an assessment year cannot be revoked or withdrawn for that year (portal FAQ 13). Finish the comparison before filing.
  • Earlier opt-out carries forward: the AY 2026-27 ITR-3 asks whether a Form 10-IEA for the old regime was filed for an earlier year and, if so, for its acknowledgement number; if the client has not re-entered, the return goes in the old regime (Notification No. 47/2026, ITR-3 Part A General).
  • Verification: Form 10-IEA is filed electronically under digital signature or electronic verification code (Rule 21AGA(3)); the user manual walks through e-verification before submission.

Two client messages

1. Regime choice: "Your tax return for FY 2025-26 is due by 21 November 2026. Because you have business income, the new tax regime applies unless we file Form 10-IEA before that date. We are comparing both regimes for you. Please send proof of the deductions you want to claim (for example insurance, investments, home loan interest) by [date], so we can tell you which is lower and file on time."

2. Before going back to the new regime: "You opted for the old regime in an earlier year. The new regime works out lower for FY 2025-26, but for someone with business income, switching back can be done only once, and after that the old regime is not available again. Please reply 'confirm re-entry' if you want us to file Form 10-IEA for this; we will not file it without your written go-ahead."

Notes

  • This sheet is for AY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26) under the Income-tax Act, 1961. From tax year 2026-27 the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies; check its rules separately before reusing this sheet next year.
  • Business clients whose due date was 31 August 2026 and who did not file Form 10-IEA by then stay in the default new regime for AY 2026-27 unless an earlier opt-out still applies; confirm each case on the portal.
  • Re-check the portal before 21 November in case CBDT issues any further order.
