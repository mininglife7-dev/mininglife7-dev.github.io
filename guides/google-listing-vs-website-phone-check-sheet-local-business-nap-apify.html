Why check the two numbers against each other

  • Customers call the number they see first. If the Google listing and the website disagree, one of them often rings an old line, a former owner or nobody. A Different row is a lost call you can fix in five minutes.
  • Google asks for it. Its Business Profile guidelines (read 27 Oct 2026) say to represent your business "as it's consistently represented and recognized in the real world", to provide a phone number "that connects to your individual business location", and to use a local number instead of a central call-centre helpline whenever possible. We do not claim anything about rankings; the point here is that the number works and is the same everywhere.
  • Branches drift. A chain with ten locations often has one branch page still showing the head-office number. The sheet shows that on one screen.

Step 1: get the Google listings

Run a Google Maps scraper on Apify for your search (for example "physiotherapist in Hyderabad") or for your own list of places. The popular Google Maps Scraper (made by Compass, not by us) returns title, website and phoneUnformatted (the phone in international format, like +17183565168) for each place. Note the run's dataset ID.

Agency checking existing clients? Skip this step and type the client list straight into the scrape tab (see the free route below).

Step 2: run the contact scraper on those websites

Open the website contact scraper and set the input like this:

{ "datasetId": "PASTE_THE_MAPS_DATASET_ID", "datasetCarryFields": ["title", "phoneUnformatted", "address", "placeId"], "maxPagesPerSite": 5, "defaultCountry": "IN", "checkEmailMx": false }

What it does, as written in the Actor's input schema and code on 27 October 2026:

  • Reads each item's website field and ignores items without one (counted as datasetItemsWithoutWebsite). Google Maps links are never treated as the business website.
  • datasetCarryFields replaces the default list, so keep title in it. The carried values land in sourceItems on each output row.
  • Reads the homepage plus the most contact-like pages (contact, about, imprint, support). Phones are returned in international format in phones/N/phone, with phones/N/foundOn (the first page it was seen on) and phones/N/source (for example a tap-to-call link or a WhatsApp link).
  • Duplicate domains are removed, so a chain whose branches share one website gives one row; its sourceItems list holds up to 20 branches.
  • Follows robots.txt and reads each site's terms, so some rows come back as blocked-by-terms, blocked-by-tos-list or blocked-by-robots. Those you check by hand.

Export the dataset as CSV. In your sheet use File → Import → Upload, choose "Insert new sheet", untick "Convert text to numbers, dates and formulas" (otherwise +91... numbers lose their plus sign), and rename the tab scrape. Headers look like sourceItems/0/phoneUnformatted and phones/0/phone, so the formulas find columns by header name.

Free route

For a handful of businesses, make a scrape tab with the headers domain, status (type ok), sourceItems/0/title, sourceItems/0/phoneUnformatted, phones/0/phone, phones/0/foundOn, phones/1/phone, phones/1/foundOn. Copy the number from the Google listing and from the website, both written as +, country code, then digits with no spaces. The formulas work the same.

Step 3: build the check tab

Create a tab named check, put these headers in row 1 and these formulas in row 2, and fill down as many rows as scrape has.

ColHeaderFormula (row 2)
ABusiness=IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("sourceItems/0/title", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), "")
BDomain=IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("domain", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), "")
CStatus=IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("status", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), "")
DGoogle phone=IFERROR(REGEXREPLACE(TO_TEXT(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH("sourceItems/0/phoneUnformatted", scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0))), "[^0-9+]", ""), "")
EWebsite phones=IFERROR(TEXTJOIN(", ", TRUE, FILTER(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, REGEXMATCH(scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, "^phones/\d+/phone$"))), "")
FResult=IFS(C2<>"ok", "Skipped", AND(D2="", E2=""), "No phone anywhere", D2="", "No phone on Google", E2="", "No phone on website", ISNUMBER(SEARCH(D2, E2)), "Match", TRUE, "Different")
GFound on=IFERROR(INDEX(scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, MATCH(SUBSTITUTE(INDEX(scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, MATCH(D2, scrape!$A2:$ZZ2, 0)), "/phone", "/foundOn"), scrape!$A$1:$ZZ$1, 0)), "")
HNext step=IFS(F2="Match", "OK: same number on Google and the website", F2="Different", "Ask the owner which number is right, then fix the other place", F2="No phone on website", "Add the number to the contact page and footer as a tap-to-call link", F2="No phone on Google", "Owner adds a phone to the Google Business Profile", F2="Skipped", "Check the website by hand", TRUE, "Find a working number before anything else")

Notes on the formulas:

  • Column D strips spaces and dashes so +91 40 1234 5678 and +914012345678 compare as the same number.
  • Column G finds which phones/N/phone header holds the Google number, swaps it for phones/N/foundOn and returns that page.
  • A Different row is not always wrong: a clinic may list a reception line on Google and a mobile on the website. That is why the next step is to ask the owner, not to change anything yourself.
  • Chains: if sourceItems/1/phoneUnformatted exists, add columns for the other branches with the same formula as D and E, one per branch.

Step 4: work the list

Add a tab named plan. In A1: =QUERY(check!A2:H, "select F, count(B) where B is not null group by F order by count(B) desc label F 'Result', count(B) 'Businesses'", 0). Fix your own or your clients' Different and No phone on website rows first; they are the ones losing calls today.

  • Call the number on each Different row before changing anything. Write down which one answered and the date.
  • Only the owner or a manager of the Google Business Profile can edit the listing. Send them the right number and the link to the listing.
  • On the website, make the number a tap-to-call link (tel:+914012345678) in the footer, so every page has it.
  • Re-run the two scrapers a week later and check that the rows turned to Match.

Agencies: a Different row on a business that is not your client is a reason to offer help, not to send bulk email. Contact one business at a time, say how you found the mismatch, and honour every "no".

Frequently asked questions

Do I need both scrapers?

No. For a few businesses, copy both numbers by hand into the scrape tab. The scrapers save time when you check dozens or hundreds.

Why does a number show as Different when it looks the same?

Usually one side lost its plus sign or country code on import. Re-import the CSV with "Convert text to numbers, dates and formulas" unticked.

Does the contact scraper read Google Maps?

No. It reads only each business's own website. The Google listing data comes from a separate Google Maps scraper whose dataset you pass in.
