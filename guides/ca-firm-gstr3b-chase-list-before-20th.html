GSTR-3B client chase list for CA firms: the 5 days before the 20th

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer: treat the five calendar days before the monthly GSTR-3B due date (usually the 20th) as a fixed chase week. Split clients into monthly vs QRMP, ask for one concrete thing per message, and keep a shared sheet so no one double-pings the same client. Re-check gst.gov.in every morning in case an extension lands.

Confirm the due date first

  • Monthly filers: GSTR-3B for the previous month is commonly due on the 20th of the current month (example for Oct 2026: Sep 2026 return → typically 20 Oct 2026).
  • QRMP: quarterly GSTR-3B due dates differ by state category (often the 22nd or 24th). Do not use the 20th chase week for QRMP clients without checking their category.
  • If CBIC publishes an extension, rewrite the due line on your sheet and tell clients once, with the circular or portal notice named.

What belongs on the chase sheet

  • Client name, GSTIN, monthly vs QRMP
  • Last contact date and channel (email / WhatsApp / call)
  • Documents received (Y/N) and "OK to file" (Y/N/date)
  • Portal due date you are working to (and extension note if any)
  • Owner on your team for that client

Message templates that stay polite

Day −5: "Hello — GSTR-3B for [month] is due on [date] for monthly filers. Please send your sales and purchase summaries by [Day −3]. Reply to this thread if anything is delayed."

Day −1: "Quick confirm: may we file GSTR-3B for [month] with the figures we already have? Reply YES or send the correction today."

Do not claim a late fee amount unless you have calculated it for that client from the portal rules.
