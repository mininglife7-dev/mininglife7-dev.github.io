Short answer: Both run on Apify Store. Apify's own E-commerce Scraping Tool works on almost any shop or marketplace (its page names Amazon, eBay, Zalando, Otto, Lidl, Decathlon, Shopify and WooCommerce sites and more): give it product URLs, category URLs or keywords and it returns name, price, identifiers, brand, stock status and variants, and it can also collect reviews and seller details or add an AI summary. It charges per event. Its pricing (read from the Actor's public Apify listing, checked 28 Oct 2026) is $0.006 per product on the Apify Free plan, $0.0015 on Bronze, $0.00125 on Silver and $0.001 on Gold (the Store page shows “from $1.00 / 1,000 product details”), plus fees per listing page and optional fees when a site needs residential proxies, an unblocker or browser rendering. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker only reads Shopify and WooCommerce shops, takes just the shop URL, returns one row per product with variants, price, compare-at price, stock, SKU/GTIN, images and URLs plus a per-shop summary, and has no Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you watch Amazon, other marketplaces or big retailers, pick Apify's tool. If your competitors are small Shopify and WooCommerce shops, try ours.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Apify's E-commerce Scraping Tool cost?

Its public Apify listing (checked 28 Oct 2026) charges per event: $0.006 per product on the Apify Free plan, $0.0015 on Bronze, $0.00125 on Silver and $0.001 on Gold, with lower prices on higher plans. Listing pages, residential proxy, unblocker and browser rendering are charged as separate events where used.

Does it work on Shopify and WooCommerce?

Yes. Its FAQ lists Shopify stores and WooCommerce sites among the platforms it works with, along with marketplaces such as Amazon and eBay.

Can either Actor show price history?

No. Both capture current data. Schedule weekly runs and compare the datasets to build your own history.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Can I use both?

Yes. Use Apify's tool for marketplaces and big retailers, and ours for the small Shopify and WooCommerce shops on your list.
