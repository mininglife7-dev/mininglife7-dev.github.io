The pain this page is about

“I set up alerts on ten competitor product pages, and now I get emails I don't read. What I need is their full price list, every week, in one sheet.” Page monitoring is about noticing a change. Price research is about seeing all the prices at once.

Who should pick Visualping instead

  • You watch specific pages on any kind of website (regulator notices, tender pages, a price list) and want to know when they change.
  • You want AI summaries of each change and alerts in Slack, Teams or Telegram.
  • The pages need a login or clicks before the content shows.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want a complete product and price list for each competitor shop, not one alert per page.
  • Your competitors run Shopify or WooCommerce, and you want variants, compare-at prices and stock flags.
  • You want to sort and chart the data yourself in a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Visualping cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Free (5 pages, 150 checks a month), Personal 1K at $14 a month or $120 a year, Personal 5K at $35 or $300 a year, Personal 10K at $70 or $600 a year, and Business plans from $140 a month or $1,200 a year up to $350 a month or $3,000 a year. Solutions plans are quoted.

Does Visualping have a free trial?

Its FAQ says a Business trial gives 14 days of Business features with no card; when it ends, monitoring stops unless you pick a plan, and nothing is charged automatically.

What is Visualping's refund policy?

Its FAQ says that if you cancel an annual plan early, unused months are refunded without the annual discount.

Does the Unadhi Actor alert me when a price changes?

No. Each run exports the current catalogue. Schedule it in Apify and compare runs in a sheet, or use an Apify webhook for your own alert.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
