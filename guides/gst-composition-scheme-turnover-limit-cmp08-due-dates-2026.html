GST composition scheme: turnover limit and CMP-08 due dates (2026)

Last updated 14 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Under GST composition you pay a fixed rate on turnover instead of normal GST on every invoice. The portal checks your PAN-based aggregate annual turnover against the category you chose: goods/restaurant composition is commonly capped at ₹1.5 crore (₹75 lakh in special-category states), and composition for most services under s.10(2A) is capped at ₹50 lakh (GST portal: opt for composition; CMP-08 FAQs). You file Form GST CMP-08 every quarter by the 18th of the month after the quarter (or a later date if the Government notifies an extension), and an annual return in GSTR-4.

Why composition exists

Smaller businesses often find full invoice-level GST (GSTR-1 + GSTR-3B every month or quarter) heavy. Composition trades that paperwork for a flat rate and a quarterly payment statement, with limits so it stays for genuinely small suppliers.

Common mistakes

  • Collecting tax on invoices. Composition suppliers issue a bill of supply, not a tax invoice with GST charged to the customer.
  • Crossing the limit mid-year and staying silent. Once turnover crosses the threshold, you must exit composition and follow normal returns.
  • Missing the 18th. CMP-08 is a payment statement; late filing attracts late fee and interest on unpaid tax.
  • Forgetting GSTR-4. The annual return is still due after the year ends (see Important Dates on gst.gov.in).

Checklist

  • Confirm your composition category and turnover ceiling on the GST portal
  • Calendar reminders for the 18th after each quarter
  • Tax paid with CMP-08, acknowledgment saved
  • No GST collected from customers on composition supplies
  • GSTR-4 date noted for the financial year

Frequently asked questions

What is the CMP-08 due date?

The 18th of the month after the quarter, or a later date if the Government extends it by notification.

What is the composition turnover limit for services under section 10(2A)?

₹50 lakh, according to the GST portal CMP-08 FAQ.

Do composition taxpayers file GSTR-3B?

No. Composition taxpayers file CMP-08 quarterly and GSTR-4 annually, not the normal GSTR-1/GSTR-3B cycle.

Where do I check current composition timelines?

On the Important Dates section of https://www.gst.gov.in/ and the portal composition help pages.
