EU AI Act dates after the Digital Omnibus: one-page deadline sheet for small EU businesses (2 December 2026, 2 December 2027, 2 August 2028)

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the EURO AI Register mentioned below is an Unadhi product with a free edition. This guide is for information only, not legal advice.

What you get: a finished EU AI Act deadline sheet you can paste into Google Sheets or Excel: every date that matters to a small business, what starts on that date, who it hits, a yes/no column for your business, the action to take, an owner and a done date. Plus a short "do this before 2 December 2026" list and a checklist. Every date is taken from Article 113 of the AI Act as consolidated after the Digital Omnibus (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744), as shown on the Commission's AI Act Service Desk.

The short version

  • Already in force for every business using AI: AI literacy (Article 4) and the banned practices (Article 5), since 2 February 2025. National enforcement of the bans and of the Article 50 transparency rules (chatbots, deepfakes, AI-generated text published to inform the public) started on 2 August 2026.
  • Next date: 2 December 2026. New bans on AI that generates non-consensual intimate images or child sexual abuse material, and the end of the grace period for providers of older generative AI systems to mark their output as AI-made.
  • High-risk rules moved. The Digital Omnibus pushed the high-risk rules to 2 December 2027 for Annex III uses (such as recruitment, staff management and creditworthiness) and to 2 August 2028 for AI built into regulated products under Annex I.

One change small businesses should know about: Article 4 was amended by the Digital Omnibus. It still says providers and deployers "shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy" of their staff, and it now adds that this "does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual". The Commission and Member States must support SMEs in meeting it, and the Commission will publish practical examples.

Do this before 2 December 2026

  • List every AI tool and every use of it (SME AI inventory template)
  • Run each tool against the banned practices, including the two new bans (Article 5 list)
  • Record the AI literacy training you have given, with dates and names (Article 4 guide)
  • Check that any chatbot on your site tells people it is AI, and that published AI images and AI news text are labelled (Article 50 chatbot checklist, AI content label sheet)
  • Ask your image or video generation supplier how it marks output as AI-made
  • Note which tools might be high-risk in December 2027, and which role you hold for each (Annex III check, role check sheet)
