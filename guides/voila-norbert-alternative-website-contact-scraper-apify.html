Short answer: Voila Norbert is an email finder: you give it a person's name and company domain and it looks for that person's address, one by one, in bulk from a CSV, by API or from its Chrome extension. Its pricing page (checked 23 Oct 2026) shows 50 free credits on sign-up, and plans of Valet (up to 1,000 leads a month) at $49 a month or $39 a month on the yearly toggle, Butler (5,000) at $99/$79, Advisor (15,000) at $249/$199 and Counselor (50,000) at $499/$399, with unlimited team members and credit rollover; it says only successful finds are counted. If you have names, Norbert is the right kind of tool. If you only have a list of business websites and want the inbox, phone and WhatsApp each one publishes, our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does that, free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage.

The pain this page is about

“I have 300 dental clinics and dealers, but no names. Who do I even search for?” An email finder needs a first name, last name and domain. A website scraper needs only the domain and returns what the business has chosen to publish.

Who should pick Voila Norbert instead

  • You know the names (from LinkedIn, an event list, a press release) and need each person's work email.
  • You want email verification or enrichment on a list you already have, pay as you go.
  • You want a team account with unlimited members and credits that roll over.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You start from businesses, not people: a Google Maps export, a directory or a member list.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp as well as email, which many Indian SMEs publish instead of a manager's address.
  • You prefer shared role inboxes by default and the source page of every contact.

Frequently asked questions

Is Voila Norbert free?

Its FAQ (checked 23 Oct 2026) says you get 50 free credits once you create an account. Paid plans start with Valet at $49 a month, or $39 a month on the yearly toggle.

Does Voila Norbert charge if it does not find the email?

Its FAQ says only successful finds are counted.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a specific person's email from their name?

No. It does not guess or construct addresses. It returns what each company's website publishes and, by default, keeps only shared role inboxes.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many teams scrape the website for the general inbox, phone and WhatsApp first, then use an email finder only for the few named decision-makers they need.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
