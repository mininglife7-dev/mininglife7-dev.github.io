Short answer: Pricefy is price-monitoring software: AI product matching, competitor discovery, daily price updates, alerts and, from Pro, dynamic repricing. Its pricing page (checked 21 Oct 2026) lists Free at $0 (up to 50 SKUs, 5 competitors, 1 daily update), Starter $49 a month ($37 billed annually, 100 SKUs), Pro $99 ($74; 2,000 SKUs, dynamic repricing), Business $189 ($142; 15,000 SKUs) and Enterprise $499 ($374; 25,000 SKUs). If you want ongoing monitoring of your own products against competitors, Pricefy (even its free plan) is the better pick. If you need every product and price of a few independent Shopify or WooCommerce competitors before Black Friday on 27 November 2026, our Actor reads their whole catalogue in one run, is free during launch, and you pay only Apify platform usage.

Who should pick Pricefy instead

  • You want daily monitoring of your own products with alerts and reports — and up to 50 SKUs fits its free plan.
  • You want automatic product matching and competitor discovery rather than a spreadsheet.
  • You want repricing rules or MAP/MSRP monitoring.
  • You track marketplace sellers.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You need a competitor's whole catalogue (titles, variants, prices, compare-at prices, stock) once or before each sale, not just your matched SKUs.
  • Your competitors are independent Shopify or WooCommerce shops.
  • You want no subscription and to test inside Apify's free $5 monthly usage.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pricefy have a free plan?

Yes. Its pricing page (checked 21 Oct 2026) lists a Free plan at $0 for up to 50 SKUs and 5 competitors with one daily price update, AI automatch and competitor discovery, and says no credit card is required.

How much is Pricefy Starter?

$49 a month billed monthly, or $37 a month billed annually, for up to 100 SKUs with unlimited competitors and two daily updates.

Does the Unadhi Actor send price-change alerts?

No. It exports the catalogue each time you run it. You can schedule runs on Apify and compare datasets yourself, but there are no built-in alerts or repricing.

Can the Unadhi Actor scrape Amazon or Flipkart prices?

No. It refuses marketplaces by design and works only on independent Shopify, WooCommerce and schema.org shops.
