Who should pick Browse AI instead

  • You want to monitor pages that are not product catalogues, or need screenshots and visual change alerts.
  • You want a no-code recorder and built-in monitoring with integrations, for a team.
  • Your competitor sites have no structured product data our Actor can read.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your competitors run independent Shopify or WooCommerce stores.
  • You want full catalogues (hundreds of products) without per-row credits.
  • You are happy to export a CSV and compare in a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Browse AI cost?

Browse AI's pricing page (read 14 Oct 2026) lists a Free plan, Personal at $19 a month and Professional at $69 a month billed annually, and Premium from $500 a month billed annually. Monthly billing is priced higher; check the live page.

What is a Browse AI credit?

Per Browse AI's FAQ, one credit extracts 10 rows of data from a page or captures one screenshot; premium sites have a minimum of 2 to 10 credits per run.

Does the Unadhi Actor alert me when a price changes?

No built-in alerts. You can schedule runs on Apify and compare the datasets, or load them into a sheet that highlights changes.

Is the Actor free?

The Actor charges nothing during launch; you pay Apify platform usage. Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
