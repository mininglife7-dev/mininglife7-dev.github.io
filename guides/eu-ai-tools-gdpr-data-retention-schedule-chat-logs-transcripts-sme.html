How long should you keep AI chat logs, transcripts and prompts? A GDPR retention schedule for small EU businesses

Updated 22 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the EURO AI Register mentioned below is an Unadhi product with a free edition. This guide is for information only, not legal advice.

What you get: a finished retention schedule for the data your AI tools keep, with seven rows already filled in (chatbot chats, call transcripts, staff prompts, uploaded files, saved outputs, high-risk system logs and supplier copies). Each row has the purpose, a suggested period to adjust, where to delete it, who checks and the next check date. Plus the privacy-notice line to copy.

The short answer

The GDPR sets no fixed number of days. It says personal data must not be kept in a form that identifies people for longer than the purpose needs (Article 5(1)(e)), and Recital 39 says you should set time limits for erasure or for a periodic review. So for each kind of data your AI tools keep, pick a period, write down the reason, tell people (Article 13(2)(a)), put it in your records (Article 30(1)(f)), and set the tool to delete by default where it can (Article 25(2) covers the period of storage).

The periods in brackets are examples, not legal rules. Your national tax, accounting, employment or sector law may require a minimum period for some records, and an open complaint or legal claim can justify keeping specific items longer. Write the reason next to any period you choose.

Copy this privacy-notice line

We keep chats with our website assistant for [90 days] and call transcripts for [30 days], then delete them automatically, unless you have an open complaint, in which case we keep the relevant messages until it is closed. Our AI suppliers delete the data when our contract ends.

Do this week

  • List each AI tool and what it stores: chats, transcripts, prompts, files, outputs
  • Open each tool's admin settings and note its default retention and history setting
  • Pick a period for each row and write the reason
  • Switch on auto-delete or shorter history where the tool allows it
  • Add the periods to your privacy notice and your Article 30 records
  • Put a review date in the calendar, at least once a year
