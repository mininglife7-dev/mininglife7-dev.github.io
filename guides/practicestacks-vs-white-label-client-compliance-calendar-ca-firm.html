Short answer: They do different jobs. PracticeStacks is practice management software for Indian CA, CS and tax firms, with 12 modules in every plan: tasks, a compliance emailer that notifies clients about upcoming deadlines, client records with portal logins, billing, reimbursements, team management, dashboards, WhatsApp + AI reminders, leads, proposals, OTP e-sign and onboarding. Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Growth at ₹749 a month or ₹7,490 a year and Scale at ₹999 a month or ₹9,999 a year (Scale adds Gmail and Outlook sync), each including 5 users, prices plus GST, with a 14-day free trial and a 30-day money-back offer. Our White-Label Client Compliance Calendar is not software: four quarterly packs (DOCX, PDF, WhatsApp image, .ics, .xlsx) with your firm's name, for unlimited clients, at ₹999 a year in total. If you want to run tasks, billing and client reminders in one system, PracticeStacks is the better pick. If you only need a branded calendar every client can see, start with ours.

The pain this page is about

“Clients keep calling to ask when GST and TDS are due. I don't need new software for my three-person office; I want one clean calendar with my firm's name that I can send on WhatsApp every quarter.” Practice software runs the office. A client calendar is a single, branded page your clients read.

Who should pick PracticeStacks instead

  • Your firm wants one system for tasks, billing, proposals and client reminders, with several staff logging in.
  • You want reminders sent per client automatically by email and WhatsApp, not a shared calendar.
  • You want GST, Income Tax and TDS notices fetched and tracked (its ₹4,999/year add-on).

Who the calendar fits better

  • A small practice that wants clients to see the national dates under the firm's own name, with no new software.
  • A firm that already has practice software but wants a client-facing, branded page to share each quarter.
  • Anyone who wants to see a free sample before paying anything.

You can use both: software runs your office; the branded calendar keeps every client aware of the dates.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PracticeStacks cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Growth at ₹749 a month or ₹7,490 a year and Scale at ₹999 a month or ₹9,999 a year, each with 5 users, all prices plus GST. Extra users cost ₹149 a month on Growth and ₹199 a month on Scale.

Does PracticeStacks have a free trial or refund?

Its page says there is a 14-day free trial with nothing charged until it ends, and a 30-day money-back offer.

Does PracticeStacks remind clients about deadlines?

Yes. Its module list includes a compliance emailer to notify clients about upcoming deadlines and WhatsApp + AI automated reminders.

Is the ₹999 calendar a subscription?

Yes, a yearly Gumroad membership that renews unless cancelled. Year one gives four quarterly packs with your firm's name. 14-day refund from when you receive the first pack.

Can I use both?

Yes. Practice software runs your firm's tasks and per-client reminders; the branded calendar is a simple page every client can keep.
