Short answer: Tomba.io is an email finder and verifier: it finds a person's work email from a name and domain, lists the addresses it holds for a company domain with the public pages where each was found, verifies addresses, and finds phone numbers. Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan with 25 searches every 30 days and domain search capped at 10 addresses, then Basic $49 a month (2,500 searches + 2,500 verifications), Growth $89 (5,000 + 5,000), Pro $199 (20,000 + 20,000) and Pro Plus $449 (50,000 + 50,000), with 25% off billed yearly. Searches that find nothing cost nothing. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does not hold a database or guess addresses: it visits each company's own website now and returns the role emails, phones, WhatsApp numbers and social links published there, with the page each came from. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need named people's verified emails or an API at high volume, pick Tomba. If you want what each business publishes on its own site today, including WhatsApp, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I need to contact 200 local businesses. Half of them list a WhatsApp number on their website and never read email.” An email finder answers “what is this person's address?”. A website contact scraper answers “how does this business ask to be contacted?”.

Who should pick Tomba.io instead

  • You need the verified work email of a named person, or bulk verification before a campaign.
  • You want an API with high rate limits, team roles and SSO, or a Google Sheets/Excel add-in.
  • You want addresses a company has published anywhere on the web, not only on its own site.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your list is small businesses whose own site shows a phone or WhatsApp button.
  • You want a fresh read of the site today, with the page each detail came from.
  • You already use Apify (for example, a Google Maps Actor) and want one platform bill.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Tomba.io cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Free (25 searches every 30 days), Basic $49 a month with 2,500 searches and 2,500 verifications, Growth $89 with 5,000 + 5,000, Pro $199 with 20,000 + 20,000 and Pro Plus $449 with 50,000 + 50,000. Yearly billing takes 25% off. Larger Scale plans (from $899 a month) are sales-assisted, and Enterprise is $4,999 a month on an annual contract.

Does Tomba charge when no email is found?

Its page says searches that find nothing cost nothing, and repeating the same request in the same usage window is free.

What is Tomba's refund policy?

Its FAQ says refunds apply only to your most recent payment, within 72 hours of the charge, and only if you used less than 10% or 1,000 credits (whichever is lower) of that purchase.

Does the Unadhi Actor find a person's email from their name?

No. It does not guess or construct addresses and has no database. It reads the websites you list and reports what each publishes, role inboxes only unless you turn personal addresses on.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
