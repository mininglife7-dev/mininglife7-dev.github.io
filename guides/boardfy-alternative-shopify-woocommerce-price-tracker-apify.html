Short answer: Boardfy is a price monitoring and dynamic pricing platform for online shops and brands, with its app in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French and German. Its e-commerce pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) is a plan builder: monitoring of marketplaces and price comparison sites (Amazon, Google Shopping, idealo and others) starts at €49 a month per channel/country for 1,000 products, with every seller on a shared product detected automatically; direct competitor websites are added at €39.90 per competitor per month on top of the plan. Add-ons are Campaign Optimizer (+€49 per channel/country), Dynamic Pricing (+€99 per channel/country) and the API (+€39 a month), with an optional product matching service. Prices exclude VAT, annual billing gives two months free, there is a 7-day free trial with no card, and its FAQ says prices are tracked with a maximum delay of 6 hours. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does a narrower job: it reads public product and price rows from the Shopify and WooCommerce shops you list. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only; no marketplaces, matching, alerts or repricing. If you sell on marketplaces and comparison sites, Boardfy covers that. If you want an occasional export of competitor shop catalogues, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Who undercuts us on idealo and Google Shopping, and by how much?” That is a marketplace-monitoring job. “What does every product on these two competitor Shopify shops cost today?” is a catalogue-export job.

Who should pick Boardfy instead

  • You sell on Amazon, Google Shopping or idealo and want every competing seller's price per product.
  • You want dynamic pricing rules or bid adjustments by price position on Google, Meta or TikTok.
  • You want a hosted tool with alerts, history, unlimited users and a Spanish, Portuguese, French or German interface.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You only need competitor shop websites (Shopify or WooCommerce), not marketplaces.
  • You want whole catalogues now and then, without a per-competitor monthly fee.
  • You compare prices yourself in a sheet.
