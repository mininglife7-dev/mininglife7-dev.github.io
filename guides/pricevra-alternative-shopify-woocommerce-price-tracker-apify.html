Pricevra or Unadhi's price tracker? Scheduled competitor price alerts vs a pay-per-use competitor price sheet

Competitor facts below come only from their own public pages, read on 17 Nov 2026; each is linked in Sources. We did not log in, start trials or read paywalled material. Short answer: Pricevra watches the competitor product pages you choose, checks their prices on a schedule, keeps the history and shows an alert in your dashboard when a change reaches your threshold. It is built for Shopify, WooCommerce and PrestaShop shops and says its data is hosted in Europe. Its page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Free at €0 for life (10 products, 2 competitors each, 1 check a day, 14-day history), Starter at €19 a month or €190 a year (50 products, 3 competitors, a check every 12 hours), Pro at €59 a month or €590 a year (200 products, 5 competitors, every 6 hours, WhatsApp and SMS alerts too) and Business at €99 a month or €990 a year (500 products, 25 competitors, every 3 hours, REST API). Prices exclude tax. The same page says online payment is not open yet and there is no catalogue CSV export route today. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does a different job: give it competitor shop URLs and it returns their whole public product and price catalogue as rows for your own sheet, plus a summary of skipped shops. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It sends no alerts and keeps no history for you. If you want a few matched products watched and alerts on change, Pricevra (even its free plan) is built for that. If you want competitors' full catalogues in a spreadsheet now and then, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Tell me when the shop down the road cuts the price of our best-selling serum.” That is an alert job. “Give me every product and price three competitors list, in one sheet, before our Black Friday planning” is an export job.

A function-and-price summary in our own words, not a review.

Who should pick Pricevra instead

  • You want alerts when a competitor's price moves past a threshold, with history kept for you.
  • You track a small list of your own products: its free plan covers 10.
  • You run PrestaShop as well as Shopify or WooCommerce.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want competitors' whole catalogues, not a short matched list.
  • You want the data in a spreadsheet (CSV or Excel) today.
  • You check competitors occasionally and do not want a monthly plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Pricevra cost?

Its page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Free at €0 for life, Starter at €19 a month (€190 a year), Pro at €59 (€590) and Business at €99 (€990), excluding tax. It also says online payment is not open yet.

Can I export Pricevra data to CSV?

Its page says there is no catalogue CSV export route today; you can download your account data as a ZIP with a JSON file, and Business includes a REST API.

Does the Unadhi Actor send price alerts?

No. It returns public product and price rows each time you run it; you compare them in your own sheet or schedule runs on Apify.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 17 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
