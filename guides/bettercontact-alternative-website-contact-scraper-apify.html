Short answer: BetterContact is a waterfall enrichment tool: it asks 20+ data providers in turn for a person's work email or mobile number and verifies what it finds. Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) lists Starter at $15 a month for 200 credits, Pro from $49 a month for 1,000 credits (higher monthly credit steps up to 50,000 are offered), and Enterprise from $799 a month above 20,000 credits. One mobile number costs 10 credits and one email 1 credit; you start with 50 free credits; the page says you are charged only for valid data, unused credits roll over (capped at your subscribed amount), and on Pro its connect-scoring charges only for numbers scored likely to connect. Annual billing gives two months free. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a different job: it visits each website in your list and returns the inboxes, phones and WhatsApp links that business publishes, with the source page. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only (Apify Free includes $5 of usage a month). If you need named people's mobiles and emails, BetterContact is built for that. If your list is business websites, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“My reps need the direct mobile of 150 named buyers.” That is a person-enrichment job. “I have 150 supplier websites and need the shared inbox, phone and WhatsApp each one shows” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick BetterContact instead

  • You need named people's mobile numbers or work emails for calling or outbound sales.
  • You want several data providers checked in one waterfall and to pay only for valid results.
  • You already work in HubSpot, Clay or Google Sheets and want enrichment inside them.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your input is a list of business websites, not a list of people.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp numbers as the business printed them, with the page they came from.
  • You want shared role inboxes by default and no person-level data.

How many BetterContact credits does a mobile number cost?

Its pricing page says 1 mobile phone = 10 credits and 1 email = 1 credit.
