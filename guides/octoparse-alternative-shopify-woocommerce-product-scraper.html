An honest limit of our Actor

Many shops forbid scraping in their terms, and Shopify's default terms do. In our own test of 32 shop URLs on 29 Sep 2026, the Actor skipped 9 shops for their terms, 2 because robots.txt hid the terms page, 5 marketplaces, 1 robots.txt block and 1 rate limit; 2 had no machine-readable products. It returned 536 products from 11 shops, and on a re-check of 88 products the title and price matched the page 88/88. Skipped shops were not charged. If you need data from shops that forbid scraping, we will not help — and you should think twice with any tool.

Frequently asked questions

What is an Octoparse alternative for scraping Shopify and WooCommerce products?

If your competitors run independent Shopify or WooCommerce shops, Unadhi's Product + Price Tracker on Apify reads their structured product data directly, with no scraper to build per shop. It returns title, brand, price, sale price, stock, SKU, variants and images. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Actor is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage, and Apify's free plan includes $5 a month.

How much does Octoparse cost in 2026?

Octoparse's pricing page, read on 14 Oct 2026, lists a free-forever plan with 10 tasks and local extraction, Standard from $69 a month and Professional at $249 a month, both billed annually and 16% less than monthly. Paid plans have a 5-day money-back window. Enterprise is custom.

How does the Unadhi Actor read a Shopify or WooCommerce shop?

It tries Shopify's products.json first, then the WooCommerce Store API, then schema.org JSON-LD or microdata, and finally a last-resort heuristic. You paste the shop URL; there is no workflow to record. The output is one row per product with prices, stock and variants.

Will the Actor scrape any shop I give it?

No. It checks robots.txt and scans each shop's terms, skips shops that forbid scraping, and never fetches Amazon, eBay, Flipkart and about 70 other marketplaces. Many Shopify shops are skipped because Shopify's default terms forbid scraping. Skipped shops are not charged, and a summary record tells you which were skipped and why.

When is Octoparse the better choice?

Pick Octoparse when you need sites that are not shops, such as directories, listings or news, or pages with no structured product data. It also suits people who prefer recording a point-and-click workflow on their own computer, or need one tool for many site types with scheduling and integrations.

Can the Unadhi Actor scrape Amazon or Flipkart?

No. Marketplaces whose terms forbid scraping, including Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Etsy and Flipkart, are refused and never fetched.

Why did the Actor skip a Shopify shop?

Shopify's default terms forbid scraping, and the Actor skips shops whose terms or robots.txt say no. The SHOPS_SUMMARY record names the reason. Skipped shops cost nothing.

Is the Actor free?

The Actor charges nothing during launch. You pay Apify for platform usage; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
