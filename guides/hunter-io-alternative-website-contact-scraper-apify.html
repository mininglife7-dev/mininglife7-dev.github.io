Who should pick Hunter.io instead

  • You need a specific person's work email (for example the purchasing head), not the company's shared inbox.
  • You already have a list of addresses and want them verified before a campaign.
  • You want finding, verifying and sequences in one tool, shared across a team.
  • You do not want to run cloud jobs or read JSON at all.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You start from a list of websites (a Google Maps run, a directory, a trade-fair list) and want what each site publishes.
  • You care about phones and WhatsApp as much as email — common for Indian SMEs, clinics, shops and dealers.
  • You want a privacy-first default: role mailboxes only, robots.txt respected, sites whose terms forbid scraping skipped.
  • You want to pay nothing to the developer during launch and keep costs inside Apify's free $5 monthly usage while you test.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Unadhi contact scraper really free?

The Actor itself charges nothing during launch. It is a pay-per-usage Actor, so you pay Apify for the platform resources your run uses. Apify's free plan includes $5 of platform usage a month, per apify.com/pricing (read 14 Oct 2026).

Does Hunter.io have a free plan?

Yes. Hunter's pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 with 50 credits a month that do not roll over. Paid plans start at Starter, $49 a month or $34 a month billed yearly.

Can the Apify Actor find a named person's email like Hunter's Email Finder?

Not by default. It only reads what each website publishes and returns shared role mailboxes. Personal addresses found on a site are dropped unless you switch them on, which you should only do with a lawful basis under the DPDP Act or GDPR.

Which is cheaper for 1,000 websites?

We cannot give an honest single number for the Actor, because Apify usage depends on the sites. Run 10 to 20 sites first and read the usage in Apify billing. Hunter's cost depends on how many emails are found and verified against your credit plan.

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
