MSME Form 1 chase list for CA firms: April–September 2026 return due 31 October 2026

Updated 19 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 19 Oct 2026): The half-yearly MSME Form 1 for April–September 2026 is due on Saturday 31 October 2026. Under the 2019 Specified Companies Order made under section 405 of the Companies Act, 2013, as amended on 15 July 2024 (S.O. 2751(E)), only companies that have payments pending to a micro or small enterprise for more than 45 days from acceptance (or deemed acceptance) of the goods or services file it. The revised form is supplier-wise, by PAN, and it also asks for dues that were paid after 45 days during the half-year. So a company that cleared a late bill on 28 September may still have to file. Sources: the MCA MSME Form I page and the text of S.O. 2751(E). This is a starting point, not legal advice. Confirm on the MCA portal before you file.

What you get from this guide: a finished 10-day chase plan for your company clients, ending with MSME Form 1 filed by 31 October 2026. Sort every company client into one of three groups: no micro/small suppliers (nothing to file), micro/small suppliers but all paid within 45 days (no return; keep the working paper), and dues pending or paid after 45 days (file). Two clocks, not one. Form 1 sorts dues at 45 days. The MSMED Act itself makes payment due on the agreed date, which can be at most 45 days, or within 15 days of acceptance where there is no written agreement (s.15). Interest under s.16 and the income-tax rule that delays the buyer's deduction for late payments to micro and small suppliers (s.43B(h) under the 1961 Act; check the matching provision for FY 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025) follow the Act's clock. A clean Form 1 does not prove the client paid on time. Our 45-day payment rule guide explains the supplier side.

The form also asks for the outstanding at the start of the period, the amount cleared, the amount added and the outstanding over 45 days at the end of the period. Build those four totals from the same sheet so they tie up. The MCA instruction kit allows one row per unique PAN; read it on the MCA page before you upload, since a filed return cannot simply be resubmitted.

Collect list for each company client

  • Company name, CIN, financial year, whether it has any micro or small suppliers (Y/N)
  • Creditors ageing as on 30 Sep 2026, bill-wise
  • For each micro/small supplier: name, PAN, Udyam number and category, written payment terms (if any)
  • For each bill in Apr–Sep 2026: invoice date, date of acceptance or deemed acceptance, payment date, mode (TReDS or other), amount
  • Bills still unpaid on 30 Sep 2026 and their age in days
  • Reason for delay per supplier (disputes, missing documents, cash-flow, other)
  • Last MSME Form 1 filed (period and SRN), to carry forward the opening outstanding
  • Director who will sign, DSC validity, owner on your team, draft sent date, sign-off date, SRN

WhatsApp / email script pack

First check: "Hello. The half-yearly MSME return for April to September 2026 is due on 31 October. Please send your creditors list as on 30 September, and tell us which suppliers are micro or small enterprises (Udyam registered), by [date]."

Supplier status request (for the client to forward): "Dear supplier, for our MSME compliance, please share your Udyam registration certificate, or confirm that you are not registered, by [date]. Thank you."

Bill-wise data: "For each micro or small supplier, please send the bills for April to September 2026 in Excel: invoice date, date you accepted the goods or service, payment date and amount. We need it by [date] to file before 31 October."

Reason for delay: "These [n] suppliers were paid, or are still unpaid, more than 45 days after acceptance. The return asks for a reason for each. Please choose one per supplier: quality dispute, documents pending, cash-flow, other (please say)."

Sign-off: "Your MSME return for April–September 2026 is ready. Summary attached. Please reply 'OK to file' and keep the director's DSC ready by [date], so we can file before 31 October."
