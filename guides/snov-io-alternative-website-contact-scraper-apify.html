The pain this page is about

You already have the companies: a Google Maps export, a dealer list, a trade-fair list. What is missing is a way to reach each one. A full sales suite is one answer. Reading what each company's own website publishes is a smaller, cheaper one.

Who should pick Snov.io instead

  • You need a named decision-maker's email, not the company's shared inbox.
  • You want to verify a large list before sending, in bulk.
  • You want prospecting, sequences, LinkedIn steps, warm-up and a CRM in one tool for a team.
  • You never want to look at JSON or run a cloud job.

Frequently asked questions

Does Snov.io have a free plan?

Snov.io's pricing page (read 20 Oct 2026) describes a Trial that renews every 30 days with 50 credits, 100 recipients, 25 enrichment tokens and 1 warm-up slot. Export, bulk search, bulk verification, integrations and API are not available on the Trial.

How much is Snov.io Starter?

The pricing page shows Starter at $39 a month. Its FAQ says one credit on Starter costs 3.9¢ and that credits are cheaper on larger plans.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a named person's email like Snov.io's email search?

No, not by default. It only reads what each website publishes and returns shared role inboxes. Personal addresses are dropped unless you switch them on, which needs a lawful basis under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR.

Is the Unadhi contact scraper free?

The Actor charges no fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform resources your run uses; Apify's free plan includes $5 of monthly usage, per apify.com/pricing (read 20 Oct 2026).

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
