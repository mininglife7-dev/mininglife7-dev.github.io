Q2 TDS returns for CA firms: what to collect from clients before 31 Oct 2026

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer: before Q2 TDS filing (commonly cited deadline around 31 Oct for the Jul–Sep quarter for many deductors — always re-check income-tax / TRACES / CBDT notices), collect deductee PANs, challan details, salary vs non-salary split, and confirmation of Form 16A / salary Form 16 readiness. Keep one shared sheet so no one double-pings the same client.

Confirm the due date first

  • Q2 (Jul–Sep): many firms plan against a filing date around 31 October, but that is not a substitute for the portal. Open the income-tax e-filing site and TRACES, and note any CBDT extension or return-specific rule before you message clients.
  • Return type matters: salary (often Form 24Q) vs non-salary (often Form 26Q / 27Q) packs need different extracts. Do not send one generic “send TDS data” note to every client.
  • If an official notice changes the date, rewrite the due line on your sheet and tell clients once, naming the notice or portal screen you checked.

Message templates that stay polite

Day −10: "Hello — Q2 (Jul–Sep) TDS filing is on our calendar for [date]. Please send the deductee list with PANs and challan details by [Day −7]. Reply to this thread if anything is delayed."

Day −1: "Quick confirm: may we file the Q2 TDS statement with the figures we already have? Reply YES or send the correction today."

Do not quote late-fee amounts unless you have calculated them for that client from current rules.
