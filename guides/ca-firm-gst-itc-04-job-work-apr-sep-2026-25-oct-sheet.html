ITC-04 job work sheet for CA firms: which clients file April–September 2026 by 25 October, and which challans are past the one-year limit

Short answer (checked 21 Oct 2026): under rule 45(3) of the CGST Rules, a principal whose aggregate turnover in the preceding financial year exceeded ₹5 crore files Form GST ITC-04 half-yearly, by the 25th of the month after the period. For April–September 2026 that is Sunday 25 October 2026. Everyone else files once for the financial year (for FY 2026-27, by 25 April 2027). Sources: CBIC's text of rule 45 (as substituted by Notification 35/2021-CT, from 1 October 2021), section 143 of the CGST Act, and the GST portal's ITC-04 user manual. We found no extension notified as of 21 Oct 2026; the Commissioner can extend by notification, so check gst.gov.in before you act. This is a starting point, not tax advice.

Why it is easy to miss

ITC-04 carries no tax payment, so it does not show up in the GSTR-3B rhythm. It lands on a Sunday, three days after the quarterly GSTR-3B dates, and the client usually has the challan data while you have the portal login. Plan to file by Friday 23 October.

Why the flag matters: section 143(3) and (4) treat inputs not back (or supplied from the job worker) within one year, and capital goods within three years, as supplied by the principal on the day they were sent out; rule 45(4) says that supply goes in GSTR-1 with tax and interest. Moulds and dies, jigs and fixtures, and tools have no such limit. The Commissioner can extend the limits by up to one and two years on sufficient cause. Any challan showing "Past limit" is a conversation with the client before you file, not after.

Three client messages

1. Data request: "Your ITC-04 for April–September 2026 is due 25 October (a Sunday). Please send by Thursday the list of job-work challans sent out and goods received back in that period, with job worker GSTIN, quantity and any loss or waste."

2. Past-limit challan: "Challan [no.] dated [date] for [goods] has not come back within one year. Under the GST job work rules it is treated as a supply on the day it was sent, so an invoice with tax and interest is needed. Can you confirm whether it was returned or supplied from the job worker's premises?"

3. Annual filer: "Your turnover last year was within ₹5 crore, so ITC-04 is annual: FY 2026-27 is due 25 April 2027. Keeping the challan register monthly will make that a 20-minute job."
