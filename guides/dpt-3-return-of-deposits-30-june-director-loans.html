Form DPT-3: the 30 June return of deposits, director loans and why 'we have no deposits' is not enough

Last updated 21 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Every company other than a government company files Form DPT-3 by 30 June each year, with figures as on 31 March (Rule 16, Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014). Since 2019 it must also report money received that is not treated as a deposit, such as loans from directors, so a company that took a director loan usually has to file even if it has no 'deposits'. An auditor's certificate is needed where actual deposits are reported.

What goes into DPT-3

  • Deposits accepted and outstanding (with auditor's certificate)
  • Money received that is excluded from 'deposit' under Rule 2(1)(c): loans from directors (with their declaration that it is not borrowed money), loans from banks and companies, share application money, trade advances, and similar
  • Net worth, paid-up capital and free reserves figures

Who must file

CompanyFile DPT-3?
Private company with a director loan at 31 MarchYes (as exempt receipt)
Company with unsecured loans from other companiesYes
Company with only share capital and trade payablesUsually no filing needed; confirm with your CS
Government companyNo

Common mistakes

  • Skipping it because "no deposits". Exempt receipts like director loans are reportable.
  • No written declaration from the director that the loan is not out of borrowed funds; without it the money may be treated as a deposit.
  • Using figures other than 31 March.
  • Filing late. Rule 21 provides a fine on the company and officers in default, with a continuing amount per day.

Checklist

  • All loans and advances at 31 March listed
  • Director declarations on file
  • Auditor certificate if any deposits
  • DPT-3 filed on MCA21 by 30 June

Frequently asked questions

What is the due date for DPT-3?

30 June every year, with information as on 31 March (Rule 16, Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014).

Do I need to file DPT-3 for a director loan?

Usually yes. Loans from directors are exempt from being deposits but must be reported in DPT-3 as money received that is not a deposit.

Do private companies file DPT-3?

Yes, every company other than a government company files DPT-3 when it has deposits or reportable exempt receipts.

Is an auditor's certificate needed?

Yes where deposits are reported; it is not normally needed for exempt receipts alone.
