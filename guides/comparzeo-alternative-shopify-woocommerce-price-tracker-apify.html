Short answer: Comparzeo is a French competitor price monitoring service with daily price updates, price-drop email alerts and Excel or CSV exports, plus a paid “Proof” add-on that captures dated page evidence of competitor promotions and your own. Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €29 a month excluding VAT (100 tracked products, 1 user, 30-day history; €348 a year), Pro at €49 (500 products, 3 users, 90-day history, bulk import; €588 a year) and Business at €89 (2,000 products, 10 users, unlimited history, Shopify and PrestaShop APIs, automatic competitor search verified by AI; €1,068 a year). The Proof add-on is +€29 a month or €290 a year per block of 20 products, or a €49 one-off campaign pass per 20 products for a promotion of up to 60 days. VAT (20%) is added; the 14-day trial needs no card. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does a smaller job: give it competitor shop URLs and it returns their public product and price rows for your own sheet, plus a summary of skipped shops. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It keeps no history, sends no alerts and makes no evidence captures. If you want daily monitoring with alerts, or dated proof of promotions, Comparzeo is built for that. If you want an occasional catalogue-and-price export, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“We need dated screenshots showing what competitors advertised during the sales.” That is an evidence job. “I want this week's prices from four competitor shops in one sheet” is an export job.

Who should pick Comparzeo instead

  • You want daily monitoring with price-drop alerts and history kept for you.
  • You need dated captures of competitor promotions (Proof add-on) for a sales period.
  • You run PrestaShop or Shopify and want the API connection (Business).

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want competitors' whole public catalogues from Shopify or WooCommerce, not a tracked list.
  • You check prices occasionally and do not want a monthly plan.
  • You are happy to compare prices in your own spreadsheet.

How much does Comparzeo cost?

Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €29 a month, Pro at €49 and Business at €89, excluding VAT, with annual totals of €348, €588 and €1,068.

What is Comparzeo Proof?

A paid add-on that captures dated page evidence of competitor promotions and your own: +€29 a month or €290 a year per 20 products, or a €49 one-off campaign pass per 20 products for up to 60 days.

Does the Unadhi Actor keep price history?

No. Each run returns the public product and price rows at that moment; keep the outputs if you want history.
