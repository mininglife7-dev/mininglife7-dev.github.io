November 2026 due-date chase list for CA firms: every client deadline from 7 to 30 November on one sheet

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 20 Oct 2026): November 2026 has twelve client deadlines on ten dates that a typical CA firm has to chase, and four of those dates fall on a Saturday or Sunday. The ones you can rely on: TDS/TCS for October deposited by Sat 7 Nov (rule 218, Income-tax Rules, 2026), GSTR-1 by Wed 11 Nov and GSTR-3B by Fri 20 Nov for monthly filers (GST portal), Form 131 certificates for Q2 by Sun 15 Nov (Form 131 FAQs), the AY 2026-27 return for ordinary audit cases by Sat 21 Nov (CBDT Circular 07/2026), and on Mon 30 Nov both the return for transfer-pricing cases and the last date for FY 2025-26 GST input tax credit, credit notes and GSTR-1 corrections (CGST Act, s.16(4)). This is a starting point, not tax advice. Confirm each date on the portal before you file.

About the weekend dates: 7, 15, 21 and 29 November fall on a Saturday or Sunday. Do not plan on a date moving because of the weekend. Aim for the working day before, as in the last column, and treat any later relief as a bonus.

The 30 November GST cut-off, in one line for clients: any FY 2025-26 purchase invoice whose credit was missed, any FY 2025-26 credit note not yet reported, and any FY 2025-26 GSTR-1 mistake has to be dealt with by then, or before the annual return if the client files GSTR-9 earlier. For monthly filers the October GSTR-3B is the return that carries it. QRMP clients file their next GSTR-3B only after the October–December quarter, in January 2027, so check the official source and the client's own return position before you advise them how to claim a missed credit. Do not let a client file GSTR-9 early while credit is still unclaimed.

The easy mistake this month: lumping every income-tax return into "21 November". Transfer-pricing clients are on 30 November and non-audit clients' original due date for AY 2026-27 has already passed (for their belated or revised return, see revised ITR last date and check the official source). Keep the two return dates in separate columns.

Collect list

  • TDS: October payment list with deductee PAN, section code and amount; challan details once paid
  • Q2 TDS: TRACES processing status for Form 140/144, so Form 131 can be generated (it cannot be issued offline)
  • GST monthly: October sales register, credit and debit notes, e-invoice and e-way bill exceptions
  • FY 2025-26 credit check: purchase register April 2025–March 2026 in Excel, vendor ledgers for unmatched invoices, any FY 2025-26 credit notes still to be issued or reported
  • QRMP: B2B invoices for October (for IFF) and the chosen PMT-06 method (fixed sum or self-assessment)
  • Employers: October wage sheet, joiners and leavers, UAN and IP numbers
  • Audit-case and transfer-pricing ITR: audit report or Form 3CEB acknowledgement, tax computation, "OK to file" in writing, the person who will e-verify
  • Companies: AGM date, signed financial statements, shareholder and director changes, valid DSCs for MGT-7

WhatsApp / email script pack

Your November dates: "Hello. Your compliance dates for November: [list only theirs]. Deepavali week is in between, so please send [items] by [date before 5 Nov]. We will confirm each filing as it is done."

TDS: "Tax deducted in October has to be paid by 7 November, a Saturday. Please send the October payment list with PANs by [date], and we will prepare the challan for payment by Friday 6 November."

GST credit cut-off: "30 November is the last date to claim any input credit from 2025-26 that was missed. Please send your purchase register for April 2025 to March 2026 in Excel by [date], so we can check it before your October GSTR-3B."
