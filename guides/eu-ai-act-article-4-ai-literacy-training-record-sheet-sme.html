EU AI Act Article 4 AI literacy record sheet for small businesses: who uses which AI tool, what they were shown, and when

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the EURO AI Register mentioned below is an Unadhi product with a free edition. This guide is for information only, not legal advice.

What you get: a finished AI literacy record sheet: 10 columns, one row per person and AI tool (who, which tool for what, your role, what they already know, the risks, who the output affects, what they were shown, how, when and by whom, and the next refresh), each tied to Article 4 or the Commission's AI literacy Q&A, plus a made-up example row and an eight-line gap check. Made for small shops, agencies, clinics, trades and service businesses whose staff use chat assistants, writing tools, transcription or email helpers.

The short answer

Article 4 of the AI Act has applied since 2 February 2025. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (the Digital Omnibus on AI), Article 4(1) says providers and deployers of AI systems shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy of their staff and other persons dealing with the operation and use of AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their technical knowledge, experience, education and training, the context of use, and the persons on whom the systems are used. It does not require you to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual. Article 3(56) defines AI literacy as the skills, knowledge and understanding to make an informed deployment of AI systems and to gain awareness about the opportunities and risks of AI and possible harm it can cause.

The Commission's Q&A says no certificate is needed and organisations can keep an internal record of trainings and/or other guiding initiatives. National market surveillance authorities supervise and enforce Article 4 from 2 August 2026. A short record, one row per person and tool, is the practical way to show what you did.

Do this week

  • List every AI tool in use, then add one row per person who uses it, including contractors working on your behalf
  • Fill columns D to F before deciding what to show people: the Q&A ties the content to knowledge, risk and context
  • Run one short briefing per role and fill G to I the same day
  • Put the next refresh dates from column J in the diary
  • Hand your staff AI use policy out in the same briefing and name it in column H
