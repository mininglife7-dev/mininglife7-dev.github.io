Short answer: Altosight is a competitor price monitoring and repricing service for online retailers and brands, with a published address in Piraeus, Greece. Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) prices plans by URL allowance, not by product: for online retailers, Marketplace is €43 a month for up to 500 URLs on one marketplace, Starter €53 for 300 URLs (competitor websites plus one marketplace), Growth €79 for 1,000 URLs, Business €139 for 3,000 URLs, Pro €209 for 6,000 URLs and Scale €299 for 12,000 URLs (up to €629 for 50,000); Enterprise is custom. Prices exclude VAT, annual billing is 12 months for the price of 10, and the 14-day free trial needs no card. Every plan includes one daily price check per URL, a repricer with min/max guards, alerts, daily Excel reports and API access; WooCommerce auto-repricing is +15% below Business and included from Business. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify does a smaller job: give it competitor shop URLs and it returns their public product and price rows, plus a summary of skipped shops, for your own sheet. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It does no matching, alerts or repricing. If you want competitor prices matched to your products every day and pushed into repricing, Altosight is built for that. If you want an occasional catalogue-and-price export from Shopify or WooCommerce shops, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I want to see every product and price that three competitor shops list before I set our winter prices.” That is a one-off export job. “I want 2,000 competitor product pages checked every morning and my WooCommerce prices adjusted inside my limits” is a monitoring-and-repricing job.

Who should pick Altosight instead

  • You want competitor prices matched to your own products and checked every day without running anything yourself.
  • You sell on marketplaces (Amazon, Google Shopping, idealo, eMAG and others) and need every seller's price on a listing.
  • You want repricing with min/max guards, daily Excel reports and onboarding help.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You need a one-off or occasional look at competitor catalogues, not a monthly plan.
  • Your competitors run Shopify or WooCommerce and you want their whole public catalogue, not a matched URL list.
  • You are happy to compare prices in your own spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Altosight cost?

Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) lists retailer plans from €43 a month (Marketplace, 500 URLs on one marketplace); Starter €53 for 300 URLs; Growth €79 for 1,000; Business €139 for 3,000; Pro €209 for 6,000; Scale €299 for 12,000 URLs, excluding VAT. Annual billing is 12 months for the price of 10.

What counts as a URL at Altosight?

Its FAQ says each competitor product page you monitor counts as one URL, and a marketplace listing counts as one URL even when every seller's price is read from it.

Does the Unadhi Actor reprice my shop?

No. It only reads public product and price data from the Shopify and WooCommerce shops you list and never connects to your own shop.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 10 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
