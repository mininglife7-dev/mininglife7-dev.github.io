Short answer: They do different jobs. Zync CA is a client portal for Indian CA practices: each client gets a secure link to upload documents, filings move across a Pending → Received → In Progress → Filed board, and reminders go out automatically by WhatsApp and email at 7, 3 and 1 day before each deadline. Its pricing section (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows one plan with every feature at ₹251 per seat per month, or ₹3,012 per seat per year, with unlimited clients, a compliance calendar, 7-year document storage and custom branding on the client portal, free to start with no card. Our White-Label Client Compliance Calendar is not software: it is four quarterly packs (DOCX, PDF, WhatsApp image, .ics, .xlsx) with your firm's name, for unlimited clients, at ₹999 a year. If your pain is chasing each client's documents, Zync CA is the better pick. If you want every client to see the national dates under your brand, start with ours.

The pain this page is about

“Clients send Form 16 and bank statements two days before the deadline, by WhatsApp, as blurry photos.” A portal with automatic reminders fixes the chasing for each filing. A branded quarterly calendar works earlier: every client sees the month's dates in advance, from your firm, before you need to chase anyone.

Who should pick Zync CA instead

  • Your biggest time sink is chasing documents client by client, and you want reminders tied to each filing.
  • You want a status board for every ITR, GST and TDS filing, with staff assigned to clients.
  • You want documents stored for years in one place instead of WhatsApp chats.

Who the white-label calendar fits better

  • You do not want new software or seats; you want a branded quarterly calendar in every client's hands.
  • You want a client-facing piece with your firm's name that clients keep and forward.
  • You use a portal already and only miss the calendar; many firms could use both.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Zync CA cost?

Its pricing section (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows one plan at ₹251 per seat per month, or ₹3,012 per seat per year, with every feature and unlimited clients. It is free to start with no card.

When does Zync CA send reminders?

Its FAQ says reminders go to the client automatically at 7 days, 3 days and 1 day before the deadline you set, by WhatsApp and email.

Do clients need a Zync CA account?

No, per its FAQ: the client receives a WhatsApp or email link and uploads from there.

Is the ₹999 calendar a subscription?

It is a yearly licence sold as a Gumroad membership, so it renews yearly unless you cancel. Year one covers four quarterly packs, starting with your first pack.

Can I use both?

Yes. A portal can chase each client's documents while a branded quarterly calendar keeps every client aware of the national dates.
