Get the WhatsApp number and social links a business publishes on its own website

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the Contact Details Scraper linked below is an Unadhi product on the Apify Store. This guide is free, the manual method needs no tool at all, and the Apify method works with any contact scraper that reads social links.

Short answer: most small businesses link their WhatsApp and social profiles from their own site: a floating chat button, the header or footer icons, or the contact page. Open the site, hover over those icons, and copy the link. A WhatsApp click-to-chat link such as wa.me/919800000000 already contains the full international number. For tens or hundreds of websites, paste the homepages into a contact scraper on Apify with personal profiles off and robots.txt respected, and export the WhatsApp and social columns with the page each link was found on.

Why start from the business's own website

  • It is the real account. A profile the business links from its own domain is the one it wants you to use. A name search on Instagram or Facebook can return look-alike, fan or abandoned pages.
  • It is the channel they chose. Many shops, clinics, coaches and service businesses (especially in India, the Gulf, Latin America and Africa) answer WhatsApp faster than email. If they put a chat button on the site, that is an invitation to message them.
  • It avoids scraping social networks. Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn generally forbid automated collection in their terms. Reading a business's own public pages politely, and following its robots.txt, is a different and much safer job.

Step 1: check the places businesses link their profiles

  • Floating chat button, usually a green WhatsApp icon in a bottom corner. Hover or long-press to see the wa.me or api.whatsapp.com link behind it.
  • Header and footer icons: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X, TikTok and so on.
  • Contact page (/contact, /contact-us, /kontakt): often lists the WhatsApp number in text and links the same number.
  • About and team pages: LinkedIn company pages and YouTube channels often live here.
  • Page source: press Ctrl+U and search for wa.me, instagram.com or sameAs. Many sites list all their profiles in structured data (a JSON-LD sameAs list) even when the icons are hidden on mobile.

Step 2: read the WhatsApp number from the link

Link format you seeWhat it means
https://wa.me/919800000000Click-to-chat. The digits after wa.me/ are the full international number without the plus: +91 98000 00000.
https://api.whatsapp.com/send?phone=971500000000Older click-to-chat format. The number follows phone=: +971 50 000 0000.
https://wa.link/abc123A short link from a third-party link maker. The number appears only after it redirects.
https://chat.whatsapp.com/AbCdEf…A group invite, not a business chat. Do not treat it as a contact number.
https://whatsapp.com/channel/…A WhatsApp Channel (one-way broadcast you can follow), not a chat.

Keep the "found on" column: it is your proof the business published the link, and it lets you re-check later. Leave out personal profiles such as a founder's linkedin.com/in/… page. They identify a person, so they need a separate, lawful reason.

Step 5: check the result and use it the way it was offered

  • Export as CSV or Excel and keep the "found on" page for every link.
  • Open a sample of rows: make sure the Instagram or LinkedIn link really belongs to that business (some sites link their web designer in the footer).
  • Message one business at a time, about something relevant to what they sell. A click-to-chat button is not consent to be added to a broadcast list, a group or a bulk sender. WhatsApp's business rules require opt-in, and bulk unsolicited messages get numbers banned.

Common mistakes

  • Treating a chat.whatsapp.com group invite as the business's number.
  • Copying the web agency's links from a "Website by…" footer credit.
  • Scraping Instagram or Facebook directly for phone numbers. Their terms forbid it; start from the business's own site.
  • Keeping a list for months. Numbers and handles change; re-check the "found on" page before you write.
