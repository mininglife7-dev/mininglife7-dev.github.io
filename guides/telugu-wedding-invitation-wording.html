Telugu wedding invitation wording (పెళ్లి శుభలేఖ), with English, and AI prompts that help

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · Telugu speakers everywhere

Short answer. A traditional Telugu wedding card (శుభలేఖ) usually has: a blessing at the top, the families inviting you, the bride's and groom's names with honorifics, the ముహూర్తం (auspicious time), the date and venue, and a closing from the hosts. Families abroad often print Telugu on one side and English on the other, so every guest can read it.

The parts of a Telugu invitation

TeluguMeaning / use
శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తుCommon opening blessing: "may there be prosperity, auspiciousness and no obstacles"
శుభలేఖ"Auspicious letter": the invitation itself
వివాహ మహోత్సవంWedding celebration
చి॥ (చిరంజీవి)Honorific before the groom's name
చి॥ల॥సౌ॥Honorific before the bride's name (Chiranjeevi Lakshmi Sowbhagyavathi)
ముహూర్తంThe auspicious time of the ceremony
వేదికVenue
సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా"With your whole family": the usual way to invite everyone

A simple bilingual template

శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తు
మా ఇంట జరుగు వివాహ మహోత్సవమునకు మీరు సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా విచ్చేసి, వధూవరులను ఆశీర్వదించవలసిందిగా కోరుచున్నాము.
చి॥ [వరుని పేరు]  తో  చి॥ల॥సౌ॥ [వధువు పేరు]
ముహూర్తం: [తేదీ, సమయం]
వేదిక: [స్థలం]

English side: With the blessings of our elders, we warmly invite you and your family to the wedding of [groom's name] and [bride's name] on [date] at [time], at [venue]. Your presence and blessings will mean a great deal to us. — [hosts' names]

Tips for families abroad

  • Give the time in the guests' time zone if many will join a livestream, and say which zone you mean.
  • Add a map link and a livestream link on the English side or as a separate WhatsApp message.
  • Ask an elder to check family names, gotram and the muhurtham wording; families differ.
  • Use a Telugu font that renders on phones (for example Noto Sans Telugu) and test the file on an Android and an iPhone before sharing.

Common mistakes

  • Broken Telugu letters when a design app doesn't support the script. Export to PDF/image and check every word.
  • Wrong honorifics (the bride's and groom's are different).
  • Missing RSVP details for receptions abroad, where venues need head counts.

AI tools can make mistakes in Telugu spelling, so have a fluent reader check the final text.

తెలుగులో సంక్షిప్తంగా

శుభలేఖలో ఆశీర్వచనం, ఆహ్వానించే కుటుంబాలు, వధూవరుల పేర్లు, ముహూర్తం, తేదీ, వేదిక ఉండాలి. విదేశాల్లో ఉన్న బంధువుల కోసం ఒక వైపు తెలుగు, మరో వైపు ఇంగ్లీష్ ఇవ్వడం మంచిది. పంపే ముందు పెద్దలతో ఒకసారి సరిచూసుకోండి.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Telugu wedding invitation called?

Shubhalekha (శుభలేఖ), meaning an auspicious letter.

What honorific is used before the bride's name?

Commonly చి॥ల॥సౌ॥ (Chiranjeevi Lakshmi Sowbhagyavathi); the groom's name usually has చి॥ (Chiranjeevi).

How do I write a Telugu invitation for relatives abroad?

Print Telugu on one side and English on the other, give times with the time zone, and add livestream and map links.
