The pain this page is about

Many lead lists are just company websites — from Google Maps, a directory or a trade fair. Social automation tools are built around profiles and accounts. The question is which kind of tool your list actually needs.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PhantomBuster cost?

Its pricing page (read 20 Oct 2026) shows Start at $56 a month ($672 billed annually), Grow at $128 a month ($1,536) and Scale at $352 a month ($4,224), with a free Trial. A monthly billing option is shown with a toggle.

Does PhantomBuster have a free plan?

It has a free Trial with 5 automation slots, 2 hours of execution time, 50 email credits and 1,000 AI credits; exports are limited to 10 rows.

Does the Unadhi Actor scrape LinkedIn?

No. It only visits the public company websites you list. It does not log in anywhere and does not read social profiles; it returns links to a company's social pages when the website publishes them.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
