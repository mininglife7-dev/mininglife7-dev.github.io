Short answer: after Omnibus I, only companies and groups with more than EUR 450 million net turnover and more than 1,000 employees on average must publish sustainability reports (new Article 19a(1) and 29a(1) of Directive 2013/34/EU; Article 5(2) of Directive 2022/2464 for financial years from 1 January 2027). A company in their value chain that did not exceed an average of 1,000 employees in the preceding financial year is a "protected undertaking": it has a statutory right to decline any information beyond the voluntary standard when the request is for that sustainability reporting (Article 19a(3)). The voluntary standard and its cap list are in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560; the cap applies from the reporting company's financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027 (its Article 4). Countries must put the Directive into national law by 19 March 2027 (Directive 2026/470, Article 5).

What to tell a client in one minute

  • You probably do not have to report yourself. Mandatory reporting is now only for companies over EUR 450 million turnover and 1,000 employees.
  • Big customers may still ask you questions for their own report. If you had 1,000 employees or fewer on average last year, they can only require the items on the cap list; you may say no to the rest (Article 19a(3)).
  • They must tell you which questions go beyond the cap and that you have a right to decline (Article 19a(3), point (c)).
  • A contract clause that forces you to give more for their CSRD report is not binding, but the rest of the contract is (Article 19a(3), point (b)).
  • The cap does not cover everything. Questions for due diligence, a bank loan, or the customer's own risk management are outside it.

Why AI tools matter here

Large customers now send ESG questionnaires through supplier portals, and many SMEs answer them with AI writing tools or let a tool pre-fill every field. Check the cap first: anything outside the 23 datapoints is optional for a protected client in a CSRD request, and an AI tool should not volunteer it by default. Checklist before 1 January 2027

  • For each client, write down average employees for the last financial year and keep a signed size self-declaration ready.
  • Collect the cap datapoints the client can answer (energy, GHG estimate, water, waste, staff numbers, accidents, minimum wage, collective bargaining, training hours, and the comprehensive items if over 10 employees).
  • Log every questionnaire question in the sheet with its purpose.
  • Answer cap items; decline or share the rest by choice, and keep the note.
  • Check supplier contracts for ESG clauses that demand more than the cap.
  • Check what AI tools the client uses to fill questionnaires and what they send.

Limits and our reading

  • Not read, flagged: national laws transposing Directive 2026/470 (due 19 March 2027); the full text of Annex I of Delegated Regulation 2026/1560 beyond paragraph 27 and the paragraphs named in Annex II; EFRAG guidance; the revised ESRS delegated act; Recommendation 2025/1710 (no longer producing legal effects, recital 5).
  • Our reading: the sheet uses one employee number for both the 1,000 test and the 10-employee split in Annex II (Annex II does not say how to count the 10); a question is "inside the cap" only if it matches an Annex II datapoint (recital 4 says only essential datapoints are covered); purpose CSRD covers both individual (Art 19a) and group (Art 29a) reports; the cap start date is read against the requester's financial year.

Questions

Can an SME refuse a large customer's ESG questionnaire after Omnibus I?

Partly. If it did not exceed an average of 1,000 employees in the preceding financial year, it may decline any information beyond the voluntary standard when the request is for the customer's sustainability reporting (Article 19a(3) of Directive 2013/34/EU as amended by Directive (EU) 2026/470).

What information is inside the value chain cap?

The 23 datapoints in Annex II of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560, such as total energy use in MWh, estimated GHG emissions, water withdrawal, waste, employee numbers by contract type and gender, accidents, minimum wage, collective bargaining coverage and training hours. Companies with 10 employees or fewer have a shorter list.

When does the value chain cap apply?

Article 3 of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1560 applies from financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2027. Member States must transpose Directive 2026/470 by 19 March 2027.

Is a contract clause demanding full ESG data binding?

A clause requiring a protected undertaking to give more than the voluntary standard for CSRD reporting is not binding; the rest of the contract remains binding (Article 19a(3), point (b)).

Does the cap cover bank or due diligence questionnaires?

No. It covers only requests for sustainability reporting under the Accounting Directive. Banks are only encouraged to keep to the voluntary standard, and due diligence requests fall under the CSDDD, where from 26 July 2029 partners with fewer than 5,000 employees should only be asked for information that cannot reasonably be obtained otherwise.

Who must still publish a CSRD report?

Companies and groups exceeding both EUR 450 million net turnover and an average of 1,000 employees during the financial year, for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2027.
