Short answer: priceq is a competitor price monitoring app for Shopify shops, run by Outflanq (Netherlands) on servers in Germany. Its Shopify page (checked 9 Nov 2026) says it connects to your shop, matches your variants to competitor listings by barcode (EAN), checks them daily and sends alerts; from the Business plan it proposes prices and can push a price you have signed off back to Shopify inside your guardrails. Start is €49 a month for 500 products checked once a day, Business €149 for 2,000 products with two checks a day and price proposals, Professional €299 for 5,000 products with two checks a day and personal support; every plan tracks up to five competitor shops, Enterprise is on request, prices exclude VAT and every plan starts with a free trial. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify is simpler: you give it competitor shop URLs and it returns their public product and price rows plus a summary of skipped shops, for your own sheet. No Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. It does not connect to your shop, match products or reprice. If you run Shopify and want matched alerts and repricing, priceq is built for that. If you want a cheap, occasional look at competitor catalogues (Shopify or WooCommerce), try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Before Black Friday I want to see what four competitor shops charge for their whole range, once.” That is a catalogue-and-price export job. “I want my 1,500 variants matched to competitors every day and prices pushed back to Shopify” is a repricing-app job.

Who should pick priceq instead

  • You run a Shopify shop and want competitor prices matched to your own variants by EAN, every day.
  • You want price proposals and signed-off push-back to Shopify with floors and daily-move limits.
  • You want alerts when a competitor undercuts you, and EU (German) hosting.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want a one-off or occasional competitor price sheet without a monthly app plan.
  • Your competitors run WooCommerce as well as Shopify.
  • You do not want to give any app write access to your shop.

Frequently asked questions

How much does priceq cost?

Its Shopify page (checked 9 Nov 2026) lists Start at €49 a month for 500 products, Business at €149 for 2,000 products and Professional at €299 for 5,000 products, each with up to five competitor shops, excluding VAT, with a free trial. Enterprise is on request.

Does priceq change my prices automatically?

Its page says every change is a proposal until a person or a rule you switched on accepts it; automatic pushing is off by default and can be switched on per rule from the Business plan.

Does the Unadhi Actor reprice my shop?

No. It only reads public product and price data from the shops you list and never connects to your own shop.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 9 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
