Telugu Upanayanam invitation wording — with English meanings

Last updated 14 Oct 2026 · Telugu speakers, any country

Short answer. An Upanayanam invitation usually names the parents/guardians, the vatu (the boy receiving the sacred thread), the muhurtham (date and time), the venue, and a warm request to attend with family. Below are common Telugu lines with English meanings you can paste into WhatsApp or a printed card. Proofread names, date and year — relatives notice a wrong year first. Type Telugu with Gboard transliteration or the iPhone Telugu keyboard (see our free typing guide).

Core lines (Telugu → English)

Telugu (common wording)English meaning
శ్రీ సుబ్రహ్మణ్యేశ్వర స్వామి వారి సన్నిధిలోIn the presence / blessings of Lord Subrahmanya (or replace with your family deity)
మా కుమారుని ఉపనయన మహోత్సవముOur son's Upanayanam (sacred-thread) ceremony
వటువు: [పేరు]Vatu (the initiate): [name]
ముహూర్తము: [తేదీ, సమయం]Muhurtham (auspicious time): [date, time]
వేదిక: [ఇంటి / కళ్యాణ మండపం చిరునామా]Venue: [home / kalyana mandapam address]
మీరు సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా ఆగమించి ఆశీర్వదించవలసినదిగా కోరుచున్నాముPlease come with your family and bless us
— [తల్లిదండ్రుల పేర్లు] మరియు కుటుంబ సభ్యులు— [parents' names] and family

Optional respectful openings

  • ఆహ్వానము — Invitation
  • సాదర ఆహ్వానము — Warm / respectful invitation
  • నమస్కారము — Greetings (namaskaram)

What to double-check before you send

  • Child's name spelling in Telugu and English
  • Muhurtham date, day and year (no “year mistake”)
  • Venue pin-code and landmark for out-of-town guests
  • RSVP phone number that someone answers
  • Whether elders want a gotra / family-deity line included

Typing Telugu on a phone

You do not need a paid font app. Add Telugu in Gboard (Android) or Settings → Keyboard (iPhone), then type in English letters for transliteration. Step-by-step: Telugu typing on mobile.

Wording varies by family tradition and region (Coastal Andhra, Rayalaseema, Telangana). Treat these as starting lines; elders may prefer a different deity line or seating note.

Frequently asked questions

What does Upanayanam mean on an invitation?

It is the sacred-thread ceremony for the vatu (the boy receiving the upanayana). The invitation asks guests to attend and bless the family.

What must every Upanayanam invite include?

At minimum: who is inviting, the vatu's name, muhurtham (date and time), venue, and a request to attend. Many families also name the family deity.

How do I type these lines on WhatsApp?

Add a Telugu keyboard (Gboard transliteration or iPhone Telugu) and paste or type the lines. Proofread names and the year before sending.

Can I mix Telugu and English on one card?

Yes. Many families put Telugu on one side or block and English meanings or an English block for relatives abroad.
