White-label client compliance calendar vs agency SaaS for CA firms (2026)

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year). Competitor prices below were read from public pricing pages on 13 Oct 2026.

Short answer: if your pain is clients asking “when is GSTR-3B?” and staff retyping the same dates into WhatsApp, buy (or generate) a branded client pack first. If your pain is partners not seeing which of 200 clients is overdue inside the firm, buy an agency SaaS tracker. They solve different jobs. A white-label pack at ₹999/year is about 5% of a typical agency SaaS starter (example: ComplyCal Agency Starter ₹19,999/year on their public pricing page).

What agency SaaS usually includes (function only)

Public pages for ComplyCal and similar India CA tools commonly advertise: entity-based due-date generation, multi-client agency dashboards, staff assignment, email/Slack reminders, and sometimes Google/Outlook sync. White-label client branding is often waitlisted or enterprise-only. Sources: complycal.com, pricing. This is a function summary in our own words — not a review score.

What a white-label pack must get right

  • Firm name and logo on every client-facing file (not a waitlist).
  • Each due-date row tied to an official source you can re-check (GST portal / Income Tax / MCA notices).
  • Formats clients actually open: PDF, DOCX, .ics, and a WhatsApp-sized image.
  • A refund window stated in plain language (ours: 14 days from first pack).
  • Honest limits: packs are not a live staff tracker and are not tax advice.

Free gift: WhatsApp chase templates (copy-paste)

Use these before the next monthly GSTR-3B due date (commonly the 20th for monthly filers — always re-check gst.gov.in). Replace brackets. Do not invent late-fee amounts.

Day −5: Hello — GSTR-3B for [month] is due on [date] for monthly filers. Please send sales + purchase summaries (or your Tally/Zoho export) by [Day −3]. Reply here if anything is delayed. — [Firm name]
Day −1: Quick confirm: may we file GSTR-3B for [month] with the figures we already have? Reply YES or send the correction today. — [Firm name]
Due-day internal: Filing only clients with written OK-to-file. Unconfirmed clients parked with reason logged. No invented consent.
