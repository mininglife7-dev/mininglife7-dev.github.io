Short answer: Enrow sells an email finder, phone finder and email verifier on one shared credit balance. Its pricing page (checked 9 Nov 2026, dollar view) shows Start at $17 a month for 1,000 credits with up to 2 team members, Pro at $87 a month for 10,000 credits with up to 10 team members and credit roll-over, and Scale at $397 a month with unlimited team members and CRM integration (its volume table lists 50,000 credits). Every plan starts with 50 free credits. A found email costs 1 credit, a found phone number 40 credits and a verification 0.25 credits; the page says there is no charge when there is no result. Pay-as-you-go credit packs that never expire are also offered. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a different job: it visits each website in your list and returns the inboxes, phones and WhatsApp links that business publishes, with the source page. No Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage (Apify Free includes $5 of usage a month). If you know the person and company and need their email or phone, Enrow is built for that and is cheap to start. If your list is business websites, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“I know the founder's name and company domain; I need the email.” That is a finder job. “I have 200 shop and agency websites and need the contact inbox, phone and WhatsApp each one shows” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick Enrow instead

  • You need a named person's email or phone and already know their name and company.
  • You want a low entry price ($17 a month) and pay-only-for-results credits, or credit packs that do not expire.
  • You want email verification and an API or MCP server on the same balance.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your input is a list of websites, not a list of people.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp as the business printed them, with the source page; phone finds are 40 credits each on Enrow.
  • You want to test a few dozen sites without starting a subscription.
