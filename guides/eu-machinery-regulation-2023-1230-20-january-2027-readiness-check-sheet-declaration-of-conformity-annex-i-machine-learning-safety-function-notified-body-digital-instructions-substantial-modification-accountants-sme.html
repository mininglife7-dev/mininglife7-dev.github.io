Short answer: the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 January 2027 (71 days from 10 Nov 2026) and the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC is repealed from the same day (Articles 51(2) and 54, dates as corrected in OJ L 169 of 4.7.2023; the original text said 14 January 2027). Every machine placed on the EU market or put into service from that day needs technical documentation, the right conformity procedure, an EU declaration of conformity under the Regulation, the CE marking, instructions and contact details as the Regulation sets them out. Units placed on the market under the Directive before 20 January 2027 can still be sold afterwards (Article 52(1)). A client that makes a substantial modification to a machine, or sells one under its own name, becomes its manufacturer (Articles 17 and 18).

What to tell a client in one minute

  • The date is per unit. "Placing on the market" is the first supply of each product. Stock placed on the market before 20 January 2027 under the old Directive can stay on sale; new units from that day need the new paperwork.
  • New declaration. The EU declaration of conformity must name Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 and follow Annex V Part A. It may come as a web address or machine-readable code in the instructions (Article 10(8)).
  • Digital instructions are allowed, with conditions: say on the machine how to reach them, make them printable and downloadable, keep them online for at least 10 years, and send paper free within one month if the buyer asks at purchase. If consumers could use the machine, the essential safety information must still be on paper (Article 10(7)).
  • Machine learning in a safety function means a notified body. Safety components, and embedded systems, with fully or partially self-evolving behaviour using machine-learning approaches that ensure safety functions are in Annex I Part A, so internal control is not enough (Article 25(2)).
  • Retrofits count. Adding guards that need changes to the safety control system, or extra measures for stability or strength, after a new hazard or higher risk, is a substantial modification (Article 3(16)); the modifier takes on the manufacturer's duties (Article 18).
  • Keep records 10 years. Who supplied you and whom you supplied (Article 19); manufacturers keep the technical documentation and declaration 10 years (Article 10(3)).

Why AI matters here

The Regulation is the first EU product law with its own rules for self-evolving (machine-learning) control systems: they must stay within their defined task and movement space, record data on safety-related decisions and keep it one year, and always be correctable (Annex III point 1.2.1). Safety software must be identified and protected against corruption, with evidence of interventions and a five-year log of safety software versions (Annex III points 1.1.9 and 1.2.1(f)). Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI) moved machinery to Section B of Annex I of the AI Act and tells the Commission to add AI requirements to Annex III of the Machinery Regulation by delegated acts that apply by 2 August 2028. What we tested

Checklist before 20 January 2027

  • List each client machine, batch and retrofit planned for after 20 January 2027, and the units already placed before it (keep the delivery notes or invoices that show the date).
  • Find each product's Annex I code; anything with a machine-learning safety function needs a notified body.
  • Book notified body slots early for Part A products and Part B products not built fully to the harmonised standards.
  • Re-issue declarations of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 using Annex V Part A.
  • Decide paper or digital instructions; if digital, set up the access marking, the 10-year hosting and the paper-on-request route.
  • Check name, postal address and digital contact, model, year of construction and serial number on each product.
  • Importers and distributors: ask suppliers now for the new declarations and check CE marking and instructions on the first deliveries after 20 January 2027.
  • Identify safety software and switch on the intervention and version logs; for self-evolving systems, set up the one-year safety decision records.

Questions

When does the EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 apply?

From 20 January 2027. The Official Journal corrigendum of 4 July 2023 changed the dates in Articles 51, 52 and 54 from 14 January 2027 to 20 January 2027; the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC is repealed from the same day.

Can we still sell machines made under the old Machinery Directive after 20 January 2027?

Yes, if they were placed on the market in conformity with Directive 2006/42/EC before 20 January 2027: Member States shall not impede making them available (Article 52(1)). Units placed on the market from 20 January 2027 need the Regulation.

Does a machine with an AI or machine-learning safety function need a notified body?

Yes. Safety components, and embedded systems, with fully or partially self-evolving behaviour using machine-learning approaches ensuring safety functions are in Annex I Part A, so the manufacturer must use module B + C, H or G (Article 25(2)).

Are paper instructions still required?

No, instructions may be digital, but the machine must show how to reach them, they must be printable and downloadable and stay online for at least 10 years, and paper must be supplied free within one month if the buyer asks at purchase. Where non-professional users are foreseeable, the essential safety information must be on paper (Article 10(7)).

When does a retrofit make our client the manufacturer?

When it is a substantial modification: a change not foreseen by the manufacturer that affects safety by creating a new hazard or increasing a risk and requires guards or protective devices that need changes to the safety control system, or extra measures for stability or strength (Articles 3(16) and 18). Non-professional users modifying their own machine are excepted.

What changed for AI in machinery in 2026?

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 moved the Machinery Regulation to Section B of Annex I of the AI Act and asks the Commission to add AI requirements to Annex III of the Machinery Regulation by delegated acts that apply by 2 August 2028.
