Short answer: LeadMagic is a pay-per-result enrichment and finder API. Its pricing page (checked 3 Nov 2026) lists Essential at $99 a month (about $82.50/mo on yearly, $990 upfront) with 5,000 credits a month; Growth $249 a month ($2,490 yearly) with 20,000 credits; Professional $499 a month ($4,990 yearly) with 50,000 credits; Ultimate $849 a month ($8,490 yearly) with 100,000 credits. Failed lookups are free. Its rate card on that page says a found work email costs 1 credit and a mobile 5 credits (email validation 0.25 credits). Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does not search an enrichment API: it reads each website you list and returns published role emails, phones and WhatsApp links with the source page. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you need waterfall enrichment, Clay/API waterfalls and high-volume email/mobile finds, LeadMagic is built for that. If you have company websites and want what they publish, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“Our Clay table needs work emails and mobiles for rows that already have a person or company.” That is an enrichment-API job. “Our CSV is only domains from a chamber directory” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick LeadMagic instead

  • You need an enrichment API (email, mobile, company) with pay-per-result credits and free failed lookups.
  • You run high volume in Clay, Zapier, n8n or your own stack and want plan tiers from $99 to $849 a month.
  • You already know people or companies and need their contact fields filled in.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your input is a list of websites, not a person database.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp as the business printed them, with the source URL.
  • You want to avoid a monthly credit subscription while you test a few dozen sites.
