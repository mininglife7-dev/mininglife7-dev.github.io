Frequently asked questions

Does Skrapp.io have a free plan?

Yes. Its pricing page (checked 21 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan at $0 with 50 credits, the Email Verifier and the LinkedIn Basic, Sales Navigator and LinkedIn Recruiter integrations. List exports and CRM sync are on paid plans.

How much is Skrapp.io Professional?

The page shows Professional at $29 a month billed annually, with 2,000 credits a month and 2 users; a $39 figure is also shown on the plan card. Check the billing toggle on their page for the monthly price.

Does Skrapp.io charge for invalid emails?

Its FAQ says no: credits are used only for emails marked Valid or Catch-all, not for Invalid or Unknown results.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a named person's email like Skrapp.io?

No, not by design. It reads only what each website publishes and by default returns shared role inboxes such as info@ and sales@. Personal addresses are dropped unless you switch them on, which needs a lawful basis under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
