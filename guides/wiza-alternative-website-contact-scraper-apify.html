Short answer: Wiza is a B2B contact reveal and prospecting product. Its pricing page (checked 3 Nov 2026) shows a Free plan at $0 with 20 valid emails and 5 phone numbers. On monthly billing, Starter is $49 per user per month (100 valid emails and 100 phone numbers, with $0.15 per extra email and $0.35 per extra phone), Email is $99 per user per month (500 valid emails, $0.15 extra, phones at $0.35 each), and Email + Phone is $199 per user per month (500 emails and 500 phones, same extras). On annual “Unlimited” billing, Email is shown as $83 per user per month billed at $990 per year (unlimited email reveals, $0.35 per phone, 2,500 exports per month), and Email + Phone as $166 per user per month billed at $1,990 per year (unlimited email and phone reveals, 2,500 exports). A Team plan is custom pricing for 3+ users. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a narrower job: it visits each website in your list and returns the inboxes, phones and WhatsApp links that business publishes, with the source page. There is no Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage (Apify Free includes $5 of usage a month). If you need LinkedIn-style reveals of named people and CRM sync, Wiza is built for that. If you have a list of business websites and want their published contacts, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“I exported 250 exhibitors. I need the contact page each company shows, including WhatsApp.” That is a website-reading job. “I have LinkedIn profiles and need verified work emails for outreach” is a reveal job.

Who should pick Wiza instead

  • You need named people's work emails or phones for outbound sales, with per-user plans.
  • You want unlimited email reveals on annual Email or Email + Phone plans, plus CRM integrations.
  • You prefer a product built around prospecting credits rather than crawling websites you already have.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You start from business websites (directory, member list, Maps export) and want what each site publishes.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Wiza cost?

Its pricing page (checked 3 Nov 2026) lists Free at $0; monthly Starter $49, Email $99 and Email + Phone $199 per user; annual Email at $990 a year and Email + Phone at $1,990 a year; Team custom for 3+ users.

Does Wiza have a free plan?

Yes. Free includes 20 valid emails and 5 phone numbers, per its pricing page.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a specific person's email from LinkedIn?

No. It only reads the websites you give it and, by default, keeps shared role inboxes.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
