Short answer: Web Scraper Cloud (webscraper.io) is a general, fully managed scraping platform: you define extraction visually (AI-assisted), and it runs at scale with proxies, CAPTCHA and bot-protection handling, a scheduler, a parser, data-quality checks and exports to Sheets, Drive, S3 and more. Its pricing page (checked 21 Oct 2026) shows Project at $50 a month (5,000 URL credits, 2 concurrent scrapers), Professional at $100 (20,000 URL credits) and Scale from $200, each shown billed annually, with a 7-day free trial and no card. If your job is just “give me every product and price from these few Shopify or WooCommerce shops”, our Actor does that without building a sitemap, is free during launch, and you pay only Apify platform usage. For many different sites, marketplaces or protected sites, Web Scraper Cloud is the better pick.

The pain this page is about

You want a competitor's product list in a spreadsheet before you set your festive or Black Friday prices. A general scraper can do it, but first you build and maintain the extraction for each shop.

Who should pick Web Scraper Cloud instead

  • You scrape many kinds of sites, not just Shopify/WooCommerce shops.
  • You need marketplaces (Amazon, eBay) or sites with heavy bot protection.
  • You want a managed platform with data-quality monitoring, a parser and direct cloud-storage exports for a production pipeline.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your competitors are independent Shopify or WooCommerce shops and you want their catalogue today, without designing an extraction.
  • You prefer a tool that respects blocks rather than bypassing them.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Web Scraper Cloud cost?

Its pricing page (checked 21 Oct 2026) shows Project at $50 a month with 5,000 URL credits, Professional at $100 with 20,000, Scale from $200 and custom Enterprise, shown billed annually, each with a 7-day free trial that needs no card.

What is a URL credit on Web Scraper Cloud?

Per its FAQ, one URL credit is used each time a page is loaded, however many records you extract from it.

Does the Unadhi Actor work on any website?

No. It is built only for independent Shopify, WooCommerce and schema.org shops, and refuses marketplaces such as Amazon, eBay and Flipkart.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
