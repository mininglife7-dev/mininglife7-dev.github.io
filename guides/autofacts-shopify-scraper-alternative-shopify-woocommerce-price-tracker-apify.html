Short answer: Both run on Apify Store. The Shopify Scraper by autofacts reads public Shopify stores in depth: every variant's price, SKU and stock status, plus barcodes, weights, subscription plans, videos and, where a store publishes it, real stock counts; it can also run a keyword search across stores you list. Its Store page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows pay-per-result pricing: $0.001 per product on the Apify Free plan, falling to $0.0007 (from $0.70 per 1,000) on Gold and higher plans, with optional extras such as real inventory at $0.001 per product. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker reads both Shopify and WooCommerce shops and returns one row per product with variants, price, compare-at price, stock, SKU/GTIN, images and URLs, plus a per-shop summary. It has no Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you only track Shopify and want barcodes, real stock counts or subscription plans, pick the autofacts Actor. If your competitor list mixes Shopify and WooCommerce, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Three of my competitors are on Shopify and two are on WooCommerce. I want one weekly price sheet for all five, not two tools.” Some Actors go very deep on one platform. Ours covers the two most common shop platforms with the same columns.

Who should pick the autofacts Shopify Scraper instead

  • All the shops you watch are on Shopify, and you want the deepest Shopify fields.
  • You need barcodes (UPC/EAN/GTIN), real stock counts, B2B price breaks or subscription plans.
  • You want a keyword search across a list of Shopify stores.

Who our Actor fits better

  • Your competitors are a mix of Shopify and WooCommerce, and you want the same columns for all.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the autofacts Shopify Scraper cost?

Its Apify Store page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows pay-per-result pricing of $0.001 per product on the Apify Free plan, $0.0009 on Bronze, $0.0008 on Silver and $0.0007 on Gold and higher, with collections at $0.0004 and optional real inventory or information panels at $0.001 per product.

Does the autofacts Actor work on WooCommerce?

Its page describes it as a Shopify scraper and says a site that is not a Shopify store fails at once. For WooCommerce shops you need another Actor.

Does the Unadhi Actor return real stock counts or barcodes?

It returns availability and SKU/GTIN where the shop publishes them, but not real stock counts, B2B price breaks or subscription plans.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many teams use a deep Shopify Actor for Shopify-only shops and ours where WooCommerce shops are in the list.
