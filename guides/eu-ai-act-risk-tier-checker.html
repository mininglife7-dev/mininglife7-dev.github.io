1. What is your role for this AI system?

Definitions: Art. 3(3) provider, 3(4) deployer, 3(6) importer, 3(7) distributor. If you change a bought-in system's intended purpose or put your name on it, you may become its provider (Art. 25).

2. Prohibited-practice screen (Article 5). Does the system do any of these?

Tick all that apply. Leave blank if none.

3. Is it intended for any of these uses (Annex III)?

Tick all that apply. Leave blank if none.

3a. Does one of these describe the system's only role in that use (Article 6(3))?

Tick only if true and the system does not materially influence the outcome of decisions.

3b. Does it profile natural persons (Art. 4(4) GDPR)?
4. Is the AI system a product, or a safety component of a product, covered by EU product law in Annex I (for example medical devices, toys, lifts, radio equipment, machinery, vehicles)?
4a. Does that product need a third-party (notified body) conformity assessment under its sector law?
4b. Does the AI only handle non-safety aspects (user assistance, performance optimisation, efficiency, automation, convenience or quality control) so that its failure would not endanger health or safety?
5. Transparency triggers (Article 50). Tick all that apply.

These duties already apply. AI literacy for staff (Article 4) since 2 Feb 2025; chatbot and AI-content transparency (Article 50) since 2 Aug 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
