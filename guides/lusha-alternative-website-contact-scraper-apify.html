Short answer: Lusha is a B2B contact database with a browser extension, CRM integrations, an API and MCP access. It works on credits: per its pricing FAQ (checked 22 Oct 2026), revealing a verified email costs 1 credit and a phone number 5 credits. The plan data on the page lists Free at $0 (40 credits a month), Starter at $49.90 a month billed monthly (400 credits) or $37.45 a month billed annually ($449.10 a year, 4,800 credits), Pro at $69.90 or $52.45 (600 credits a month) and Premium at $399.90 or $299.95 (3,400 credits a month). If your job is “I have a list of company websites and want the emails, phones, WhatsApp and social links each site publishes, one row per site”, our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does that, is free during launch, and you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need a named decision-maker's email or mobile number, Lusha is the better pick.

The pain this page is about

“I have 200 clinics, dealers or shops from Google Maps. I need a way to reach each business this week.” A contact database answers who works there and how to reach that person; a website scraper answers what this business publishes for customers to contact it. Paying 5 credits per phone reveal makes sense for a decision-maker's mobile, less so for a shop's own front-desk number that is already on its contact page.

Who should pick Lusha instead

  • You sell to companies and need the named decision-maker's work email or direct phone, not the front desk.
  • You prospect from LinkedIn or your CRM and want contacts revealed in place, with CRM sync.
  • You want one credit balance across a web app, browser extension, API and MCP for AI agents.
  • 40 free credits a month is enough for your volume.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You start from a list of websites (Google Maps, a directory, a dealer list) and want what each publishes, including phones and WhatsApp that Indian SMEs, clinics and shops often list instead of email.
  • You want a privacy-first default (shared role inboxes only) and the source page of every contact, so you can show where it came from if asked.
  • You want to test at zero cost inside Apify's free $5 monthly usage, with no credits to budget.

Frequently asked questions

Does Lusha have a free plan?

Yes. Its pricing page (checked 22 Oct 2026) describes a permanent Free plan with up to 40 credits a month, basic prospecting features, the browser extension and CRM integrations.

How many Lusha credits does a phone number cost?

Its pricing FAQ says revealing a verified email costs 1 credit and revealing a phone number costs 5 credits.

How much is Lusha Starter?

The plan data on its pricing page shows Starter at $49.90 a month billed monthly with 400 credits a month, or $37.45 a month billed annually ($449.10 a year for 4,800 credits), before tax.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a decision-maker's mobile number like Lusha?

No, not by design. It reads only what each company's website publishes and by default returns shared role inboxes and the business's own phone and WhatsApp numbers. Personal addresses are dropped unless you switch them on, which needs a lawful basis under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
