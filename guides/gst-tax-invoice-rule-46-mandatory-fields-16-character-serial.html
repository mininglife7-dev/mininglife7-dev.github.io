What a valid GST tax invoice must show: Rule 46 fields, the 16-character serial number and HSN digits

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules a tax invoice must show the supplier's name, address and GSTIN, a consecutive serial number of up to 16 characters (letters, numbers, '-' and '/') that is unique for the financial year, the date, the recipient's name, address and GSTIN (if registered), HSN/SAC, description, quantity, taxable value, rate and amount of tax, place of supply for inter-state supplies, whether tax is payable on reverse charge, and a signature or digital signature. For services, issue it within 30 days of supply (Rule 47).

Field checklist

FieldNote
Supplier name, address, GSTINAs on the registration certificate
Invoice numberMax 16 characters; one or more series; unique per FY
Recipient detailsGSTIN if registered; for unregistered recipients with value ₹50,000 or more, name, address and state
HSN / SAC4 digits up to ₹5 crore turnover, 6 digits above (Notification 78/2020-CT)
Values and taxTaxable value after discount, rate and amount of CGST/SGST/IGST
Place of supplyWith state name, for inter-state supplies
Reverse chargeState whether tax is payable on reverse charge
SignatureSignature or digital signature of the supplier or authorised person

Copies (Rule 48)

Goods: original for recipient, duplicate for transporter, triplicate for supplier. Services: original and duplicate. Businesses covered by e-invoicing must also report to the IRP.

Common mistakes

  • Restarting numbering mid-year or reusing a number across branches on one GSTIN.
  • Long invoice numbers over 16 characters (e.g. full dates plus branch codes).
  • No place of supply on inter-state invoices, leading to wrong IGST/CGST split.
  • Missing reverse-charge flag.

Frequently asked questions

How long can a GST invoice number be?

Up to 16 characters, using letters, numbers, hyphen or slash, unique for the financial year (Rule 46(b)).

Is HSN code mandatory on a GST invoice?

Yes: 4 digits for turnover up to ₹5 crore and 6 digits above, as notified in Notification 78/2020-Central Tax.

What is the time limit to issue an invoice for services?

Within 30 days of the date of supply of service (Rule 47).

How many copies of a GST invoice are needed for goods?

Three: original for the recipient, duplicate for the transporter and triplicate for the supplier (Rule 48).
