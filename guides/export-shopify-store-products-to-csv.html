How to export a Shopify or WooCommerce store's product list to CSV (for price tracking)

Updated 13 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the Apify Actor mentioned in option 3 is built and published by Unadhi.

Short answer: most Shopify shops publish their catalogue at /products.json, and most WooCommerce shops at /wp-json/wc/store/v1/products. Open that URL, or let a scraper read it for many shops at once and give you a CSV.

Who this is for

Sellers watching competitor prices, agencies auditing a client's catalogue before a migration, and anyone who needs a product list from a shop they don't own the admin login for. If it's your own shop, use the built-in export in your admin first; it's free and complete.

Option 1: Shopify's products.json (free, one shop)

  1. Take the shop's address, for example https://example-shop.com.
  2. Add /products.json?limit=250 and open it in your browser.
  3. If you see JSON, the shop's public catalogue is readable. Add &page=2, &page=3 and so on until a page comes back empty.
  4. Paste the JSON into a JSON-to-CSV converter, or load it in Google Sheets with a script.

Some shops switch this endpoint off or put it behind a bot check. If you get an error page, respect that and stop.

Option 2: WooCommerce Store API (free, one shop)

Open https://example-shop.com/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=100. Add &page=2 for the next page. Prices come back in the shop's minor units (for example paise or cents), so divide by 100 where needed.

Option 3: many shops at once, straight to CSV

Doing this by hand for 20 competitors every week gets old. Our E-commerce Product Scraper on Apify tries Shopify products.json, then the WooCommerce Store API, then schema.org data on the page, and gives you one row per product: title, brand, price, currency, sale price, stock, SKU/GTIN, variants and images.

When the run finishes, download the dataset as CSV or Excel from the run page, or by API:

curl "https://api.apify.com/v2/datasets/<DATASET_ID>/items?format=csv&token=<YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN>" -o products.csv

To track prices, schedule the same input daily in Apify and compare the price column between runs. Current pricing is shown on the Store page; Apify's free monthly credit covers small test runs.

What it won't do

  • It checks robots.txt and the shop's terms first, and skips shops that forbid scraping. A summary record tells you which shops were skipped and why.
  • It never fetches marketplaces such as Amazon, eBay or Flipkart.
  • It never logs in or gets around blocks.
