Short answer: Dealfront identifies the companies that visit your website and, on higher plans, turns them into contact leads with verified emails and phones, intent filters and CRM automation. Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026, English and German) lists Lite at €0 (the last 100 companies a month in Web Visitors, 7-day visitor history, unlimited users), Discover starting at €79 a month billed annually (full company profiles, ad reports, AI reports, CRM sync, API and MCP access), Activate starting at €369 a month billed annually (verified emails and phones, intent filters, prospect lists built with AI) and Scale starting at €599 a month, billed annually only. The price tier depends on how many companies it identifies on your site each month, and AI enrichment is paid with credits. Its tier table shows two figures per row under a Monthly/Annual (save 30%) switch, for example €79 and €113 for Discover up to 50 companies. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a much narrower job: give it company website addresses and it reads their public pages for role emails, phone numbers and WhatsApp links, returning rows with the page each was found on. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It does not know who visits your site. If you want to see which companies read your website and reach the right people there, Dealfront is built for that. If you have a list of company websites and want the contacts they publish, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Which companies looked at our pricing page this week, and who should we call there?” That is a visitor-identification job. “I have a list of 200 suppliers' websites; what contact email does each publish?” is a website-reading job.

  • You want to know which companies visit your website and which campaigns bring them.

  • You want CRM sync and automation for a whole revenue team.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Dealfront cost?

Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists Lite at €0, Discover from €79 a month, Activate from €369 and Scale from €599, each billed annually (Scale is annual only). The tier depends on how many companies it identifies on your site each month.

Does Dealfront's free Lite plan include contacts?

Its page lists the last 100 companies a month in Web Visitors and a 7-day visitor history on Lite; verified emails and phones are listed from Activate.

Does the Unadhi Actor track my website visitors?

No. It reads public contact details from the websites you list and nothing else.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 17 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
