The EC Sales List explained: Articles 262 to 264 of the VAT Directive

Last updated 24 Oct 2026 · European Union

Short answer. Under Article 262 of the EU VAT Directive, a VAT-registered business must file a recapitulative statement (EC Sales List) of its intra-Community supplies of goods to VAT-identified customers and its B2B services taxed under the reverse charge (Article 196). It is filed for each calendar month, within a period set by the member state of no more than one month (Article 263). Member states may allow quarterly filing for goods where intra-Community supplies do not exceed €50,000 a quarter, and for services. Each line shows the customer's VAT number and the total value supplied (Article 264).

Checklist

  • Every intra-EU B2B goods sale and reverse-charge service listed
  • Customer VAT numbers checked in VIES
  • Totals agree with the VAT return for the same period
  • Filed by your member state's deadline

Common mistakes

  • Filing quarterly after crossing €50,000 of goods in a quarter, where your member state requires a switch to monthly.
  • Leaving out services because only goods were expected.
  • VAT return and EC Sales List not matching, which often triggers questions.
  • Including B2C sales, which belong in OSS, not the EC Sales List.

Frequently asked questions

Who must file an EC Sales List?

A VAT-registered business making intra-Community supplies of goods to VAT-identified customers or B2B services taxed by the customer under the reverse charge (Article 262).

Can the EC Sales List be quarterly?

Member states may allow quarterly filing for goods where intra-Community supplies do not exceed EUR 50,000 a quarter, and for services (Article 263).

Do B2C sales go on the EC Sales List?

No. It covers supplies to VAT-identified customers; B2C distance sales are handled through OSS.
