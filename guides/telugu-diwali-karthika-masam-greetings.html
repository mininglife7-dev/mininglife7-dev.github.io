Telugu Deepavali and Karthika Masam greetings (దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు), for family, customers and relatives abroad

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · Telugu speakers everywhere

Short answer. The standard Telugu wish is దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు (Deepavali shubhakankshalu, “Deepavali wishes”). For a family message: మీకు, మీ కుటుంబ సభ్యులందరికీ దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు (“Deepavali wishes to you and all your family”). After Deepavali comes Karthika Masam: కార్తీక మాస శుభాకాంక్షలు. In 2026, Telangana's official holiday list puts Naraka Chaturdasi and Deepavali on Sunday 8 November; panchangam sites give Karthika Masam from about 10 November. Check your family's panchangam for exact days.

Why the wording matters

Festival wishes are often the one message a customer or a distant relative reads fully all year. A short, correct Telugu line feels personal; a forwarded image with broken letters or a hard sell does the opposite.

Greetings and what they mean

TeluguMeaning / use
దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలుDeepavali wishes (the everyday greeting)
నరక చతుర్దశి శుభాకాంక్షలుNaraka Chaturdasi wishes (the day before or the morning of Deepavali, depending on the year)
మీకు, మీ కుటుంబ సభ్యులందరికీ దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలుDeepavali wishes to you and all your family

కార్తీక మాస శుభాకాంక్షలుKarthika Masam wishes
కార్తీక పౌర్ణమి శుభాకాంక్షలుKarthika Pournami wishes (full-moon day of the month)

A polite greeting from a shop or business

మా వినియోగదారులందరికీ దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు! మీ ఆదరణకు హృదయపూర్వక ధన్యవాదాలు. — [దుకాణం పేరు]

English: Happy Deepavali to all our customers! Heartfelt thanks for your support. — [shop name]

If you mention festival timings or an offer, add one plain line with real dates and prices only. Keep the greeting first and the offer second.

For relatives and clients abroad

మీకు, మీ కుటుంబానికి దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు. దూరంగా ఉన్నా మా దీపాల వెలుగు మీకు చేరాలని కోరుకుంటున్నాము.

English: Deepavali wishes to you and your family. Though you are far away, we hope the light of our lamps reaches you. Tip: the morning of 8 November in India is still the evening of 7 November in the US, so send it at a time that suits their zone.

Common mistakes

  • Broken Telugu letters in images made with apps that don't support the script. Use a Telugu font such as Noto Sans Telugu and check on a phone.
  • Messaging people who never opted in. WhatsApp's business policy requires the customer's number and their permission before you message them; bulk-forwarding to every contact can get a business number blocked.
  • Turning the greeting into an ad. One wish, one thank-you, at most one line about an offer.
  • Wrong day. Festival days can differ by region and by panchangam; confirm locally.

Checklist before sending

  • Greeting checked by a fluent reader
  • Shop or family name spelled right
  • Only customers who agreed to hear from you
  • Any offer has real dates and prices
  • Image tested on Android and iPhone
  • Time zone considered for relatives abroad

తెలుగులో సంక్షిప్తంగా

దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు చిన్నగా, స్పష్టంగా ఉండాలి. వ్యాపార సందేశంలో ముందు శుభాకాంక్షలు, తర్వాత ధన్యవాదాలు చెప్పండి. అనుమతి ఇచ్చిన వినియోగదారులకు మాత్రమే పంపండి.

How do you say Happy Diwali in Telugu?

దీపావళి శుభాకాంక్షలు (Deepavali shubhakankshalu), meaning Deepavali wishes.

When is Deepavali in 2026 in Telangana?

Telangana's official 2026 holiday list (G.O.Rt.No.1715) gives Naraka Chaturdasi and Deepavali on Sunday 8 November 2026.

When does Karthika Masam start in 2026?

Panchangam websites give about 10 November 2026 for the Telugu (amanta) Karthika Masam. Confirm with your local panchangam.

What is a Karthika Masam greeting in Telugu?

కార్తీక మాస శుభాకాంక్షలు (Karthika masa shubhakankshalu).
