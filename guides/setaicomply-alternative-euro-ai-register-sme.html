Why this matters now (official dates)

  • Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 to providers and deployers, whatever the risk level. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, it asks organisations to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff; it does not require them to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The European Commission's Q&A says there is no need for a certificate: an internal record of trainings or guidance is enough, and national authorities supervise and enforce from August 2026 (Commission AI literacy Q&A).
  • Article 50 transparency duties (for example, telling people they are talking to a chatbot) apply from 2 August 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
  • High-risk rules were moved by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III uses (such as recruitment and credit scoring) from 2 December 2027, and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SetAIComply cost?

SetAIComply's pricing page (read 14 Oct 2026) says from €39 a month. Yearly plans are Starter €390 (up to 5 AI systems), Growth €1,290, Scale €3,490 and Enterprise €9,588. It offers a 14-day trial without a card and a free Compliance Snapshot.

Do we have to buy an AI literacy course for Article 4?

Not necessarily. The European Commission's AI literacy Q&A says there is no need for a certificate; organisations can keep an internal record of trainings or other guidance.

Is EURO AI Register enough for a small business?

For a deployer with a handful of non-high-risk AI tools, an inventory, a dated literacy record and an Article 50 check often cover the duties that apply today. It is a record-keeping tool, not proof of compliance; get legal advice for anything that may be high-risk.

Do the high-risk rules apply from August 2026?

No. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 set Annex III high-risk obligations for 2 December 2027 and Annex I (regulated products) for 2 August 2028.
