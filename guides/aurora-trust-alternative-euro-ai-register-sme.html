EURO AI Register is for small businesses that mostly use AI tools such as chatbots, writing assistants and AI inside software: an AI inventory, duties by role with dates and a printable Article 4 literacy record, free for up to 10 AI systems in your browser, or $19 a month for unlimited. If you are a provider of a possibly high-risk system, Aurora Trust fits better. If you need your firm's AI list and literacy record, start with ours.

The pain this page is about

“We are a 12-person agency. We use ChatGPT, Copilot and an AI note-taker. Do we need a €49-per-system platform?” Most small firms are deployers of other people's AI. Their first duties are knowing what they use and supporting staff AI literacy. Firms that build a hiring or credit model have far heavier provider duties.

Why this matters now (official dates)

  • Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 to providers and deployers, whatever the risk level. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, it asks organisations to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff; it does not require them to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The European Commission's Q&A says there is no need for a certificate: an internal record of trainings or guidance is enough, and national authorities supervise and enforce from August 2026 (Commission AI literacy Q&A).
  • Article 50 transparency duties (for example, telling people they are talking to a chatbot) apply from 2 August 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
  • High-risk rules were moved by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III uses (such as recruitment and credit scoring) from 2 December 2027, and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028.

Who should pick Aurora Trust instead

  • You are, or may be, a provider of a high-risk system (for example, CV screening, credit scoring, clinical decision support) and need technical documentation and a risk management record.
  • You fine-tune or substantially change a model for your own product and want documents mapped to the AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001.
  • You want ongoing monitoring of a model's documentation as rules and the system change.

Who EURO AI Register fits better

  • You use AI tools rather than build them, and need a clear list of them with owners and purposes.
  • You want a printable Article 4 AI-literacy record today, free, without uploading anything.
  • You are an accountant or adviser starting a simple inventory for SME clients.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Aurora Trust cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows Starter at €49 a month for risk classification of 1 AI system, Growth at €149 a month for up to 3 systems with continuous monitoring, Scale at €299 a month for up to 15 systems with a compliance calendar, and Enterprise on request. Access is by request through its form.

Is Aurora Trust for AI users or AI builders?

Its pages describe connecting AI models or datasets by API or model metadata and generating provider documents such as technical documentation and conformity declarations, with examples in credit, hiring and clinical AI. It also lists an AI system inventory. Small firms that only use AI tools should ask whether a per-system plan fits them.

When do the EU AI Act high-risk rules apply?

Under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027 and Annex I (AI in regulated products) from 2 August 2028. Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 and Article 50 transparency from 2 August 2026.

Does EURO AI Register write technical documentation or conformity declarations?

No. It records your AI systems, duties by role and AI-literacy measures. If you may be a provider of a high-risk system, use a provider tool and a qualified lawyer.

What does EURO AI Register cost?

Free for up to 10 AI systems in your browser with no account. Membership is $19 a month or $190 a year (plus VAT where applicable) for unlimited systems, with a 14-day money-back promise.
