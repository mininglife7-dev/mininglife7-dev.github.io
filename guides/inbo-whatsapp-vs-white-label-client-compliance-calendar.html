Short answer: They do different jobs. INBO CA Compliance sends automated WhatsApp reminders to each client from one shared, verified INBO number, branded “via INBO” with your firm name; its public plans are Starter ₹999/month (up to 50 clients) and Professional ₹2,499/month (up to 200 clients). A white-label calendar gives you the content — a quarterly DOCX/PDF/WhatsApp image with your firm's name, plus .ics and .xlsx — that you send yourself, to unlimited clients, for ₹999/year. If you want hands-off per-client reminders with delivery tracking, pick INBO. If you want a branded quarterly deliverable for every client at the lowest cost, start with the calendar. Many firms could use both.

Who should pick INBO instead

  • You want reminders to go out automatically to each client without anyone at the firm pressing send.
  • You need delivery tracking to follow up only with clients who did not receive a message.
  • Your client list is within 50 or 200 active clients and the monthly fee is easy to recover.

Who the white-label calendar fits better

  • You want a deliverable with your own brand (no “via” line) that clients keep and print.
  • You have many small clients and per-month pricing by client count does not suit you.
  • You want every date tied to an official source and an extension update if a date moves.

Frequently asked questions

How much does INBO CA Compliance cost?

INBO's public page (read 14 Oct 2026) lists Starter at ₹999 a month for up to 50 active clients and Professional at ₹2,499 a month for up to 200, with a shared INBO WhatsApp number. A higher tier with unlimited clients and a dedicated number is listed without a price.

Does the white-label calendar send WhatsApp messages automatically?

No. It gives your firm branded files and a WhatsApp-sized image to send yourself. For automatic per-client sending with delivery tracking, a tool like INBO does that job.
