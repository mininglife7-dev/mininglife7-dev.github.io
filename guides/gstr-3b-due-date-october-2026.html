Short answer (checked 14 Oct 2026): In October 2026, monthly GSTR-3B filers must file the September 2026 return by 20 October 2026 in every state; QRMP quarterly filers must file GSTR-3B for July–September 2026 by 22 October 2026 (22nd-group states, including Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra and Gujarat) or 24 October 2026 (24th-group states, including Delhi, Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal), per the GST portal Form GSTR-3B user guide and the state groups in the GSTN QRMP advisory. This guide is for GST-registered businesses and their accountants in India who file GSTR-3B. Common mistakes

  • Using one national date for quarterly GSTR-3B. The date is 22nd or 24th by state group.
  • Confusing the October tax period with due dates that fall in October. The September monthly return is due 20 Oct; the October monthly return is due 20 Nov.
  • Waiting for an extension rumour. Extensions appear as notifications on the GST / CBIC channels. Until you see one, file on time.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GSTR-3B due date in October 2026 for monthly filers?

20 October 2026, for the September 2026 tax period, in every state.

What is the GSTR-3B due date in October 2026 for quarterly (QRMP) filers?

22 October 2026 in 22nd-group states, or 24 October 2026 in 24th-group states, for July–September 2026.

Where do these dates come from?

The GST portal Form GSTR-3B user guide (20th monthly; 22nd and 24th quarterly by State/UT) and the GSTN QRMP advisory for the state groups.

Has the October 2026 GSTR-3B due date been extended?

As of 13 October 2026 we found no official CBIC or GST portal notification extending the 20/22/24 October 2026 dates. Confirm on the GST portal before filing.
