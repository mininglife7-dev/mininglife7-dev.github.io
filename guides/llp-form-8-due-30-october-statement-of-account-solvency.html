LLP Form 8 is due 30 October: what goes in it, who signs, and the ₹100-a-day additional fee

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Every LLP files its Statement of Account and Solvency in Form 8 within 30 days from the end of six months of the financial year, so FY 2025-26 accounts are due by 30 October 2026 (Rule 24, LLP Rules 2009). The annual return, Form 11, is due within 60 days of the year end (30 May). Late filing carries an additional fee of ₹100 per day per form. Accounts must be audited if turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh.

LLP annual filing calendar

FormWhatDue
Form 11Annual return (partners, contribution)30 May
Form 8Statement of Account and Solvency30 October
Income-tax returnSeparate, under the income-tax lawAs notified for the year

What Form 8 needs

  • Statement of assets and liabilities and income and expenditure for the year
  • Solvency declaration by the designated partners
  • Signatures of two designated partners with DSC
  • Certificate by a practising CA, CS or cost accountant
  • Auditor's report where the audit limits are crossed

Common mistakes

  • "No business, so no filing". A dormant LLP still files Form 8 and Form 11.
  • Expired DSC of a designated partner discovered on the last day.
  • Contribution in Form 8 not matching Form 11 / Form 3.
  • Ignoring the per-day fee. It runs per form for every day of delay, so small delays add up.

Checklist

  • Accounts finalised (audited if over the limits)
  • Both designated partners' DSCs valid
  • Professional certifying the form lined up
  • Form 8 filed by 30 October
  • Form 11 status checked for the same year

Frequently asked questions

What is the due date of LLP Form 8 for FY 2025-26?

30 October 2026: within 30 days from the end of six months of the financial year (Rule 24, LLP Rules 2009).

What is the late fee for LLP Form 8?

An additional fee of ₹100 per day of delay.

When is LLP audit compulsory?

When turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the financial year.

Does an LLP with no business need to file Form 8?

Yes. The filing duty does not depend on having business in the year.
