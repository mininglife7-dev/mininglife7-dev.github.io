GSTR-1 for quarterly (QRMP) filers: 13 October 2026, and when to use IFF

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. If you are in the QRMP scheme (aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore), your quarterly GSTR-1 for July–September 2026 is due on Tuesday 13 October 2026, in every state. In the first two months of a quarter you may also use the optional Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) by the 13th of the next month, so your business customers get input tax credit monthly. IFF is for B2B invoices only, is capped at ₹50 lakh of invoice value per month, has no late fee, and simply expires after the 13th. As of 11 Oct 2026 we found no notification extending 13 Oct.

Why IFF exists

A quarterly filer's invoices normally reach the buyer's GSTR-2B only once a quarter. Your registered customers then wait up to three months for their input tax credit, and some prefer suppliers who file monthly. IFF (Rule 59(2) of the CGST Rules) lets you upload B2B invoices for month 1 and month 2, so they appear in the buyer's GSTR-2A/2B that month, while you still file one GSTR-1 for the quarter.

What goes into IFF and what doesn't

  • In IFF: B2B invoices (tables 4A, 4B, 4C, 6B, 6C), credit/debit notes to registered persons (9B) and their amendments (9A, 9C).
  • Not in IFF: B2C sales. Report them in the quarterly GSTR-1.
  • Invoices filed in IFF are not entered again in the quarterly GSTR-1.
  • If the total value of the IFF exceeds ₹50 lakh, the portal shows "Error in submission". Move some invoices to the next period.

Late fee for GSTR-1

IFF has no late fee. A late GSTR-1 does. Under Notification 20/2021-Central Tax, the total CGST late fee per return is capped at ₹250 for a nil return, ₹1,000 if last year's aggregate turnover was up to ₹1.5 crore, and ₹2,500 for ₹1.5–5 crore. States levy a matching amount under their SGST laws, so the total you pay is usually double these caps. A late GSTR-1 also delays your customers' credit, which can cost you the customer.

Returns now time out. Under sections 37(5) and 39(11) of the CGST Act, a return cannot be filed after three years from its due date. The GST portal began blocking such returns from the November 2025 tax period (GSTN advisory, 29 Oct 2025).

A claim we could not confirm. One May 2026 blog post said IFF was withdrawn from July 2026 and that QRMP filers must now file GSTR-1 monthly, citing "Notification 14/2026-Central Tax". We found no such Central Tax notification, the GST portal's IFF tutorial still describes the facility, and other practitioners describe IFF for July 2026 as available. Check the Returns Dashboard on gst.gov.in, which shows the returns that apply to you.

Common mistakes

  • Waiting for the quarter to upload B2B invoices. If a big customer needs credit this month, use IFF by the 13th.
  • Submitting IFF and not filing it. A submitted IFF must be filed, or the quarterly GSTR-1 cannot be filed.
  • Putting B2C sales into IFF. They belong in the quarterly GSTR-1.
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B that don't match. If GSTR-1 tax exceeds GSTR-3B tax beyond the notified limit, the portal sends DRC-01B and asks you to pay with interest or explain within 7 days (Rule 88C).
  • Missing the 31 Oct switch window. If you want monthly filing from October, change it by 31 Oct; otherwise your current choice continues.

Checklist for 13 October

  • All Jul–Sep invoices, credit and debit notes listed
  • Invoices already in Jul/Aug IFF not repeated
  • B2C summary and HSN summary filled
  • Customer GSTINs checked
  • GSTR-1 filed with EVC or DSC by 13 Oct, acknowledgment saved
  • Decide quarterly vs monthly for Oct–Dec before 31 Oct
  • Calendar reminder: IFF for October by 13 Nov (if you use it)

Frequently asked questions

What is the GSTR-1 due date for QRMP filers for July–September 2026?

13 October 2026, the 13th day of the month after the quarter. As of 11 October 2026 we found no notification extending it.

Is IFF mandatory for QRMP taxpayers?

No. IFF is optional. Use it only if you want your registered customers to get input tax credit in months 1 and 2 of the quarter.

What is the IFF limit?

The total value of invoices in one month's IFF cannot exceed ₹50 lakh. Above that the portal will not allow filing.

Is there a late fee for IFF?

No. IFF simply expires after the 13th of the next month; unfiled invoices go into the next IFF or the quarterly GSTR-1.

Can I switch from quarterly to monthly GSTR-1 for October–December 2026?

Yes, the frequency for the October–December quarter can be changed from 1 August to 31 October.
