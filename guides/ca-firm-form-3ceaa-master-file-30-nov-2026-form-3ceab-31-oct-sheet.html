Short answer (checked 22 Oct 2026): every constituent entity of an international group must furnish Part A of Form 3CEAA, even if it is below the thresholds (Rule 10DA(3), Income-tax Rules, 1962). The full master file (Part B) is needed only if the group's consolidated revenue for the accounting year exceeds ₹500 crore and the entity's international transactions exceed ₹50 crore, or its intangible-property transactions exceed ₹10 crore (Rule 10DA(1)). Form 3CEAA is due on or before the due date for the return under section 139(1) (Rule 10DA(2), as substituted by Notification No. 03/2020, G.S.R. 14(E), 6 Jan 2020). For an entity that files a section 92E (Form 3CEB) report, that is 30 November 2026, a Monday (department's return of income page, Sl. No. 1). Where a group has more than one constituent entity resident in India and wants only one of them to file, the designation must be conveyed in Form 3CEAB thirty days before the Form 3CEAA due date (Rule 10DA(4)): against 30 November that is 31 October 2026, a Saturday. This is a starting point, not tax advice.

Tab "part-b-pack": what Part B asks for (for "Part A + Part B" rows)

One row per item, with columns Item, Asked from (group tax team / Indian finance), Requested on, Received on, Notes. The items, from Rule 10DA(1) and Form 3CEAA Part B:

  • List of all group entities with addresses, and a chart of legal status and ownership of the whole group.
  • Business description: nature of the businesses, profit drivers, supply chain for the five largest products or services and any others above 5% of group revenue, important service arrangements, transfer pricing policies for intra-group services, major geographical markets, functions-assets-risks of entities contributing at least 10% of revenue, assets or profits, and important restructurings, acquisitions and divestments.
  • Intangibles: overall strategy and principal R&D locations, entities that develop and manage intangibles, important intangibles and their legal owners, related agreements (cost contribution, research services, licences), transfer pricing policies for R&D and intangibles, and important transfers of interests in intangibles.
  • Financing: financing arrangements with the top ten unrelated lenders, entities providing central financing functions, and transfer pricing policies for intra-group financing.
  • A copy of the annual consolidated financial statement, and a list of existing unilateral APAs and other tax rulings on allocating income between countries.

Foreign parents usually hold most of this in their group master file, so the "Requested on" date matters more than anything else in this tab: ask this week.

The rules the formulas follow

  • Part A for everyone in the group: name, address and PAN of the entity, the group's name and address, the accounting year, and the number, names, PANs and addresses of all constituent entities in India. Required even below the thresholds (Rule 10DA(3)).
  • Part B only above both tests: group revenue over ₹500 crore and international transactions over ₹50 crore, or intangible-property transactions over ₹10 crore (Rule 10DA(1)).
  • Due date: on or before the section 139(1) return due date (Rule 10DA(2)).
  • One filer for the group: only where the group has more than one constituent entity resident in India, has designated one of them, and that has been conveyed in Form 3CEAB thirty days before the Form 3CEAA due date (Rule 10DA(4)). If that does not happen, plan on each entity filing its own Form 3CEAA.
  • Penalty: failing to furnish the information and document required under section 92D(4) can attract a penalty of ₹5,00,000 under section 271AA(2), levied by the prescribed authority; section 273B allows a reasonable-cause defence.

Two client messages

1. Group designation: "Your company is part of an international group, so it must file Form 3CEAA (master file) by 30 November 2026. If the group has more than one entity in India and wants only one of them to file for all, that entity must file Form 3CEAB by 31 October 2026. Please confirm by [date] which Indian entity, if any, the group designates, and send last year's group consolidated revenue and accounting year."

2. Part B documents: "Your group's revenue and your international transactions are above the limits for the full master file (Part B of Form 3CEAA). Please ask the group tax team for the group master file, ownership chart, consolidated financial statements and list of APAs and tax rulings for the accounting year by [date], so we can file before 30 November 2026."

Notes

  • This sheet is for AY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26) under the Income-tax Act, 1961 and the 1962 Rules. From tax year 2026-27 the master file moves to rule 123 of the Income-tax Rules, 2026, as Form No. 56 with the designation in Form No. 57, keeping the same ₹500 crore / ₹50 crore / ₹10 crore tests and the thirty-day designation window.
  • Country-by-country reporting (Form 3CEAC, 3CEAD, 3CEAE) has different dates and is not covered here.
  • The sheet holds group financial data: share it only inside your firm.
