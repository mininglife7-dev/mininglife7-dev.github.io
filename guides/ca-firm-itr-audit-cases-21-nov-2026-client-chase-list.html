ITR chase list for CA firms: tax-audit clients file by 21 November 2026 (AY 2026-27)

Updated 20 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 20 Oct 2026): For AY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26), CBDT Circular No. 07/2026 dated 28 September 2026 extended the income-tax return due date for persons at Sl. No. 2 of the table below Explanation 2 to section 139(1) of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (broadly, companies and others whose accounts must be audited, and partners of such firms) from 31 October to Saturday 21 November 2026. The tax audit report date moved to Wednesday 21 October 2026. Transfer-pricing (s.92E) cases are not covered by the circular. Sources: Circular 07/2026 and the PIB release of 28 Sep 2026. This is a starting point, not tax advice. Confirm on the Income-tax e-filing portal before you file.

What you get from this guide: a finished four-week chase plan for your audit clients, from audit report day (21 Oct) to every return filed and verified by Wednesday 18 November 2026, three days before the Saturday due date. Sort every audit client into one of three groups: report filed and accepted (start the return now), report uploaded but not accepted by the client (chase the acceptance today), and report not filed by 21 Oct (escalate to the partner). The one-row-per-client sheet, the collect list, the elections that must go in by the due date, and short client scripts are below. Copy them into your own sheet.

Why "file by 18 Nov" and not "by 21 Nov": 21 November is a Saturday. Partners' returns need the firm's final figures first, signatories travel, DSCs expire, and the portal is busiest in the last days. Treat any later extension as a bonus, not a plan.

What a late return costs (no amounts beyond the Act): a fee under s.234F of ₹5,000, or ₹1,000 where total income does not exceed ₹5 lakh (incometaxindia.gov.in); interest under s.234A on tax still unpaid; and business and capital losses of the year cannot be carried forward (s.80 read with s.139(3)). If a return is uploaded on time but verified after 30 days, the verification date counts as the filing date and the late-filing consequences follow (ITR-V FAQs).

If a client's audit report was not filed by 21 October, the partner handling the file should speak to the client the same day; the return also needs the audit details, so a late report delays the ITR. Penalty exposure for a late audit report sits in the Act (s.271B); check the current text before you quote anything to a client.

Collect list for each audit client

  • Audit report acknowledgement number and proof the taxpayer accepted it on the portal
  • Audited financial statements and the final tax computation inputs (disallowances, depreciation, deductions claimed)
  • Form 26AS, AIS and TIS downloaded after the audit report date, with any mismatch explained
  • Advance tax challans for FY 2025-26 and any self-assessment tax already paid
  • Brought-forward losses and unabsorbed depreciation from last year's return
  • For partners: remuneration, interest on capital and share of profit from the audited firm
  • Regime choice in writing; Form 10-IEA where an individual or HUF with business income opts out of, or re-enters, the default regime
  • Companies exercising a s.115BAA or s.115BAB option for the first time: the option form (10-IC / 10-ID) is due by the s.139(1) due date; check the official source
  • Foreign income with tax credit claimed: Form 67, due on or before the s.139(1) due date (e-filing portal)
  • Bank account for refund pre-validated on the portal
  • Signatory's DSC valid and registered on the portal (mandatory for companies)
  • Pending notices or outstanding demands on the portal, so the return does not contradict a reply already given

WhatsApp / email script pack

Accept the audit report: "Hello. We have uploaded your tax audit report for FY 2025-26. Please log in to the income-tax portal and accept it today; the last date is 21 October. Reply 'done' once accepted."

Start the return: "Your income-tax return for FY 2025-26 is due on 21 November (a Saturday). We plan to file by 18 November. Please send [list] by [date]."

Mismatch list: "Form 26AS/AIS shows [n] items that do not match your books (list attached). Please confirm or send the supporting document for each by [date], so the return matches the department's data."

Tax to pay: "Self-assessment tax of ₹[amount] is payable before we file. Please pay it by [date] using the challan details attached and send us the challan copy."

Sign-off: "Your return for FY 2025-26 is ready. One-page summary attached. Please reply 'OK to file' by [date], and keep your DSC (or Aadhaar-linked mobile for OTP) ready so we can file and verify the same day."
