Short answer (checked 23 Oct 2026): the CGST Act sets one outer date for correcting a financial year's returns. Under the proviso to section 37(3) (details of outward supplies, i.e. GSTR-1/IFF) and the proviso to section 39(9) (returns, i.e. GSTR-3B), no rectification is allowed after the thirtieth day of November following the end of the financial year, or the date the relevant annual return is furnished, whichever is earlier. For FY 2025-26 that is Monday 30 November 2026, or earlier if the client files its FY 2025-26 GSTR-9 before then. This is a starting point, not tax advice.

What the law and the portal say

  • GSTR-1 / IFF (section 37(3)). A registered person who discovers an error or omission in the details furnished "shall ... rectify such error or omission in such manner as may be prescribed, and shall pay the tax and interest, if any, in case there is a short payment of tax". Proviso: no rectification "after the thirtieth day of November following the end of the financial year to which such details pertain, or furnishing of the relevant annual return, whichever is earlier" (CBIC section 37 page).
  • GSTR-3B (section 39(9)). An omission or incorrect particulars discovered "other than as a result of scrutiny, audit, inspection or enforcement activity by the tax authorities" is rectified in a later return, "subject to payment of interest under this Act", with the same 30 November / annual return cut-off (CBIC section 39 page). The 30 November date applies from 1 October 2022 (Finance Act 2022, notified by Notification No. 18/2022-CT).
  • The GST portal's own example. The GSTR-1 FAQ says rectification of invoices of the previous financial year "will not be allowed after 30th November of the following financial year", and gives the example: for FY 2022-23 the cut-off "for amendment or for adding any missed invoice" was 30-Nov-2023. Same logic: FY 2025-26 → 30-Nov-2026.
  • GSTR-1A fixes the current period only. The same FAQ: GSTR-1A amends a GSTR-1 of the same tax period, opens after GSTR-1 is filed (or its due date, if later), can be filed once per period and only before that period's GSTR-3B; its changes auto-populate GSTR-3B.
  • GSTR-3B liability is fixed by GSTR-1. GSTN advisories (2025) made the auto-populated liability in GSTR-3B non-editable from the July 2025 tax period; corrections go through GSTR-1A before filing GSTR-3B, or through GSTR-1/IFF of later periods. So most output-tax mistakes for FY 2025-26 are fixed in a GSTR-1 or IFF you file by 30 November 2026.
