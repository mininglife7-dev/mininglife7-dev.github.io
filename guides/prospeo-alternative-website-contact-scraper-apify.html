Short answer: They answer different questions. Prospeo is a B2B contact database and enrichment tool: you search people and companies with filters (28 to 41 depending on plan), find verified work emails and direct mobile numbers, and sync to a CRM. Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan with 100 credits a month and paid plans per user: Starter $49 a month (2,000 credits), Growth $99 (5,000) and Pro $249 (15,000), or 25% off billed yearly ($441, $888 and $2,241 a year with all credits upfront). One verified email costs 1 credit, a mobile number 10 credits, and credits do not roll over. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify is not a database: it reads each company's own website and returns the role emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers and social links that site publishes, with the page each came from. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need named decision-makers by job title, pick Prospeo. If you already have a list of company websites and want what each business publishes, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I have a list of 400 small-business websites from Google Maps. I don't need the CFO's mobile number; I need the shop's own email and WhatsApp so I can ask one simple question.” A contact database starts from people and filters. A website contact scraper starts from the websites you already have. Pick by where your list starts.

Who should pick Prospeo instead

  • You sell to named roles (heads of sales, founders, HR managers) and need their verified work email or mobile.
  • You build lists from filters, not from websites you already have.
  • Your team wants CRM sync, job-change tracking and intent topics in one account.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have the websites (from Google Maps, a directory or a trade fair list) and want each business's own published contacts.
  • You need phones and WhatsApp numbers that small businesses put on their own sites.
  • You want the source page for every detail, which makes it easier to explain where you got it under India's DPDP Act or the GDPR.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Prospeo cost?

Its pricing page (checked 25 Oct 2026) shows a Free plan with 100 credits a month, and per-user plans: Starter $49 a month with 2,000 credits, Growth $99 with 5,000 and Pro $249 with 15,000. Billed yearly they are $441, $888 and $2,241 per user, with all yearly credits granted upfront.

How do Prospeo credits work?

Per its FAQ: one verified business email costs 1 credit, a direct mobile number 10 credits, and a company export 1 credit. People exports cost 0 when no email is found. Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over; add-ons start at 1,000 credits for $10.

Does Prospeo have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan gives 100 credits a month with no time limit, 28 search filters and the Chrome extension, with a 25-row selection limit.

Does the Unadhi Actor search for people by job title?

No. It has no people database. It reads the websites you give it and reports the emails, phones and links each company publishes, with role inboxes only unless you turn personal addresses on.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
