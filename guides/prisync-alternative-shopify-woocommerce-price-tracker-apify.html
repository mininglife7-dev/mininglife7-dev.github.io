Who our Actor fits better

  • You need a snapshot of a few competitors' whole catalogues (price, sale price, stock, SKU/GTIN) before a sale or a range review.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Prisync cost?

Prisync's pricing page (read 20 Oct 2026) lists URL-based monitoring at 99 a month for up to 100 products, 199 for up to 1,000 and 399 for up to 5,000, with a 14-day free trial. Channel-based plans start at 199 and hybrid at 299 a month; API access costs an extra 20%.

Does the Unadhi Actor send price-change alerts?

No. It returns a dataset per run. You can schedule runs with Apify Schedules and compare datasets in a sheet. If you need alerts and history out of the box, a monitoring service like Prisync does that.

Can the Actor track Amazon or Flipkart prices?

No. It refuses marketplaces by design and only reads independent shops whose robots.txt and terms allow it.

What does a run cost?

No Actor fee during launch; you pay Apify platform usage. We do not quote a cost per 1,000 products because it depends on the shops; run three shops first and read the usage in Apify billing.

Not legal advice. Check each shop's terms before you collect its data; the Actor skips shops whose robots.txt or terms forbid it.
