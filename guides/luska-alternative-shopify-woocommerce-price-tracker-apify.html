Short answer: Luska is pricing software that reads competitor prices, models the margin effect and recommends a price for each product, with an approval step and a record of every change. Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €149 a month (100 products, 3 competitors each, daily price and stock monitoring, alerts, pricing calendar, CSV export, 14 days of history, no recommendations), Professional at €449 a month (1,000 products, 20 competitors each, AI price recommendations, margin guardrails, opt-in autopilot, Shopify price push, 60 days of history) and Business at €1,290 a month (5,000 products, 50 competitors each, REST API, 180 days of history). Enterprise starts from €30,000 a year. Yearly billing is ten times the monthly price, and every new workspace gets a 14-day Professional trial with no card. Its FAQ says WooCommerce stores import products by CSV, with WooCommerce sync and price push coming soon, and data is hosted in the EU. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify only reads competitor shops' public product and price data into rows for your sheet. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you want recommended prices with reasons and approvals, Luska is built for that. If you need a cheap competitor price export now and then, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Our finance lead wants to know why each price was set, and who signed it off.” That is a pricing-governance job. “I just need what four competitor shops charge this week, in a sheet” is an export job.

Who should pick Luska instead

  • You want recommended prices with reasons, margin floors and an approval queue before anything changes.
  • You run Shopify and want prices pushed back to the shop after sign-off.
  • You need a record of who changed which price, when and on what evidence.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want competitor prices from Shopify and WooCommerce shops without a €149-a-month plan.
  • You need whole catalogues, not a 100-product watchlist with three competitors each.
  • You make pricing decisions yourself in a spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Luska cost?

Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) lists Starter at €149 a month, Professional at €449 and Business at €1,290, with Enterprise from €30,000 a year. Yearly billing costs ten times the monthly price.

Does Luska's Starter plan include price recommendations?

No. Its pricing page says recommendations are not included in Starter; they start on Professional.

Does the Unadhi Actor recommend prices?

No. It returns competitor product and price rows for each run; the decisions stay with you.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 10 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
