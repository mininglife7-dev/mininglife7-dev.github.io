Short answer: UpLead is a B2B contact database: you search by filters (industry, revenue, technologies and more) and spend one credit to unlock a contact's email and mobile direct dial. Its pricing page (checked 22 Oct 2026) shows a 7-day free trial with 5 credits, Essentials at $99 a month (170 credits) and Plus at $199 a month (400 credits) on monthly billing; the annual view shows Essentials at $74 a month (2,040 credits) and Plus at $149 a month. Professional is annual only, by quote. Free, Essentials and Plus are single-user. If you need named decision-makers found by filters, UpLead is the better pick. If you already have a list of business websites and want the contact details each one publishes, one row per site, our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does that, is free during launch, and you pay only Apify platform usage.

The pain this page is about

“I already have the list: 300 businesses from Google Maps or a trade directory. I just need a way to contact each one.” A database priced per unlocked person is built for finding people you do not yet know about. When the companies are already chosen, the contact page on each one's own website is often enough.

Who should pick UpLead instead

  • You do not have a list yet and want to build one by filters such as industry, revenue or the technologies a company uses.
  • You need named decision-makers with mobile direct dials, exported to a CRM.
  • You want enrichment, suppression lists or intent data in the same tool.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have the businesses (Google Maps, a directory, a dealer or member list) and want what each website publishes.
  • Your list is Indian SMEs, clinics, shops or dealers where WhatsApp and the front-desk phone matter more than a manager's email.
  • You want role inboxes only by default, robots.txt respected, and sites whose terms forbid scraping skipped for free.

Frequently asked questions

Does UpLead have a free plan?

Its pricing page (checked 22 Oct 2026) shows a free trial at $0 for 7 days with 5 credits, not a permanent free plan.

What is an UpLead credit?

Per its pricing page, one credit unlocks one contact for download or CRM export, with access to that contact's email and mobile direct dial.

How much is UpLead Essentials?

$99 a month with 170 credits on monthly billing. The annual view of the same page shows $74 a month with 2,040 credits.

Can the Unadhi Actor search for companies like UpLead?

No. It does not have a company database or filters. You give it the websites, for example from a Google Maps run, and it reads what each site publishes.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
