Short answer: Societeinfo is a French B2B data service: search engines and enrichment over French company legal and financial data, contacts, web data and company events, with an API, Zapier/Make and webhooks. Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) charges by credits (1 credit per contact or company exported or enriched). On annual billing it lists Starter at €39 a month (€468 a year, 3,600 credits a year, 1 user), Pro at €99 (€1,188, 12,000 credits), Growth at €199 (€2,388, 36,000 credits, 2 users) and Scale at €299 (€3,588, 72,000 credits, 3 users). On monthly billing (excl. VAT) Starter is €59 (300 credits a month), Pro €159 (1,000), Growth €319 (3,000) and Scale €449 (6,000). A second price block lower on the same page shows Starter at €32 a month (€384 a year) and Pro at €79 (€948 a year); we could not tell which applies, so ask them. Pay-as-you-go credits that do not expire are also offered. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a narrower job: give it company website addresses and it reads their public pages for role emails, phone numbers and WhatsApp links, returning rows with the page each was found on. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It holds no company database and no French register data. If you want to search and filter French companies and enrich them with legal and contact data, Societeinfo is built for that. If you already have a list of websites and want the contacts those sites publish, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Find me every French company with 10 to 50 staff in this sector, with its SIREN and a contact.” That is a database search job. “I have 300 company websites; which email and phone does each one publish?” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick Societeinfo instead

  • You need to find French companies by sector, size or region, not just read sites you already know.
  • You want legal and financial data (company identifiers, accounts, events) next to contacts.
  • You want company watch lists and CRM automation via Zapier, Make or webhooks.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have a list of websites and want the contacts they publish.
  • You want each row with the source page, so you can show where a contact came from.
  • You need companies outside France, or you do not want a credit subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Societeinfo cost?

Its pricing page (checked 17 Nov 2026) lists annual plans from €39 a month (Starter, €468 a year) to €299 (Scale), and monthly plans from €59 to €449 excluding VAT. A second block on the same page shows Starter at €32 a month (€384 a year) and Pro at €79 (€948 a year), so confirm with them.

What is a Societeinfo credit?

Its page says one credit covers exporting or enriching about one contact or company. Subscription credits reset at the end of each month or year; pay-as-you-go credits do not expire.

Does the Unadhi Actor include French company register data?

No. It only reads the public pages of the websites you give it.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 17 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
