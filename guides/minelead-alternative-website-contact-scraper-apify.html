Short answer: Minelead is an email-finding service: company email search by domain, an email finder from full names, a lead finder by keyword and location, email finders for Google Maps, YouTube and Twitter, an email verifier, an API and a browser extension. Its pricing page (checked 29 Oct 2026) shows a free plan with 25 credits a month and no card, then Starter at $39 a month (1,000 credits), Pro $69 (10,000), Business $149 (50,000) and Enterprise $299 (200,000) billed monthly, with up to 29% off on annual billing; one credit is one successful domain search, one verification or one finder operation. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a narrower job: it visits each website in your list and returns the inboxes, phone numbers and WhatsApp links that business publishes, with the source page for each. There is no Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage, and Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month. If you need named people's emails, verification and outreach campaigns, Minelead does more. If you have a list of business websites and want their published contacts, including phones and WhatsApp, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“I have 200 web shops and agencies in Germany and the Netherlands from a directory. I need the contact address and phone each one shows on its own site, and where I found it.” That is a website-reading job. Finding a named manager's email is an email-finder job.

Who should pick Minelead instead

  • You know the names and need each person's work email, or you want emails verified.
  • You want a lead list built from keywords and locations, plus email campaigns, in one account.
  • You want a browser extension and CRM integrations, with several users on one plan.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You start from business websites: a directory, a member list or a Google Maps export.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp as well as email, with the page each one came from.
  • You prefer shared role inboxes by default, which is easier to handle under GDPR than named people's addresses.

Frequently asked questions

Is Minelead free?

Its pricing page (checked 29 Oct 2026) shows a free plan with 25 credits a month and no credit card. Paid plans start with Starter at $39 a month for 1,000 credits.

What is a Minelead credit?

Its FAQ says one credit is one successful domain search, one email verification, one finder operation, one successful generator request or one campaign email sent.

Can the Unadhi Actor find a specific person's email from their name?

No. It does not guess or construct addresses. It returns what each company's website publishes and, by default, keeps only shared role inboxes.

Can I use both?

Yes. Scrape the websites for the general inbox, phone and WhatsApp first, then use an email finder only for the few named decision-makers you need.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
