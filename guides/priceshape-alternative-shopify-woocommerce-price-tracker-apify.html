Short answer: PriceShape, based in Aarhus, Denmark, is a price-intelligence platform for retailers and brands: it connects your Google Shopping and webshop product feeds, monitors competitors' or resellers' prices across retailers and markets, offers an AI pricing analyst, and gives you a dedicated Customer Success Manager with a three-step onboarding. Its pricing page (checked 28 Oct 2026) shows Growth starting from €599 a month, with the final price depending on your assortment and markets, a 14-day free trial with no card, and custom-priced Enterprise. Our Shopify & WooCommerce Product + Price Tracker on Apify is a much smaller tool: you list competitor shop URLs and it exports each shop's public catalogue (variants, price, compare-at price, stock, SKU/GTIN, images, URLs) with a per-shop summary. It has no Actor fee during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage, and you do the comparison yourself in a spreadsheet. If you are a retailer or brand with a large assortment across markets and want matched products, Google Shopping insight and a support team, PriceShape does far more. If you are a small shop watching a handful of Shopify or WooCommerce competitors, try ours first.

The pain this page is about

“We sell 300 products in one country. Four competitors are on Shopify or WooCommerce. €599 a month is more than our margin on the whole range; we just need their prices in a sheet each week.” A managed platform matches products and advises on pricing. A catalogue export gives you the raw numbers to compare yourself.

Who should pick PriceShape instead

  • You are a retailer or brand with a large assortment across several markets.
  • You want products matched to your own feed, Google Shopping insight and reseller monitoring.
  • You want a dedicated success manager and onboarding rather than doing it yourself.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You are a small shop watching a handful of Shopify or WooCommerce competitors.
  • You are happy to compare prices in a spreadsheet and want the raw catalogue, weekly.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PriceShape cost?

Its pricing page (checked 28 Oct 2026) says pricing starts at €599 a month for Growth, with the final cost depending on the number of products you track and the markets you operate in. Enterprise is custom-priced. Both show a 14-day free trial with no credit card.

Does PriceShape need my product feed?

Its page says it connects your Google Shopping feed and webshop product feed in the first days, and that a product feed with your own data is needed to use the platform fully.

Does the Unadhi Actor match my products to competitors?

No. It exports each competitor shop's public catalogue; you match products and compare prices yourself, for example in a spreadsheet.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Which shops does the Unadhi Actor read?

Shopify and WooCommerce shops with public catalogues. Marketplaces and other platforms are not covered.
