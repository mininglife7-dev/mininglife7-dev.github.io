Could children use your AI chatbot or app? A GDPR Article 8 child consent and age check sheet for small businesses

Updated 26 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the EURO AI Register mentioned below is an Unadhi product with a free edition. This guide is for information only, not legal advice.

What you get: a finished child consent sheet in three tabs: a "does Article 8 apply?" test with one row per AI chatbot, app or sign-up form and a result formula, a country age tab you fill in from each country's national law, and a parental consent record with a status formula, plus AI Act red flags and two made-up example rows.

Short answer: if you rely on consent and offer an online service directly to children, for example a homework helper chatbot, a kids' coding class app or a game with an AI character, Article 8 GDPR says a child can only consent alone from 16 (or a lower national age, never below 13). Below that age you need consent given or authorised by a parent, and you must make reasonable efforts to verify it.

Recital 38 says the specific protection of children should apply in particular to marketing, to creating personality or user profiles, and to collecting data through services offered directly to a child, so look closely at any AI feature that profiles users.

AI Act red flags for services children use

  • Article 5(1)(b) of the EU AI Act bans AI systems that exploit vulnerabilities due to age to materially distort behaviour in a way that causes, or is reasonably likely to cause, significant harm. Remove any AI feature that pushes children into purchases or streaks by playing on their age
  • Write the privacy notice so a child can understand it: Article 12(1) GDPR asks for clear and plain language, in particular for information addressed specifically to a child. See the privacy notice wording sheet
  • If your chatbot talks to children, tell them they are talking to an AI: see the Article 50 chatbot disclosure checklist
  • Profiling children for marketing is the area Recital 38 names first. Run the DPIA screening sheet before you launch
