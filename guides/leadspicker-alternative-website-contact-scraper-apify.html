Leadspicker or Unadhi's contact scraper? A lead database with signals and outreach sequences vs a pay-per-use scraper for public website contacts

Competitor facts below come only from their own public pages, read on 18 Nov 2026; each is linked in Sources. We did not log in, start trials or read paywalled material. No ratings, no reviews, no scores of our own.

Partner programme opening soon: join the free waitlist. Planned terms are 50% of each referred sale (75% once you have 5+ paying customers), single level, paid through Gumroad only from revenue received.

Short answer: Leadspicker is a sales prospecting platform with a lead database, signal tracking, email finding and LinkedIn plus email sequences, priced per company rather than per user. Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists Explorer at €199 a month (4,000 credits a month, 5 LinkedIn accounts, 10 mailboxes, 5 routines), Growth at €479 (16,000 credits, 50 LinkedIn accounts, 100 mailboxes, 10 team logins, API) and Scale at €1,599 (80,000 credits, 500 LinkedIn accounts, 1,000 mailboxes), each “billed annually · per company”, with Enterprise on request and a 7-day trial with no card. One credit is one euro cent; credits pay for verified emails, phone numbers (50 credits, €0.50, per its table), enrichments and AI. The page has a monthly-billing switch, but the monthly prices were not in the text we read. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a much smaller job: you give it company website addresses and it reads their public pages for role emails, phone numbers and WhatsApp links, returning each with the page it came from. It does not find named people or send messages. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you want a full outbound engine (database, signals, sequences) for a sales team, Leadspicker is built for that. If you need the published contacts of a list of websites, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Find decision-makers at companies that just raised money and run a LinkedIn and email sequence.” That is an outbound-platform job. “What general contact address does each of these 200 websites publish?” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick Leadspicker instead

  • You want named decision-makers with verified emails and phones.
  • You want signals and sequences (LinkedIn and email) in one tool for a sales team.
  • You prefer one company price over per-seat pricing.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You need only the contacts a website publishes, with the source page.
  • You do not want an annual plan for a one-off list.
  • You already work in Apify or a spreadsheet and send email yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Leadspicker cost?

Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists Explorer at €199 a month, Growth at €479 and Scale at €1,599, billed annually per company, with more credits available at extra cost and Enterprise on request.

What is a Leadspicker credit worth?

Its page says one credit is always one euro cent; credits pay for actions such as verified emails, phone numbers, enrichments and AI.

Does the Unadhi Actor find named people or send emails?

No. It returns the contact details published on the websites you list. What you do with them is up to you, under the GDPR.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 18 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
