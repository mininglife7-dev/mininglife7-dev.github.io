Registering a One Person Company on MCA V3: 8 mistakes that get SPICe+ sent back for resubmission

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Only a natural person who is an Indian citizen and resident in India (stayed at least 120 days in the previous financial year) can form a One Person Company or be its nominee, and a person can form only one OPC (Rule 3, Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014). You file SPICe+ (INC-32) with its linked forms (e-MoA, e-AoA, AGILE-PRO-S) and the nominee's written consent in INC-3. Most resubmissions come from avoidable document and name errors, listed below.

The 8 mistakes

  1. Name that is too close to an existing company or trademark, or a name that does not match the main object. Check the MCA name search and the trademark register before Part A.
  2. Name without "(OPC)". OPC names end in "(OPC) Private Limited", and the words "One Person Company" must appear in brackets below the name wherever it is printed (section 12(3)).
  3. Nominee consent missing or unsigned (INC-3), or a nominee who is not an Indian citizen resident in India.
  4. Old utility bill for the registered office; it should not be older than two months.
  5. No NOC from the property owner, or a rent agreement in a different name from the NOC.
  6. Name mismatch between PAN, Aadhaar and the DSC (initials vs full name, spelling).
  7. Vague main objects in the e-MoA ("all types of business"); write the actual activity.
  8. Wrong or missing attachments in AGILE-PRO-S (GST, EPFO, ESIC, bank details) that do not match SPICe+.

Before you file: who qualifies

RuleWhat it means
Member and nomineeNatural person, Indian citizen, resident in India (120 days in the previous FY)
One OPC per personYou cannot form more than one OPC or be nominee in more than one
MinorsCannot be member or nominee
Restricted activitiesAn OPC cannot carry on non-banking financial investment activity or be a section 8 company

Since 1 April 2021 the paid-up capital and turnover limits that forced an OPC to convert were removed; an OPC can convert to a private company by choice.

Checklist

  • DSC of the subscriber and director
  • Name checked on MCA and trademark search
  • INC-3 signed by the nominee with PAN and address proof
  • Utility bill not older than two months + owner's NOC
  • e-MoA objects specific; AGILE-PRO-S details consistent

Frequently asked questions

Who can form a One Person Company in India?

A natural person who is an Indian citizen and resident in India, meaning stayed at least 120 days in the previous financial year (Rule 3, Companies (Incorporation) Rules 2014).

Can I form two OPCs?

No. A person can incorporate only one OPC and be nominee in only one OPC.

Which form records the nominee's consent?

Form INC-3, filed with SPICe+ at incorporation.

Is an OPC forced to convert after crossing a turnover?

Not since 1 April 2021, when the capital and turnover limits for compulsory conversion were removed.
