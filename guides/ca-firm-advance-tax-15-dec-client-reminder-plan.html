Advance tax by 15 Dec 2026: a client reminder plan for CA firms

Updated 14 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer: the third advance tax instalment for tax year 2026-27 falls on 15 December 2026. Under the usual schedule, about 75% of the year's advance tax should be paid cumulatively by then (re-check the portal FAQ). Start collecting April–November figures from clients in mid-November, send your estimate by early December, and get a written "OK to pay" before the 15th. Presumptive clients are usually a 15 March job, so take them off the December list.

Confirm the rules on the portal first

  • New Act: tax year 2026-27 instalments are governed by the Income-tax Act, 2025, so section numbers in your old templates may be out of date. The advance tax due dates guide lists the four dates and the portal FAQ references.
  • Threshold: advance tax applies when the year's tax after TDS/TCS is ₹10,000 or more.
  • Challan year: during the 1961-to-2025 transition, clients paying on their own can pick the wrong year on the payment screen. Tell them which year to select, or pay with them on a call.

Sort your client list into four groups

GroupWhoDecember action
A. Pay by 15 DecBusiness, profession or capital-gains clients above the threshold, not presumptiveFull estimate and reminder rhythm below
B. Salaried with side incomeRent, interest, freelance or capital gains not covered by employer TDSShort estimate; ask for the side-income figures only
C. PresumptiveClients on the presumptive schemeNo December instalment as a rule; diarise 15 March
D. Below thresholdTDS already covers the tax, or tax under ₹10,000One line on file saying why no payment is needed

What to collect for the estimate

  • April–November profit figure (or books up to 30 Nov) and expected December–March trend
  • Capital gains booked since April (broker P&L statement) and any big sale planned before March
  • Rent, interest and other income not in the books
  • TDS and TCS credit so far (Form 26AS / AIS view) and expected TDS for the rest of the year
  • Advance tax already paid on 15 Jun and 15 Sep (challan details)
  • Who on your team owns the client, and the channel they answer on (email, WhatsApp or call)

The 15th of December 2026 is a Tuesday. Bank and portal load goes up on the due date, so aim to finish payments by the 13th.

Message templates

Mid-November: "Hello, the next advance tax instalment is due 15 December. To work out your amount, please send your April–November figures, any capital gains since April, and other income outside the books by 28 November."

Early December: "Based on what you sent, we estimate ₹[amount] for the 15 December instalment. Here is how we got it: [two lines]. Reply YES and we'll guide the payment, or send corrections by 10 December."

Don't quote interest amounts unless you have worked them out for that client under the current rules.
