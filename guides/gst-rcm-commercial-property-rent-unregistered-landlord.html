Renting a shop or office from an unregistered landlord? GST reverse charge, the self-invoice and the ITC

Last updated 21 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. Since 10 October 2024 (Notification 09/2024-Central Tax (Rate)), when an unregistered person rents out any property other than a residential dwelling (shop, office, godown) to a GST-registered person, the tenant pays the GST (18%) under reverse charge. From 16 January 2025 tenants registered under the composition scheme were taken out of this rule. The tenant raises a self-invoice within 30 days (Rule 47A), pays the tax in cash in GSTR-3B and can usually take the same amount back as ITC.

Does it apply to you?

LandlordTenantWho pays GST on rent
RegisteredAnyoneLandlord charges GST on the invoice (forward charge)
UnregisteredRegistered (regular)Tenant, under reverse charge
UnregisteredRegistered (composition)Not covered from 16 Jan 2025
UnregisteredUnregisteredNo GST

Residential dwellings follow a separate rule: renting a residential dwelling to a registered person has been under reverse charge since 18 July 2022, with an exception for a proprietor who rents it in a personal capacity for living.

Monthly steps for the tenant

  1. Raise a self-invoice for the rent within 30 days of the supply (Rule 47A).
  2. Report it in GSTR-3B table 3.1(d) (inward supplies liable to reverse charge).
  3. Pay the tax in cash through the electronic cash ledger; ITC cannot be used to pay reverse-charge tax.
  4. Claim ITC of the same amount in table 4A(3) if the premises are used for business and no blocking rule applies.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming "landlord not registered" means "no GST". The liability moved to you.
  • Setting the RCM off against ITC. Reverse-charge tax must be paid in cash first.
  • No self-invoice. Without it, the ITC claim is weak in an audit.
  • Forgetting maintenance or parking charges billed with the rent; they usually follow the rent.
  • Ignoring interest. Late reverse-charge payment carries interest under section 50 even if the ITC is equal.

Checklist

  • Landlord's GST status checked on the GST portal
  • Property is not a residential dwelling
  • You are a regular (not composition) taxpayer
  • Self-invoice raised within 30 days
  • RCM paid in cash in GSTR-3B, ITC claimed in 4A(3)

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay GST if my shop owner is not registered under GST?

If you are a regular GST-registered tenant, yes: from 10 Oct 2024 you pay 18% GST on the rent under reverse charge (Notification 09/2024-CT(R)).

Does RCM on rent apply to composition dealers?

No. From 16 January 2025 composition taxpayers were excluded from this reverse-charge entry.

Can I take ITC of GST paid on rent under RCM?

Generally yes, in the same GSTR-3B, if the premises are used for business and no blocked-credit rule applies.

What is the time limit for the self-invoice?

Rule 47A of the CGST Rules requires the self-invoice within 30 days of receiving the supply from an unregistered person.
