Telugu Barasala invitation wording (బారసాల ఆహ్వానం), with English meanings and a WhatsApp version

Last updated 11 Oct 2026 · Telugu speakers everywhere

Short answer. Barasala (బారసాల, from బాలసారె) is the Telugu naming and cradle ceremony, held on a muhurtham fixed by the family priest, traditionally on the 21st day after birth (some families choose the 11th or 16th day or later). An invitation usually opens with a blessing (శ్రీరస్తు శుభమస్తు అవిఘ్నమస్తు), names the occasion (నామకరణ మహోత్సవం / బారసాల), gives the date, muhurtham and address, invites guests సకుటుంబ సపరివార సమేతంగా, and closes with the parents' or grandparents' names. Wording differs by family, so please have an elder check it.

Why the wording matters

Grandparents and elders read the card for the blessing, the muhurtham and how the family is named. Relatives abroad often join by video, so the time and a link matter as much as the Telugu.

A short WhatsApp version for relatives abroad

మా చిన్నారి బారసాల [తేదీ] న [సమయం] (భారత కాలమానం) కు జరుగుతోంది. రాలేకపోతే ఈ లింక్ ద్వారా చూసి ఆశీర్వదించండి: [లింక్]

English: Our little one's Barasala is on [date] at [time] IST. If you can't come, please watch here and send your blessings: [link]. Add the time in their zone too.

Common mistakes

  • Printing the baby's name when the family wants it revealed at the ceremony. Many families announce the name only during the naming ritual; ask first.
  • Muhurtham without the full date and time. Write the weekday, date and exact time, as fixed by the priest.
  • Leaving out grandparents. In many families they are named as hosts; check who should appear and in what order.
  • Broken Telugu letters. Use a Telugu font such as Noto Sans Telugu and check every word on a phone.
  • Copying a wedding card. Wedding honorifics (చి॥, చి॥ల॥సౌ॥) are not used the same way here.

Checklist before sending

  • Date and muhurtham confirmed with the priest
  • Whether to print the baby's name decided
  • Hosts' names and order agreed with elders
  • Venue, landmark and map link
  • Meal time
  • Telugu text read by an elder or fluent reader
  • Livestream link and time zones for relatives abroad

Frequently asked questions

What is Barasala?

The Telugu naming and cradle ceremony for a newborn (బారసాల, from బాలసారె), traditionally held on the 21st day after birth, though families choose other days too.

On which day is Barasala done?

Traditionally the 21st day; some families hold it on the 11th or 16th day or later, on a muhurtham fixed by the priest.

How do I invite relatives abroad to a Barasala?

Send a short Telugu and English message with the time in IST and in their time zone, plus a livestream link.

Should the baby's name be on the invitation?

Ask your elders. Many families reveal the name only during the ceremony.
