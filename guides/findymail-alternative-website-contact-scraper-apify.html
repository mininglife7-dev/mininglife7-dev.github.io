Short answer: They answer different questions. Findymail finds and verifies the work email (and, outside the EU, the mobile number) of a named person, in bulk, through a Chrome extension or by API, and pushes results to your CRM. Its pricing page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows Individual at $99 a month (1 seat), Team $399 (5 seats) and Scale $999 (20 seats), plus Enterprise on request; a credit view on the same page starts at $99 a month for 5,000 finder credits. One email is 1 credit, one phone number 10 credits, you are not charged when nothing is found, and unused credits roll over up to twice the monthly amount. A free account has 10 credits with no card. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify is not a people finder: it reads each company's own website and returns the role emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers and social links that site publishes, with the page each came from. It is free during launch; you pay only Apify platform usage. If you need a specific decision-maker's verified email, pick Findymail. If you have a list of company websites and want what each business publishes, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“I have 400 local firms from a directory. I don't know who the owner is; I just need the email and phone number each firm shows on its own site, with proof of where it came from.” A people finder needs a name and a domain. A website reader only needs the website.

Who should pick Findymail instead

  • You sell to named roles (heads of sales, founders) and need their personal work email, verified.
  • You build lists in LinkedIn or Sales Navigator and want one-click export to a CRM.
  • You want reverse lookup (email to person) or an MCP connection for an AI agent.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You target small local businesses whose websites show a general email, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • You want each contact with the page it was published on, for your own records.
  • You want to test on a few hundred sites for the cost of Apify usage, not a monthly plan.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Findymail cost?

Its pricing page (checked 27 Oct 2026) shows Individual at $99 a month (1 seat), Team at $399 a month (5 seats) and Scale at $999 a month (20 seats), with Enterprise on request. A credit-based view on the same page starts at $99 a month for 5,000 finder credits. A free account includes 10 credits without a card.

How do Findymail credits work?

Its FAQ says one email costs 1 credit and one phone number 10 credits (mobile numbers are for non-EU contacts), you are not charged when no result is found, and unused credits roll over up to twice your monthly amount.

Does the Unadhi Actor find a named person's email?

No. It only reads what each company publishes on its own website, such as info@ or sales@ addresses, phone and WhatsApp numbers, and social links, and it shows the page each came from.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Can I use both?

Yes. Use our Actor for the company-level contacts on each website, and a people finder only for the few named decision-makers you really need.
