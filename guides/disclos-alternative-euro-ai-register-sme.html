The pain this page is about

Two different worries sound alike. A SaaS founder asks: “Does my AI feature need a disclosure banner for EU users?” An SME owner asks: “We use five AI tools at work; what do we write down?” The first is mainly an Article 50 product question. The second is an inventory and Article 4 record-keeping question.

Why this matters now (official dates)

  • Article 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 February 2025 to providers and deployers, whatever the risk level. As replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, it asks organisations to take measures to support the AI literacy of their staff; it does not require them to guarantee a specific level for any individual. The European Commission's Q&A says there is no need for a certificate: an internal record of trainings or guidance is enough, and national authorities supervise and enforce from August 2026 (Commission AI literacy Q&A).
  • Article 50 transparency duties (for example, telling people they are talking to a chatbot) apply from 2 August 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689).
  • High-risk rules were moved by the Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: Annex III uses (such as recruitment and credit scoring) from 2 December 2027, and AI in regulated products (Annex I) from 2 August 2028.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Disclos cost?

Its pricing page (checked 24 Oct 2026) shows free self-serve tools, Monitoring at €49 a month or €540 a year after a free trial, and a €997 one-time audit that includes 12 months of monitoring.

What is in the Disclos audit?

Per its page: a human-written readiness report in five business days, an AI feature inventory and risk classification, an article-by-article gap report, disclosure code in 24 EU languages, model card and policy templates, a short video walkthrough and a 30-day follow-up window.

Is the Disclos audit refund still open?

Its page states the refund window as from audit delivery to 2 August 2026. That date has passed, so ask Disclos what refund terms apply to an audit bought now.

Does EURO AI Register check my website's chatbot disclosure?

No. It records your AI systems, the duties that apply by role and your Article 4 literacy measures. It does not scan websites.

When does Article 50 apply?

Article 50 transparency duties apply from 2 August 2026. After Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, Annex III high-risk rules apply from 2 December 2027 and Annex I from 2 August 2028.
