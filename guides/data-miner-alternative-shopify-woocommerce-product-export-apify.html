For any website, including marketplaces, it is flexible. Who should pick Data Miner instead

  • The site is not Shopify or WooCommerce, or it is a marketplace or directory.
  • You want to see and adjust what you scrape on the page, and reuse community recipes.
  • You scrape a few hundred pages a month and the free plan covers it.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want several Shopify or WooCommerce competitors' whole catalogues with variant-level data, not just what a listing page shows.
  • You want scheduled re-runs before each sale without keeping a browser open.
  • You prefer a tool that respects each shop's robots.txt and terms, and skips shops that refuse it at no charge.

Frequently asked questions

Is Data Miner free?

It has a free plan of 500 pages a month (checked 23 Oct 2026). The pricing page says it is restricted on some domains and that the account is locked if you exceed 500 page scrapes in a month until you upgrade.

Do unused Data Miner credits carry over?

No. Its FAQ says the scrape counter resets to zero at the start of every billing period.

Can the Unadhi Actor scrape any website like Data Miner?

No. It works only on independent Shopify, WooCommerce and schema.org shops and refuses marketplaces by design.

Do I need to build a recipe for the Unadhi Actor?

No. You paste shop URLs; it reads the shop's structured product data.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
