The pain this page is about

Who should pick Apollo.io instead

  • You need to discover decision-makers by job title, industry and company size — not just reach sites you already know.
  • You want sequencing, calling and CRM sync in one place for a sales team.
  • You want a free trial of a full sales platform; Apollo says its free Starter plan stays free with no time limit after the trial.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have the list of businesses (Maps, directory, exhibitor list) and need each one's published contacts.
  • You are an agency delivering contact sheets for a client and want data whose source page is recorded on every row.
  • You want phones and WhatsApp numbers that the business itself put on its website.
  • You do not want a per-seat subscription for a one-off or monthly job.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Apollo.io cost in 2026?

The structured pricing data on apollo.io/pricing (read 14 Oct 2026) lists Free at $0, Basic at $49 a month and Professional at $99 a month, and the plan picker prices plans per seat. A Custom plan is quoted by sales. Annual billing is discounted; check the live page for the annual figure.

Can I resell Apollo data to my clients?

Apollo's pricing FAQ says the plans on that page are for internal business use only, and that powering external products, sharing with customers or reselling requires a separate agreement with custom pricing.

Does the Unadhi Actor search a database of people?

No. It has no database. It reads only the websites you give it and returns the public contact details each site shows, with the page it found them on.

What does the Actor cost?

The Actor charges nothing during launch. You pay Apify platform usage for each run. Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month. Test on a few sites to see your usage before scaling.
