Short answer: Both run on Apify and both read contact details from websites you list. Apify's Contact Details Scraper (vdrmota/contact-info-scraper) is pay-per-event, from $1.05 per 1,000 scraped pages, with no separate platform-usage charge; its page says the Free plan's $5 of credits covers around 2,500 pages. It also offers paid add-ons: business leads enrichment (employees' names, job titles, emails, LinkedIn), email verification and social-profile enrichment. Our Website Contact Scraper is free during launch (you pay only Apify platform usage), returns one merged row per site, turns WhatsApp click-to-chat links into phone numbers, and by default returns only shared role inboxes. If you want named employees and verified personal emails, Apify's Actor is the better pick.

The pain this page is about

You searched the Apify Store for “contact scraper” and found several. The question is not which one is “best”, but which defaults fit your list and your privacy duty.

Who should pick Apify's Contact Details Scraper instead

  • You need named employees with job titles and emails per company (leads enrichment), and you have a lawful basis for that personal data.
  • You want verified lead emails or enriched social profiles in the same run.
  • You prefer a fixed per-page price you can calculate before running, and an Actor maintained by Apify itself.
  • You crawl deep sites or Linktree pages.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You want one clean row per website without a second merge step.
  • Your list is Indian SMEs, clinics, shops or dealers where WhatsApp and phones matter more than email.
  • You want a privacy-first default (role inboxes only) and sites that forbid scraping skipped for free.
  • You are testing and want no Actor fee during launch.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Apify's Contact Details Scraper cost?

Its Store page (checked 21 Oct 2026) shows pay-per-event pricing from $1.05 per 1,000 scraped pages, with no separate platform-usage charge, and says the Free plan's $5 of credits covers around 2,500 pages. Leads enrichment, email verification and social-profile enrichment cost extra.

Is the Unadhi Actor cheaper?

We do not claim that. Ours has no Actor fee during launch, but you pay Apify platform usage, which depends on the sites. Run the same five sites through both and compare the usage in your Apify billing page.

Which one finds more emails?

We have not run a head-to-head test, so we make no claim. Ours returns role inboxes only by default, so it will show fewer personal emails on purpose.

Can I use both?

Yes. Both write an Apify dataset, so you can run one on part of a list and compare the rows by hand before choosing.

Not legal advice. Work emails and phone numbers of identifiable people are personal data under India's DPDP Act 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR; you decide what to do with the output and you stay responsible for it.
