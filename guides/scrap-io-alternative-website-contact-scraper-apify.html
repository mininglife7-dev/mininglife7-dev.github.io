Short answer: Scrap.io extracts businesses from Google Maps by activity and area: name, types, address, phone, website, first email, opening hours and social links on its Essential data, with all emails and reviews on Extended and contact pages, technologies and ad pixels on Complete. Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists monthly plans of Basic $59 (10,000 export credits, city-level search, 3 business types per search), Professional $119 (20,000), Agency $239 (40,000) and Company $599 (100,000, whole country, polygon search). Reverse search and file enrichment (start from phones, emails or websites) are on Agency and Company. A Black Friday 2026 offer halves the yearly plans to $354, $714, $1,434 and $3,594 a year. The page says you enter a card for the trial. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify starts from the other end: you give it company website addresses and it reads their public pages for role emails, phone numbers and WhatsApp links, returning each with the page it came from. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. It does not search Google Maps. If you need a list of every bakery in Lyon with contacts, Scrap.io is built for that. If you already have the websites, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Give me every dental clinic in Bavaria with a phone and email.” That is a discovery job. “I have 300 supplier websites; which contact address does each publish?” is a website-reading job.

Who should pick Scrap.io instead

  • You do not have a list yet and want every business of a type in an area.
  • You want Google Maps fields such as reviews, opening hours and categories.
  • You run regular prospecting and a monthly credit allowance suits you.

Who our Actor fits better

  • You already have the websites (customers, suppliers, a trade-fair list) and need the contacts they publish.
  • You want each contact with its source page.
  • You need a small one-off job without a subscription.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Scrap.io cost?

Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists Basic at $59 a month, Professional $119, Agency $239 and Company $599, with 10,000 to 100,000 export credits a month. A Black Friday 2026 offer lists the yearly plans at $354, $714, $1,434 and $3,594.

Can Scrap.io start from my own list of websites?

Its page lists reverse search and file enrichment, which start from a phone number, email or website, on the Agency and Company plans.

Does the Unadhi Actor search Google Maps?

No. It reads public contact details from the websites you list and nothing else.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 18 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
