Short answer: lobstr.io is a no-code scraper store (79 scrapers) paid in credits. Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026, “prices as of November 18, 2026, in US dollars, before VAT”) lists Free at $0 with 100 credits a month and no card; Starter at $20 a month billed monthly or $16 billed yearly for 10,000 credits; Pro at $100 for 100,000 credits; and Team at $500 for 1,000,000 credits. A credit costs $2 per 1,000 on Starter, $1 on Pro and $0.50 on Team (20% less yearly); most scrapers cost 1 credit per unique result, duplicates are free, options such as email extraction add credits, and a run that ends before its first result costs nothing. Its Google Maps Leads Scraper returns business name, category, phone, website and address, and can extract emails and social links from each business's website with a validation status. The plan cards show “Billed yearly” next to $20, $100 and $500, while the summary gives $20 as the monthly price, so check at checkout. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does one job: you give it company website addresses and it reads their public pages for role emails, phone numbers and WhatsApp links, returning each with the page it came from. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you want to find businesses on Google Maps (or use many other scrapers) from one credit balance, lobstr.io is built for that. If you already have the websites, try ours.

The pain this page is about

“Find me the plumbers in Lisbon and their emails.” That is a discovery job. “Here are 150 websites from our CRM; what contact details do they publish?” is a website-reading job.

Frequently asked questions

How much does lobstr.io cost?

Its pricing page (checked 18 Nov 2026) lists Free at $0 (100 credits a month), Starter at $20 a month billed monthly or $16 billed yearly (10,000 credits), Pro at $100 (100,000 credits) and Team at $500 (1,000,000 credits), in US dollars before VAT.

Does lobstr.io charge for failed runs?

Its page says you pay for the results a run brings back, and a run that ends before its first result costs nothing.

Does the Unadhi Actor search Google Maps?

No. It reads public contact details from the websites you list and nothing else.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch (its Apify pricing data showed no developer fee on 18 Nov 2026). You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.
