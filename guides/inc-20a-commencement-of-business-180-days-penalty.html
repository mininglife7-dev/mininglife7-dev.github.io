INC-20A (commencement of business): the 180-day deadline, the penalty and how CA firms avoid strike-off

Last updated 20 Oct 2026 · India

Short answer. A company with share capital incorporated on or after 2 November 2018 cannot start business or borrow until it files a declaration in Form INC-20A that every subscriber has paid for their shares. It is due within 180 days of incorporation (section 10A, Companies Act 2013). If it is missed, the company faces a penalty of ₹50,000 and each officer in default ₹1,000 per day up to ₹1,00,000, and the Registrar can start striking the company off.

What the form proves

INC-20A is a director's declaration that the subscription money was actually received. Attach proof, usually the company bank statement showing each subscriber's payment. Where the law requires it, a practising CA, CS or CMA certifies the form.

Timeline for a new company

DayAction
0Certificate of incorporation (SPICe+)
Week 1–2Open bank account (often through the SPICe+ linked form)
As soon as funds arriveSubscribers transfer the full subscription amount
Before day 180File INC-20A with the bank statement
Within 30 days of incorporation, if not given at incorporationRegistered office verification (INC-22)

Why it matters beyond the fine

  • No INC-20A means the company may not commence business or exercise borrowing powers.
  • If it is not filed and the Registrar believes the company is not doing business, the ROC can start strike-off under section 248.

Common mistakes

  • Subscribers never transfer the money because "it's our own company". The declaration needs real receipts.
  • Cash deposits with no trail per subscriber. Each subscriber's payment should be traceable.
  • Counting 180 days from bank account opening. The count starts from the incorporation date.
  • Assuming OPCs or small companies are exempt. Section 10A covers companies with share capital, including OPCs. Companies without share capital are outside it.

Checklist for CA firms

  • Incorporation date + 180 days in the client calendar
  • Subscription money received from every subscriber, bank statement saved
  • INC-22 status checked
  • INC-20A filed, SRN and challan saved
  • Client told: no business or borrowing until filed

Frequently asked questions

What is the due date for INC-20A?

Within 180 days from the date of incorporation, for companies with share capital incorporated on or after 2 November 2018.

What is the penalty for not filing INC-20A?

₹50,000 for the company, and ₹1,000 per day for each officer in default up to ₹1,00,000, under section 10A of the Companies Act, 2013.

Does INC-20A apply to a One Person Company?

Yes, if the OPC has share capital, which OPCs registered through SPICe+ normally do.

Can the ROC strike off a company for not filing INC-20A?

Yes. If the declaration is not filed and the Registrar has reasonable cause to believe the company is not carrying on business, strike-off can be started under section 248.
