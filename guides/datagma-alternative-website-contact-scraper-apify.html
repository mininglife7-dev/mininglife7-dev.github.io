Short answer: Datagma is a B2B enrichment tool for finding direct mobile numbers and verified work emails of people. Its pricing page (checked 10 Nov 2026) lists Free at $0 with 100 credits a month (100 emails or 3 phones), Regular at $49 a month or $39 a month billed yearly (3,000 credits, 1 seat), Popular at $99 or $79 (7,500 credits, 3 seats) and Expert at $249 or $209 (22,500 credits, 5 seats); Enterprise is by quote. One verified email is 1 credit and one mobile phone 30 credits; its FAQ says you spend credits only when verified data is found, unused credits roll over for up to 12 months on paid plans, extra seats are $5 a month, and every plan includes API access and a WhatsApp check on numbers. Our Website Contact Scraper on Apify does a different job: it reads each website in your list and returns the inboxes, phones and WhatsApp links the business publishes, with the source page. No Actor fee during launch; Apify usage only. If you need named decision makers' mobiles, Datagma is built for that and has a free tier. If your list is business websites, try our Actor.

The pain this page is about

“I want the mobile of the owner of each of these 50 firms.” That is a person-level finder job. “I want the front-desk phone, WhatsApp and inbox printed on each of these 500 firm websites” is a website-reading job.
