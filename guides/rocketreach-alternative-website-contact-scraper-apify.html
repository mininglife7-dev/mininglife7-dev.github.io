Frequently asked questions

Is RocketReach free?

Its pricing page (read 27 Oct 2026) says a free account gets 5 lookups with no credit card; after that you upgrade to a monthly or annual plan.

Does RocketReach charge for a lookup that finds nothing?

Its FAQ says a lookup is used only if it finds and verifies an email or phone; otherwise it is refunded.

Does the Unadhi Actor find personal emails or mobiles?

No. It reads only what each company publishes on its own website, and shows the page each contact came from.

What does the Unadhi Actor cost?

No Actor fee during launch. You pay Apify only for the platform usage of each run; Apify's free plan includes $5 of usage a month.

Can I use both?

Yes. Use our Actor for company-level contacts across many sites, then a people database for the few named contacts you need.
