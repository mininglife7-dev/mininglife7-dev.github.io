IMS action sheet for CA firms: accept, reject or keep pending every supplier invoice before the October 2026 GSTR-3B (draft GSTR-2B 14 Nov, 3B 20 Nov, FY 2025-26 credit after 30 Nov)

Updated 21 Oct 2026 · Disclosure: the white-label calendar linked below is an Unadhi product (₹999/year).

Short answer (checked 21 Oct 2026): every purchase invoice, debit note and credit note your client's suppliers report in GSTR-1, GSTR-1A or IFF lands in the client's Invoice Management System (IMS) on the GST portal. The client (or you) can Accept, Reject or keep it Pending. Anything left with no action is deemed accepted when the draft GSTR-2B is made on the 14th, and the action freezes when GSTR-3B is filed. For the October 2026 cycle that means: suppliers' GSTR-1 by Wed 11 Nov, draft GSTR-2B on Sat 14 Nov, GSTR-3B by Fri 20 Nov. Invoices of FY 2025-26 still kept pending drop out of IMS once the section 16(4) time limit passes (Mon 30 Nov 2026), so decide them in the October return. This is a starting point, not legal advice. Check the GST portal before you act for a client.

What you get from this guide: a finished IMS action sheet for your firm: one row per supplier document per client, three yes/no checks (in the purchase register, amount matches, goods or services received), and a Google Sheets formula that suggests Accept, Pending or Reject and flags the records with a deadline (FY 2025-26 invoices, credit notes). It comes with the October 2026 dates, a day-by-day plan to 20 November, the rules from the GST portal's own IMS FAQs, and three client messages. Nothing here needs software.

What each action does (from the GST portal's IMS FAQs)

  • Accept: the record goes to "ITC Available" in GSTR-2B and its ITC auto-fills in GSTR-3B.
  • Reject: the record goes to "ITC Rejected" in GSTR-2B and does not flow to GSTR-3B. If the supplier has already filed GSTR-1, the supplier has to correct it through GSTR-1A or a later GSTR-1/IFF; the corrected record comes back to IMS for a fresh action.
  • Pending: the record stays out of this month's GSTR-2B and GSTR-3B and waits in IMS. For invoices it can wait until the section 16(4) time limit, after which it is removed from IMS.
  • No action: deemed accepted at GSTR-2B generation.
  • Credit notes (from the October 2025 tax period): a credit note, an upward amendment of a credit note and some downward amendments can be kept pending for one tax period only (one month for a monthly recipient, one quarter for a quarterly one); after that the pending option is switched off and you must accept or reject. On accepting, the recipient can declare the amount of ITC to be reduced when the ITC was never taken or was taken only in part. If a credit note is rejected, the amount is added back to the supplier's liability in their next GSTR-3B. Check the official source for what happens to a record left without action after the pending period.
  • Not in IMS at all: reverse-charge records, documents from GSTR-5 and GSTR-6, records where ITC is not available because of place-of-supply rules or section 16(4), and rule 37A reversals. These go straight to GSTR-2B.
  • You can change an action as many times as you like before GSTR-3B is filed; the latest one counts and it freezes on filing. If a supplier edits a saved record before filing GSTR-1, your action on it is reset.

Three client messages

1. Purchase register for October (send now): "Please send your October purchase register and the bills you received by 10 November. From 14 November the GST portal treats every supplier bill we have not checked as accepted, so we want to check them against your books before then."

2. Bill you may not recognise: "A supplier, [name], has reported a bill of ₹[amount] dated [date] against your GSTIN. We cannot find it in your books. Is this your purchase? If not, we will reject it on the portal with a remark so the supplier corrects it."

3. Last year's bills still on hold: "These bills from 2025-26 are still on hold on the GST portal: [list]. The last date to claim credit for 2025-26 is 30 November 2026, so we need to decide on them in the October return due 20 November. Please confirm by 13 November whether each one was received and is correct."
