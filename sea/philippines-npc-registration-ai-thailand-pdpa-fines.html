Philippines NPC registration + Thailand PDPA fines: what SEA teams must do

Two live enforcement points: annual NPC registration in the Philippines, and real PDPC fines in Thailand.

What you need to know

  • Philippines: register your DPO and data processing systems with the NPC; renew yearly, 30 days before expiry (Circular 2022-04).
  • Philippines: Advisory 2024-04 says DPA duties apply to AI training and use; keep documentation.
  • Thailand: in Aug 2025 the PDPC announced 8 fines in 5 cases (weak security, late breach reports, processor oversight, missing DPO).
  • Thailand: a draft AI law was consulted on by ETDA in 2025 - not yet law.
