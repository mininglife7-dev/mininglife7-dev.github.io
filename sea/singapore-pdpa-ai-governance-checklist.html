Singapore PDPA + AI governance checklist (2026)

Using AI on customer data in Singapore? Here is what PDPA and IMDA guidance ask for.

What you need to know

  • PDPA breach notification: tell PDPC no later than 3 calendar days after you assess a breach is notifiable.
  • Fines up to 10% of Singapore turnover (if turnover is above S$10m) or S$1m.
  • PDPC's AI advisory guidelines: explain AI use in notices; consider AI Verify for testing.
  • IMDA Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI (Jan 2026, voluntary): bound agent risks, keep humans accountable, log and approve sensitive actions.
  • Start with an inventory of every AI system and the personal data it uses.
