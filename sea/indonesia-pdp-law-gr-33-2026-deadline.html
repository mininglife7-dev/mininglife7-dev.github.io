Indonesia PDP Law: GR 33/2026 applies from 16 January 2027

Indonesia's implementing regulation for the PDP Law is out. You have until about 16 Jan 2027.

What you need to know

  • PDP Law (Law 27/2022) has applied fully since 17 Oct 2024.
  • GR 33/2026 was promulgated 16 Jul 2026 and takes effect six months later (about 16 Jan 2027).
  • It covers records of processing, DPIAs, DPOs, breach response, cross-border transfers and sanctions.
  • The PDP supervisory institution still has to be formally set up; watch for its rules.
