Vietnam Personal Data Protection Law 2026: Decree 356 DPO requirements

Vietnam's PDPL (Law 91/2025) and Decree 356/2025 have applied since 1 January 2026.

What you need to know

  • Decree 356/2025 replaces Decree 13/2023 from 1 Jan 2026.
  • Designate the DPO person or unit in a formal written decision.
  • DPO: college degree or higher, 2+ years relevant experience, personal-data-protection training.
  • Old Decree 13 consents and filed dossiers stay valid; updates must follow the new law.
  • Small enterprises and startups may defer some articles for 5 years (Law Art. 38) - check if you qualify.
