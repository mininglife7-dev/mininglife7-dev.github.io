Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026 · HaanJi — operated by Lalit Kumar D (sole proprietor)

In short

  • Setup fee (₹15,000): full refund if you ask within 14 days of your go-live day. No questions asked.
  • Monthly fee (₹4,000): cancel anytime, with effect from the next monthly cycle. No partial refunds for the current month.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

1. Setup fee: 14-day money-back guarantee

  • The ₹15,000 setup fee is paid upfront.
  • If you are not happy for any reason, ask for a refund within 14 days of your go-live day and we refund the full ₹15,000. You don't need to give a reason, and there are no forms.
  • Go-live day is the first day HaanJi answers your clinic's forwarded calls, as we confirm to you in writing. The 14 days are counted from go-live, not from the day you paid.
  • Before go-live: you may cancel at any time and get a full refund of the setup fee.
  • After the 14-day window ends, the setup fee is non-refundable, because the setup work has been done and accepted.

2. Monthly subscription

  • ₹4,000 per month, paid in advance for each monthly cycle. The first cycle starts on day 15 after go-live. You pay nothing monthly during the first 14 days.
  • Cancel anytime. There is no lock-in and no cancellation fee. A cancellation takes effect from the next billing cycle. The service continues until the end of the cycle you have already paid for, and nothing more is charged after that.
  • We do not give partial or pro-rata refunds for a monthly cycle that has already started.
  • If we end the service ourselves (see Terms), we refund any monthly fee you have paid for a period after the service ends.

3. Payment mistakes

If you pay twice by mistake or pay more than you owe, we refund the extra amount in full, whenever you tell us.

4. How to cancel or ask for a refund

  1. Send a WhatsApp message or call +91 94911 45032, or email mininglife7@gmail.com. A single message is enough, for example "Please cancel and refund my setup fee."
  2. We confirm receipt, normally within 1 working day.
  3. For a guarantee refund, what counts is the date you sent your request. A request sent on day 14 qualifies even if we reply later.

5. How and when refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. For UPI payments, that is the same UPI ID or bank account you paid from. For Razorpay payments (once available), that is the original card, UPI or bank account.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days of your request. Your bank may take a little longer to show the credit.
  • We refund the full amount you paid. We do not deduct any processing fee.
