Rules to know

  • European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882), applying since 28 June 2025
  • EN 301 549 and WCAG 2.1/2.2
  • US ADA Title III case law and the 2024 DOJ Title II web rule (information only; no US cold email)
  • UK Equality Act 2010; Canada ACA and AODA; Japan and Korea disability laws
  • EU AI Act accessibility requirement for AI systems (Article 16(l))
