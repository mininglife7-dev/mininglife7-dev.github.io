California CCPA ADMT rules: the 1 January 2027 checklist for AI in hiring, lending and housing

Checked 13 Oct 2026 against the official sources listed below. Plain-English checklist, not legal advice.

Short answer: businesses covered by the CCPA that use automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT) to make significant decisions (financial or lending services, housing, education, employment or compensation, health care) must comply with the ADMT rules from 1 January 2027: a pre-use notice, opt-out and access rights. Risk-assessment duties began 1 January 2026, with the first submissions to the CPPA due 1 April 2028.

Checklist

  • Confirm you are a CCPA "business" (thresholds in the statute).
  • List tools that replace or substantially replace human decisionmaking for a significant decision. Employees and applicants count as consumers.
  • Draft the pre-use notice (purpose, how it works, rights).
  • Build the opt-out and access-request route, or document the exception you rely on.
  • Run and document a risk assessment for ADMT use; existing processing must be assessed by 31 Dec 2027.
  • Calendar: 1 Jan 2027 (ADMT) and 1 Apr 2028 (first risk-assessment submission).
