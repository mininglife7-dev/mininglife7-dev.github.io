Water connection and water charges check, and name change after buying in Vizag (GVMC), Andhra Pradesh

Official pages read on 22 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: before you buy a house or flat in Visakhapatnam (Vizag), ask the seller for the water assessment number and the connection type, check the water charges on GVMC's water charges search, and get any dues cleared before handover. After registration, ask GVMC to move the connection to your name; the exact process and fee are to verify with GVMC. A water bill in the seller's name is not proof that they own the property.

Why a water bill is not proof of ownership

A water connection shows who pays for the water, not who owns the land or flat. The Hyderabad water board, for example, says in its connection guidelines that sanction "shall not confer any right or evidence to the applicant with regard to ownership of the premises". Whether GVMC's rules say the same is to verify; either way, check title through the sale deed, the encumbrance certificate and a lawyer.

Check it yourself, then change the name

StepWhat the official site shows (read 22 Oct 2026)
1. Find the connection and its duesThe GVMC water charges search asks for the type of water connection ("Regular Tap", "Semi Bulk" or "Metered Connection"), the location (GVMC, Anakapalle or Bheemili), the assessment number, and a captcha.
2. After registration, move the bill to your nameGVMC lists water services on gvmc.gov.in (for example "Water Residential Registration"), which open a citizen login. How to change the name on an existing tap connection, and the fee, are to verify with the GVMC water supply wing.
3. Keep a recordSave the receipt or acknowledgement number and the first bill in your name with your sale deed copy.

What to ask the seller for, before you pay the balance

  1. the water assessment number and the connection type (regular tap, semi bulk or metered).
  2. The last few water bills and payment receipts, so you can see the bill is in the seller's name and paid up to date.
  3. For a flat: whether the connection is one bulk connection for the whole building (paid through the association) or a separate one for the flat. Ask the association secretary for a written no-dues note on water and maintenance.
  4. For a house: whether there is also a sewerage connection, and its bill.
  5. A line in the agreement of sale that the seller clears all water and sewerage dues up to the date of handover (ask your own lawyer for the wording).

Buying in Vijayawada, Guntur or elsewhere in AP? Water connections are run by the local municipal corporation or municipality. We could not read the Vijayawada Municipal Corporation site on 22 Oct 2026 (it returned an error), so its steps are to verify. Ask the seller for the latest water tax receipt and check it with the corporation's office.
