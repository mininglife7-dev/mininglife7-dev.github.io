Sale deed signed but not registered in Andhra Pradesh: four-month rule, penalty and NRI deeds from abroad

Official texts read on 25 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: AP's Registration department gives a buyer four months from the day the deed is signed to present it at the Sub-Registrar office without any penalty. A further four months are possible only if the District Registrar condones the delay for unavoidable circumstances and collects a penalty, capped at ten times the registration fee. The IGRS registration procedure note says no document is accepted once eight months from signing have passed. A deed signed outside India gets four months from the day it arrives in India, and you may have to prove that date.

Who this is for: anyone buying a flat, house or plot in Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati or elsewhere in Andhra Pradesh whose deed is already signed but not yet registered, and NRI families who sign papers overseas and send them to AP.

Practical plan for an AP sale deed

  1. Mark day 1 as the signing date, not the agreement date. The clock in the IGRS note starts from signing (execution). Put day 120 in your calendar as the last penalty-free day, and day 240 as the date after which IGRS says the deed is dead.
  2. Prepare through Public Data Entry and book the slot together. The IGRS procedure describes the free PDE module; generating the document and booking your AP slot in the same week keeps signing and presentation close. Carry what the AP SRO checklist lists.
  3. Late by less than four months? Apply through the Sub-Registrar to the District Registrar with written reasons and evidence of the unavoidable circumstance. Expect a penalty decided by the Registrar within the ten-times cap, plus a possible separate fine if an executant also appeared late.
  4. Deed coming from abroad? Keep the courier tracking page and delivery receipt, or an affidavit from the relative who carried it, showing the arrival date in India. Powers of attorney signed overseas need attestation (FAQ Q32). Our AP NRI power of attorney page explains the stamp side.
  5. Past eight months? IGRS will not accept it. Talk to a lawyer about executing a fresh deed and check the AP stamp refund rules for the duty already paid.

Common mistake: an NRI seller signs in Dubai or the US, the original sits with a relative for months, and nobody records when it landed in India. Without proof of the arrival date, the Sub-Registrar may count from the signing date and treat the deed as late. FAQ Q21 also reminds buyers that an unregistered deed is not admissible in evidence (s.49).

FAQ

How many months do I have to register a signed sale deed in Andhra Pradesh?

Four months from signing without penalty, per the AP IGRS document registration note and FAQ Q22.

What is the penalty for late registration in AP?

If the District Registrar condones the delay for unavoidable circumstances, a penalty of up to ten times the registration fee. The exact amount is at the Registrar's discretion; the current fee rate is to verify.

Can a deed be registered after eight months in Vizag or Vijayawada?

No. The IGRS note says no document is accepted after eight months from the date of signing.

My son signed the sale deed in the USA. How do I prove when it reached India?

The IGRS note mentions evidence like a courier delivery receipt or an affidavit from the person who carried the document. The four months run from its arrival in India.

Does the four-month rule apply to a will in AP?

No. FAQ Q10 and the IGRS note say a will can be presented at any time.
