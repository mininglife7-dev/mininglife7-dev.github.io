Vacant land tax (VLT) on an open plot in Andhra Pradesh: GVMC, VMC and municipal rates and dues to check before buying

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh, an open plot inside a municipality or municipal corporation pays Vacant Land Tax (VLT) every year as a percentage of the land's capital value. The CDMA tax rates and tariffs table (read 24 Oct 2026) lists the "Vacant Land Tax (per Year)" for every urban local body: 0.5 for each of the 17 municipal corporations, including Visakhapatnam (GVMC), Vijayawada (VMC), Guntur and Tirupati, and 0.2 for the other municipalities and nagar panchayats. Before buying an open plot in Vizag or Vijayawada, get the plot's assessment number, check the VLT dues, and have the seller clear any arrears.

How the number is worked out: rate x the plot's capital value, per year. For the value, start with the registration market value (see our AP market value guide). Interest on arrears and the exact value used for your plot are to verify with the municipality.

Buyer: VLT checks for an open plot in Vizag, Vijayawada or Guntur

  1. Ask for the assessment number (GVMC asks payers to use the new number) and the last receipts.
  2. Check the plot is inside a corporation or a municipality. That decides 0.5 or 0.2. Plots in gram panchayat areas are not in this CDMA table.
  3. Match owner name, door or plot number and extent with the sale deed, the EC and the layout plan.
  4. Get arrears cleared before registration and keep the receipt in your file.
  5. Check the layout too. A VLT assessment does not mean the layout is approved. See our AP LRS and layout check.
  6. After registration, apply to move the assessment into your name. See our AP title transfer guide.

Common mistake: leaving a bought plot empty and unpaid for years. VLT keeps adding up every year, and our reading of press reports on GO 198 is that a garbage-dumping penalty can be added on top. Pay every year and keep the plot clean.

FAQ

What is the vacant land tax rate in Visakhapatnam or Vijayawada?

The CDMA tax rates table (read 24 Oct 2026) lists 0.5 per year for Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada, as for all 17 AP municipal corporations. Municipalities show 0.2. We read this as a percentage of capital value; confirm on your assessment notice.

How do I pay VLT in Vizag?

GVMC's Citizen Services page says property tax and VLT can be paid through BillDesk with the new assessment number, under e-Payment, Property Tax / Vacant Land.

Is VLT charged on plots in gram panchayats?

The CDMA table covers urban local bodies only. Panchayat-area rules are not covered here and are to verify with the panchayat.

Does a VLT receipt mean the layout is approved?

No. Tax assessment and layout approval are separate. Check LRS or layout approval separately.

Who pays old VLT arrears after I buy?

Ask the seller to clear them before registration and keep the receipt. What the municipality can recover from a new owner is a question for your lawyer.
