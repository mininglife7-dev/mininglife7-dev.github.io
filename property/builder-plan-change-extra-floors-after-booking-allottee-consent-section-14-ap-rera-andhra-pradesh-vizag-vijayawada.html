Builder adding floors or changing the plan after booking in Andhra Pradesh: allottee consent under AP-RERA

Official texts read on 27 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: AP-RERA's FAQ says a builder may make only minor changes to the sanctioned plan on its own. A major change needs approval from two-thirds of the buyers, with the builder's own flats left out and one vote per buyer (FAQ Q68). Extra floors from new FSI are possible only with buyers' consent under section 14 (Q81). Buyers who signed before the project was registered must consent too (Q76).

Who this is for: buyers in Vizag, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati and elsewhere in Andhra Pradesh who are told the builder is adding floors, moving amenities, or changing their flat after booking.

What to do in AP when the plan changes

  1. Get the old and new plans in writing, with the permission number. Check them with our AP building permission check guide.
  2. Ask: minor or major? Extra floors, a bigger built area or a height change are not minor under the Act.
  3. For a major change, ask to see the two-thirds consent (Q68), and check that pre-registration buyers were included (Q76).
  4. Compare with your agreement for sale (our AP agreement of sale checklist) and with the plans on the AP-RERA project page.
  5. If the builder goes ahead without consent, AP-RERA takes complaints (our AP-RERA complaint guide).

Red flag: "the government gave extra FSI, so no consent is needed". FAQ Q81 says the opposite: consent of allottees is needed under section 14.

FAQ

Can a builder in Vizag or Vijayawada add floors after booking?

AP-RERA FAQ Q81: yes, but only with consent of allottees as required by section 14.

What consent is needed for a major plan change in AP?

Two-thirds of the allottees, excluding the promoter's flats, one vote per allottee (FAQ Q68).

I booked before the project was RERA-registered. Do I get a say?

Yes. FAQ Q76 says consent of all pre-registration allottees is required as well.

Can the builder change later phases?

FAQ Q75: yes, as per section 14 of the Act.
