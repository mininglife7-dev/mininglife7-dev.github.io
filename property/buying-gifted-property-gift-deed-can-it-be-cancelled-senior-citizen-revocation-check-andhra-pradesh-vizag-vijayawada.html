Buying gifted property in Andhra Pradesh: can the gift deed be cancelled? (Vizag, Vijayawada)

Official texts read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh, as across India, a registered gift deed can be revoked only in the narrow cases in section 126 of the Transfer of Property Act: a condition both sides agreed (not at the donor's mere will) or grounds like fraud. The bigger risk is section 23 of the Senior Citizens Act, 2007: a gift by a parent made on condition of care can be declared void by the Maintenance Tribunal if the care stops. If your Vizag or Vijayawada seller got the plot or flat by gift, read the gift deed and check the donor's situation before paying.

Who this is for: buyers in Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur and Tirupati where the seller received the property from a parent or grandparent by gift or settlement deed.

Buyer: five checks before you buy gifted property in Vizag or Vijayawada

  1. Get a copy of the gift or settlement deed (CC) and read every condition about care, maintenance or the donor's right to live there.
  2. Check the donor: alive, over 60, living with or apart from the seller? A care condition plus a family rift is the warning sign.
  3. Pull an EC from the gift date and look for a cancellation deed.
  4. Run an eCourts search on donor and seller, and ask in writing about any tribunal petition.
  5. Get the donor's written consent or have them join the sale deed as a confirming party (lawyer's call), and publish a newspaper notice.

Common mistake: assuming that paying full price makes you safe. Section 23(2) protects a buyer for consideration without notice only against the donor's maintenance claim; whether a later buyer keeps the property if the gift itself is declared void is a question for a lawyer.

FAQ

Can a gift deed be revoked in Andhra Pradesh?

Only in the cases in section 126 of the Transfer of Property Act: an agreed condition not depending on the donor's will, or grounds on which a contract could be rescinded. Otherwise a gift cannot be revoked.

Can parents in Vizag get back property gifted to children?

Under section 23(1) of the Senior Citizens Act, 2007, if the gift was made on condition of care and the child fails to provide it, the Tribunal can declare the transfer void at the parent's option. AP's tribunal officer details are to verify.

Is a buyer of gifted property protected?

Section 23(2) protects a transferee for consideration without notice against the donor's maintenance claim. Read the gift deed, check the donor and take a lawyer's view.

What should I look for in the gift deed?

Any condition about maintenance, care or the donor's right to live in the property, and any clause letting the donor revoke the gift.

Is a settlement deed the same as a gift deed?

It is often used to give family property to relatives. Read it the same way, for conditions and reserved rights (to verify with a lawyer for your deed).
