Rectification deed and cancellation deed in Andhra Pradesh (Vizag, Vijayawada): stamp duty and registration fee

Official documents read 14 to 20 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: In Andhra Pradesh, the IGRS duty and fee table lists a rectification deed at Nil stamp duty, a cancellation deed at Rs 30, and ratification or other supplemental deeds at Rs 5. Each carries a Rs 1,000 registration fee and Rs 100 user charges. Cancelling or revoking a settlement is listed separately at Rs 90 duty.

Re-read live on the AP IGRS home page (registration.ap.gov.in) on 20 Oct 2026, and matched against the stamp duty schedule PDF (Article 15) and the registration fee table PDF (G.O.Ms.No.463 of 17-08-2013) saved by us on 15 Oct 2026. Any later change is to verify at the Sub-Registrar office.

The rows in the IGRS table

  • Row 56, Rectification: duty Nil, fee Rs 1,000, user charges Rs 100.
  • Row 15, Cancellation Deed: duty Rs 30, fee Rs 1,000, user charges Rs 100. The schedule's Article 15 reads "Thirty rupees" for an instrument by which any earlier instrument is cancelled, if attested and not otherwise provided for.
  • Row 55, Ratification and other supplemental deeds (section 4): duty Rs 5, fee Rs 1,000, user charges Rs 100. Section 4 of the Stamp Act covers the smaller papers used to complete one sale, mortgage or settlement.
  • Row 51, Cancellation / Revocation of settlement: duty Rs 90, fee Rs 1,000, user charges Rs 100.
  • Fee table (G.O.Ms.No.463, Category 3, row 3, "Rectification / Ratification / Cancellation of any Deed"): Rs 1,000, the same as the home table.

These rows are for the document types named. If your "correction" changes the extent, price, party or property, ask the Sub-Registrar how it will be charged before anyone signs (to verify).

How it adds up

Example only, not a quote: totals from the table rows above, before stamp paper vendor or e-stamp service charges.

  • Rectification of a wrong survey number in a registered sale deed: Nil + Rs 1,000 + Rs 100 = Rs 1,100.
  • Cancellation of an earlier power of attorney or agreement: Rs 30 + Rs 1,000 + Rs 100 = Rs 1,130.
  • Revoking a settlement: Rs 90 + Rs 1,000 + Rs 100 = Rs 1,190.

Run the IGRS Duty Fee Calculator for your own document before you pay. A sale deed is 4% duty in AP (see our AP sale and gift deed charges guide), so a careful first draft is worth far more than the cost of fixing it.

What the AP department's FAQs add

  • Minor's property: if a minor's property was sold without court permission, the sale can be voidable; after coming of age, the sale "has to be perfected by obtaining a registered ratification deed by the person who was a minor at the time of sale" (Property Purchase FAQ).
  • Wills: a will can be cancelled any time in the testator's lifetime, and a change to a registered will is a codicil with no stamp duty (Registration FAQ Q11, Q12). See the AP will registration guide.
  • Power of attorney: a GPA ends on the death of the person who gave it, and they can cancel it at any time (Q35). See the AP POA guide.

Whether your mistake can be fixed by a rectification deed, whether an earlier deed can be cancelled, and who has to sign, are legal questions for your own lawyer. This page only lists the published fee and duty rows and what the department's own papers say. Not legal advice.

Checklist before a rectification or cancellation

  • An official copy (CC) of the original registered deed, with its document number and year (how to get a CC in AP)
  • The exact wrong words and the exact correct words, written side by side
  • Which row fits: rectification (Nil), cancellation (Rs 30), supplemental (Rs 5) or settlement revocation (Rs 90)
  • Who must sign; if one party is abroad, a power of attorney may be needed (AP POA guide)
  • Stamp paper or e-stamp (AP e-stamp and franking guide) and a slot (AP slot booking guide)
  • After registration, a fresh EC showing the new deed (AP EC guide), and Meebhoomi updated if the survey number or name changed (mutation guide)

FAQ

What is the stamp duty on a rectification deed in Andhra Pradesh?

The AP IGRS duty and fee table lists rectification at Nil stamp duty, with a Rs 1,000 registration fee and Rs 100 user charges (row 56).

What is the stamp duty on a cancellation deed in AP?

Rs 30 (IGRS table row 15; Article 15 of the stamp duty schedule), plus a Rs 1,000 registration fee and Rs 100 user charges. Cancelling or revoking a settlement is listed at Rs 90 duty (row 51).

What is a ratification deed and what does it cost in AP?

It confirms an earlier deed, for example by a person who was a minor when their property was sold. The IGRS table lists ratification and other supplemental deeds at Rs 5 duty, Rs 1,000 fee and Rs 100 user charges (row 55).

Can a cancellation deed undo a registered sale in AP?

Whether a registered sale can be undone, and how, is a legal question for your lawyer. This page only lists the published rows. Not legal advice.

Is the registration fee a percentage of property value?

No. Both the IGRS table and G.O.Ms.No.463 list a flat Rs 1,000 for rectification, ratification or cancellation of any deed.
