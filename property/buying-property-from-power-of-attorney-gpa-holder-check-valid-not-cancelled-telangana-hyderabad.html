Buying property from a power of attorney (GPA) holder in Hyderabad: check the POA is valid and not cancelled

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: you can buy a flat or plot in Telangana from someone who signs for the owner under a power of attorney (POA), but the agent never becomes the owner. The Telangana Registration Department says so plainly: "It is wrong to say that ownership is transferred by getting General Power of Attorney" (Registration FAQ Q32, read 24 Oct 2026). The same FAQ says a GPA "automatically gets cancelled on the death of Executant" and that the owner "may cancel it at any time" (Q34). So before you pay, confirm three things: the POA really allows selling this property, it was properly attested, and the owner is alive and has not revoked it.

Who this is for: buyers dealing with a relative, builder's man or broker who holds a GPA from an NRI or elderly owner. Paying the wrong person is hard to undo. Read this next to our guide for owners who give a POA.

Buyer: six checks before you pay a POA holder in Hyderabad

  1. See the original POA, not a photocopy or a WhatsApp image. Read the property schedule: survey or plot number, extent and boundaries must match the land you are buying.
  2. Read the powers. Does it say "sell" and "sign and present the sale deed"? A POA that only covers management, rent or court cases does not let the agent sell.
  3. Check who attested it. Signed in India: look for the Sub-Registrar's attestation. Signed abroad: a Consul, Vice-Consul or Notary, and proof the stamp duty was paid in India within 3 months.
  4. Get a fresh CC (official copy from the Sub-Registrar) if the POA was registered. Under Q40 only the owner, agent or their representative can apply, so make it a condition before the advance and compare it line by line with the original.
  5. Confirm the owner is alive and has not cancelled it. Speak to the owner directly, for example on a video call, and keep a dated note of it. If the owner is abroad and elderly, ask for a recent signed letter confirming the sale price and terms.
  6. Pay the owner's own bank account, not the agent's, unless the POA expressly allows the agent to receive the price. Then do the usual title, EC and court case checks.

Common mistake: buying "on GPA" from someone who himself bought the property only on a GPA. The FAQ is clear that a GPA transfers no ownership, so that seller owns nothing he can sell. Insist that the registered sale deed is signed by the real owner, or by an agent acting under that owner's own valid POA.

FAQ

Can a GPA holder sell my seller's property in Hyderabad?

Yes, if the POA gives the power to sell that property and is still valid, the agent can sign the registered sale deed for the owner. The Telangana Registration FAQ says a GPA does not make the agent the owner.

Does a GPA end when the owner dies?

Yes. The Telangana Registration FAQ (Q34) says a GPA is automatically cancelled on the death of the executant, and the owner may cancel it at any time.

What is an irrevocable POA?

Per FAQ Q35, a POA executed for consideration in respect of property cannot be revoked one-sidedly to the agent's prejudice (section 202 of the Indian Contract Act).

Can I get a copy (CC) of the seller's GPA myself?

Per FAQ Q40, copies of a registered GPA are given only to the executant, the claimant or their agent or representative. Ask the owner or agent to obtain it.

Is a POA signed in the US or UK valid for a Telangana sale?

It must be attested by a Notary, Consul or Vice-Consul abroad (FAQ Q36), and stamp duty must be paid within 3 months of receipt in India if not already paid (Q37).
