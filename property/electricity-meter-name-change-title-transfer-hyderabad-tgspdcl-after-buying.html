Electricity meter name change after buying in Hyderabad (TGSPDCL title transfer): documents, fee, dues

Checked against official sources on 19 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: After you buy a flat or house in Hyderabad, the electricity service connection stays in the seller's name until you apply for a title transfer with TGSPDCL. TGSPDCL's online form (headed "Title Transfer/Name Correction Details") asks for your ID proof, the registered document and an indemnity bond on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, and showed an application fee of ₹25 plus GST when we read it on 19 Oct 2026. The security deposit moves with the connection, so settle it with the seller.

Which company is yours?

This page covers TGSPDCL (Southern Power Distribution Company of Telangana), which serves Hyderabad. The 13-digit or unique service number and the company name are printed on your bill. If the bill says TGNPDCL, follow that company's own process (to verify).

Documents TGSPDCL lists for a title transfer

From TGSPDCL's "Title Transfer – Documents required" sheet (linked from its Downloads page):

  1. True copy of the sale deed, lease deed, gift deed or partition deed
  2. True copy of the General Power of Attorney, if one was used
  3. Latest house tax receipt (see our GHMC property tax check)
  4. Latest electricity bill receipt
  5. Latest municipal corporation tax demand notice
  6. Encumbrance certificate (our EC guide)
  7. Transfer application form, signed in duplicate by the earlier consumer and by you
  8. Indemnity bond on a ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, "if necessary"
  9. Link documents that connect the previous consumer to you, where applicable
  10. Demand draft of the prescribed amount

The sheet says a "true copy" means a photocopy endorsed "True Copy, seen and attested" by a Notary Public or Gazetted Officer, and that a photocopy of a true copy is not accepted. This list reads like the counter route; the online form below asks for fewer uploads.

Applying online: what the form asks for

  • New owner name, S/o or W/o, PIN code, ID proof type and registration document type
  • ID proof: JPG/JPEG, up to 100 KB
  • Indemnity bond: PDF up to 500 KB, in TGSPDCL's prescribed format on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper with the applicant's photo (our e-stamp guide explains stamp paper)
  • Registered document: PDF (the size limit was hard to read on the page; 5 MB as far as we could tell, to verify)
  • Joint owners: a No-Objection Consent on ₹10 non-judicial stamp paper. Company: MoA and AoA or partnership deed plus an authorisation letter. Legal heir: death certificate of the earlier owner and a legal heir certificate.
  • Fee: application fee ₹25 plus GST on it. Search results showed ₹29.50 in total; confirm on the payment screen (to verify).

The paper transfer application form has the seller transfer the connection "with Security Deposit of Rs. …" and has you agree to abide by the original conditions of supply. The indemnity bond lets TGSPDCL disconnect or cancel the transfer if the documents turn out to be wrong.

Before you pay: the seller's unpaid power bill

In K.C. Ninan v. Kerala State Electricity Board (Supreme Court, 19 May 2023, 2023 INSC 560), the Court held that under general law old electricity arrears are not automatically a charge on the property, but that a regulator's conditions of supply can make a later owner or occupier pay the previous owner's arrears before getting a connection. So the practical rule for a buyer is simple: get the seller's latest bill and paid receipt before the final payment, and put "all electricity dues up to handover paid by the seller" in the sale terms. Whether your state's current supply conditions carry such a clause, and how it applies to your purchase, is a question for your lawyer (to verify).

Green / Amber / Red

  • Green: the registered sale deed is in your name, the seller has signed the transfer form, the last bill is paid, and the address on the bill matches the deed.
  • Amber: the seller is abroad or unreachable for signatures, the connection is in a third person's name (a tenant or an earlier owner), or there is more than one meter. Ask for link documents and written consent before the final payment.
  • Red: the bill shows arrears or a disconnection, or the seller refuses to sign the transfer form. Don't release the balance until it is resolved in writing.

What to keep on file

  • Last bill and paid receipt from the seller, with the service number
  • Signed transfer form (both copies) and your indemnity bond
  • Registered sale deed (or a registered copy from IGRS if the original is with a bank) and EC
  • Screenshot of the online application number and payment receipt

FAQ

How do I change the electricity meter name after buying a flat in Hyderabad?

Apply for a title transfer with TGSPDCL. Its online form asks for your ID proof, the registered document and an indemnity bond on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper. The paper route also uses a transfer form signed by the earlier consumer and you.

What is the TGSPDCL title transfer fee?

When we read TGSPDCL's online form on 19 Oct 2026 it showed an application fee of ₹25 plus GST. Confirm the total on the payment screen.

What happens to the security deposit when the name changes?

TGSPDCL's transfer form transfers the connection with the security deposit already paid. Agree with the seller in writing how that deposit is settled between you.

Can I be asked to pay the seller's old electricity dues?

The Supreme Court held in K.C. Ninan v. KSEB (2023) that supply conditions framed under the Electricity Act can require a new owner or occupier to clear earlier arrears before a connection. Get the latest paid bill before you pay. Not legal advice.

Does an electricity bill in my name prove I own the property?

No. Ownership comes from the registered deed and land or municipal records. Use the bill only as supporting proof of address and dues.
