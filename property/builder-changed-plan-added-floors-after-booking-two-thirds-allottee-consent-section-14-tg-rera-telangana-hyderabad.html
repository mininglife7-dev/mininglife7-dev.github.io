Builder changed the plan or added floors after booking in Hyderabad? Two-thirds consent under RERA section 14

Official texts read on 27 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: once the sanctioned plan has been shown to you, a builder in Telangana cannot change your flat without your previous consent, except minor changes (RERA Act section 14(2)(i)). Any other change to the building or the common areas, such as extra floors, needs the previous written consent of at least two-thirds of the buyers, not counting the builder (section 14(2)(ii)). The Telangana model agreement for sale repeats this: no additions or alterations "without the previous written consent of the Allottee" (clause 1.6). A new approval from GHMC or HMDA does not replace the buyers' consent.

Who this is for: Hyderabad buyers who hear that the builder "got permission for two more floors", that the clubhouse is moving, or that the layout of their own flat is changing after they booked.

Check this if the builder announces a change

  1. Ask for the change in writing: the old and the new sanctioned plan, and the permission number. Check the approval status with our building permission check guide.
  2. Compare with the plan in your agreement for sale and its schedules. Anything outside "minor" in s.14(2)(i) needs your previous consent.
  3. For extra floors or common areas, ask how the two-thirds written consent was collected and how many buyers signed. Do not sign a blank or bundled consent form.
  4. Check the TG-RERA project page for the plans uploaded there (our TG-RERA project check guide).
  5. Talk to the other buyers. The two-thirds count is per buyer, not per flat, and the builder's own flats are left out.
  6. If the change goes ahead without consent, a complaint lies to TG-RERA (our complaint guide).

Red flag: a consent letter handed out with the possession papers, or tucked into a "no objection" for a loan, that also agrees to "future additions". Read every page before you sign.

FAQ

Can a builder in Hyderabad add floors after I booked?

Only with the previous written consent of at least two-thirds of the allottees, other than the builder (RERA Act s.14(2)(ii)).

Can the builder change my flat's layout?

Not without your previous consent, except minor changes (s.14(2)(i)). An addition to area, a change in height or cutting into walls or columns is not minor.

How is the two-thirds counted?

Per allottee, not per flat. A buyer with several flats, or flats in family names, counts once, and the builder's own flats are excluded.

Is a new GHMC or HMDA approval enough?

Section 14(2) applies notwithstanding any other law or contract. Whether a revised sanction without consent can stand is to verify with your lawyer.
