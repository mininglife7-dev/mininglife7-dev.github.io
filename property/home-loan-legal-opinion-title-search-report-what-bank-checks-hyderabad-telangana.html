Home loan legal opinion and title search report in Hyderabad: what the bank checks and what you still must check

Official pages read on 22 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: before a bank gives a home loan on a Hyderabad flat or plot, it gets a legal opinion on the title from its own approved advocate, and you usually pay the fee. That opinion protects the bank. The Telangana Registration Department still tells buyers to "Engage a competent lawyer ... to investigate the title, possession, ownership and other aspects", and says "EC cannot be taken as a sole criterion to buy a property or lend money." So use the bank's check as a second opinion, not your only one.

What Telangana's registration department says about title

  • "Before you buy a property, verify the title of the seller. A search of the records at the sub-registrar's office may be carried out for documents that may affect the property".
  • The municipal Assessment Register entry (house tax record in the owner's name) "has become important because it is required for loans, title opinions, electricity meter name change, etc." If it is not in the seller's name, ask for mutation first.
  • On a nil EC: "unregistered transactions are not included in this certificate" (FAQs, Q39).
  • On a seller's old loan: "Release certificate issued by the bank is necessary for all the debts over the land. ... if you want to take a loan in future, the release certificate is a must."

What the bank's lawyer is checking, in the bank's own words

When you take a home loan, the bank asks its own advocate to look at the property papers and give a legal opinion (often called a title search report or legal scrutiny report). One large lender, SBI, says on its home loan FAQ: "The title of the property should be clear, for which a certificate would be required from the Bank's approved advocate, safeguarding your interests as well as Bank's interests." The same FAQ says: "Out of pocket expenses like CERSAI charges and Professional fee for Legal Opinion and Valuation also will be recovered." So the opinion is usually paid for by you. Other banks' rules and fees differ; ask yours.

Your original title deeds while the loan runs, and after

A home loan is usually secured by depositing your original title deeds with the bank. The registration departments warn buyers about this from the other side: when a seller says the originals are missing, "There is a possibility of keeping the original documents in banks/financial institutions/private lenders to create mortgage/charge over the property by way of deposit of title deeds."

When you finish paying, an RBI circular (RBI/2023-24/60, 13 Sep 2023) says lenders "shall release all the original movable / immovable property documents and remove charges registered with any registry within a period of 30 days after full repayment/ settlement of the loan account". If the delay is the lender's fault, "it shall compensate the borrower at the rate of ₹5,000/- for each day of delay". The circular says the timeline and place of return will be in loan sanction letters issued on or after its effective date, and it applies where release falls due on or after 1 December 2023. It is written for "personal loans" as RBI defines them; check with your bank that your home loan is covered, and whether RBI has since moved this into newer directions (to verify).

Do this on day one: keep a photocopy and a list of every original you hand over (sale deed, link deeds, EC, approvals), signed by the branch. Check the sanction letter for the return timeline.

Five questions to ask the loan officer before you pay the seller an advance

  1. Which advocate will give the legal opinion, and how long does it take?
  2. What is the fee for the legal opinion and the valuation, and is it refundable if the bank rejects the property?
  3. Can I get a copy of the legal opinion or title search report? (to verify per bank)
  4. Which documents must be originals, and which period of EC do you need? (banks set this themselves; to verify)
  5. Where and within how many days will my originals be returned when I close the loan?

Ask these early. If the bank's advocate rejects the title after you have paid an advance, you may be left arguing with the seller over the refund. Put a clause in the agreement of sale about what happens if the loan is not sanctioned (ask your own lawyer for the wording).

FAQ

What does a bank check before a home loan in Hyderabad?

Its approved advocate reviews the title papers and gives a legal opinion. Lenders also ask for documents such as the sale deed, EC, approved plan and occupancy certificate; the exact list depends on the bank.

Who pays for the bank's legal opinion?

Usually the borrower. SBI's home loan FAQ says the professional fee for legal opinion and valuation is recovered as an out-of-pocket expense. Ask your bank for its fee.

Can I rely only on the bank's title search report?

It is written for the bank. The registration department still advises engaging your own lawyer and says an EC alone is not enough to buy or lend.

When must the bank return my original documents after closing the loan?

An RBI circular of 13 Sep 2023 says within 30 days of full repayment, with ₹5,000 per day compensation if the delay is the lender's fault. Check that your loan is covered and the current RBI direction.

Why does the house tax record matter for a loan?

The Telangana Registration Department says the Assessment Register entry is required for loans and title opinions. If it is not in the seller's name, ask for mutation.
