Registration refused in Andhra Pradesh (22-A or other reasons): appeal routes for buyers in Vizag and Vijayawada

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh, two different routes apply depending on why the Sub-Registrar refused. If the property is on the section 22-A prohibited list, para 12 of G.O.Ms.No.444 dated 22.07.2026 says the refusal must be a written order stating the survey number, sub-division, extent and 22-A category. The fix is with the Revenue Divisional Officer or Sub-Collector, then an appeal to the Joint Collector within 60 days (paras 7 and 8). For other refusals, such as the seller denying execution, the Registration Act, 1908 route applies: the District Registrar within 30 days (sections 72 and 73), then a civil suit within 30 days (section 77).

Seven steps after a refusal in Vizag, Vijayawada or elsewhere in AP

  1. Get the order on paper. For a 22-A refusal, para 12 needs a written order with the survey number, sub-division, extent and category. For other refusals, section 71 entitles you to a free copy of the reasons.
  2. Write down the date. The 30-day and 60-day clocks run from the order.
  3. Check the land yourself. Run the IGRS prohibited property search and open the Webland record on Meebhoomi. Save dated screenshots.
  4. Pick the authority. A land-list problem goes to the RDO or Sub-Collector and then the Joint Collector. A signing or paper problem goes to the District Registrar.
  5. Match a G.O. exit if the land is listed. Our assigned land guide lists the paras that remove certain lands from the list.
  6. Present again within 30 days of any order directing registration (sections 72(2) and 75(2)). Registration then counts from the first presentation.
  7. Hold the balance. Keep the unpaid amount until the deed is registered, and ask your lawyer about the refund clause.

Common mistake: signing a fresh notarised agreement or GPA after a 22-A refusal "to keep the deal alive". It does not change the land's status, and it can make the money harder to recover. Fix the record first.

FAQ

What must a 22-A refusal order in AP contain?

Para 12 of G.O.Ms.No.444 (22.07.2026) says it must be a written order stating the survey number, sub-division, extent and the 22-A category.

Who removes my land from the 22-A list?

The Revenue Divisional Officer or Sub-Collector (para 7.1). An appeal lies to the Joint Collector within 60 days (para 8), and a revision to the Government within 90 days (para 9).

Does the District Registrar handle 22-A refusals?

The District Registrar route in sections 72 and 73 of the Registration Act covers refusals by a Sub-Registrar. For a land-list entry, the G.O. sets the Revenue route; ask your lawyer which applies to your order.

If the appeal succeeds, do I pay again?

Not stated in the sources we read. Sections 72(2) and 75(2) say the deed must be presented again within 30 days and then counts as registered from its first presentation. Ask the office about fees (to verify).
