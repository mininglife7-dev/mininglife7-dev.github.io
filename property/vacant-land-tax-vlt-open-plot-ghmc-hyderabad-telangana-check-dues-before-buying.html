Vacant land tax (VLT) on an open plot in Hyderabad: GHMC rate, documents and dues to check before buying

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: an open plot inside GHMC limits pays Vacant Land Tax (VLT), not the usual house property tax. GHMC's VLT procedure note puts it at half a percent (0.5%) of the land's estimated capital value under Section 199(3) of the GHMC Act. A plot has its own VLT number, and GHMC's VLT payment page asks for that number and the registered mobile number. Before you buy an open plot in Hyderabad, ask the seller for the VLT number and receipts and check that nothing is pending. Unpaid VLT is a charge on the plot, and once you are the owner, collecting it is between you and GHMC.

How the number is worked out: 0.5% x the plot's estimated capital value, per year. For that value, start with the registration market value for the plot (see our market value guide). Exactly which value GHMC adopts for a given plot is to verify on its assessment notice.

Buyer: six VLT checks before you pay for an open plot in Hyderabad

  1. Ask for the VLT number and the last few payment receipts. If the seller has none, the plot may never have been assessed. Ask why, and ask your lawyer how that affects you.
  2. Match names and extent. The owner name and plot area on the VLT record should match the sale deed and the EC.
  3. Look for arrears. Ask the seller to clear any pending VLT before registration, and keep the receipt in the deal file.
  4. Read the documents list as a checklist. GHMC asks for the sale deed, attested link documents, LRS regularisation if any, and a recent EC. If the seller can't produce these for GHMC, they are the same gaps a buyer should worry about. See our LRS and layout check.
  5. After purchase, move the VLT into your name and update the registered mobile number so the payment page works for you.
  6. When you build, the plot moves from VLT to property tax on the building. Keep the last VLT receipt.

Common mistake: treating a VLT receipt as proof of title. GHMC checks title before it assesses, but a tax record is still a municipal record, not a title document. Your lawyer must read the full chain and a fresh EC.

FAQ

What is vacant land tax in Hyderabad?

GHMC's tax on open land that is not used only for agriculture and is not part of a building's premises. GHMC's procedure note puts it at 0.5% of the land's estimated capital value (GHMC Act s.199(3)).

Does the open land around my house pay VLT?

Under s.212, land up to three times the plinth area (including the site) or 1,000 sq m, whichever is less, is taxed with the building. Only the excess pays VLT.

How do I pay GHMC VLT online?

On GHMC's VLT payment page, with the VLT number and the registered mobile number. The page says no bank account is needed.

What documents does GHMC ask for to assess VLT?

The registered sale deed, link documents attested by a Gazetted Officer, LRS regularisation if any, and the latest EC and market value.

Does paying VLT prove I own the plot?

No. It is a municipal tax record. GHMC checks title before assessing, but only the registered documents and a lawyer's title check can confirm ownership.
