Resale flat in Vizag or Vijayawada: association NOC, unpaid maintenance and sinking fund check before registration

Official texts read on 25 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in a resale apartment deal in Visakhapatnam or Vijayawada, the seller's unpaid maintenance does not simply vanish at registration. Under section 22 of the Andhra Pradesh Apartments (Promotion of Construction and Ownership) Act, 1987, the new owner shares liability with the old one for common-expense arrears up to the sale, and may then claim the money back from the seller. The AP Registration department's own buyer guidance recommends a no dues certificate from the society. Collect that letter and settle who pays what in writing before the sale deed is presented.

Who this is for: anyone, including NRIs, buying a previously owned flat in an AP apartment complex (Vizag, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati and other towns) that is run by an owners' association, a welfare society or the builder's maintenance agency.

Five checks before the sale deed in Andhra Pradesh

  1. Ask the association (or the builder's maintenance office, if no association yet) for a no-dues certificate that states the flat number, the owner's name, the date and the period paid up to, and carries an office-bearer's signature and seal.
  2. Read the flat's maintenance account and recent general body minutes. Look for approved one-time levies, penalties for late payment and any dispute between the seller and the association. Once you own the flat, a lawfully passed resolution binds you.
  3. Trace the corpus or sinking fund deposit. Collect the original receipt from the seller and ask the treasurer whether the builder ever transferred it. Agree in writing whether any refund is part of the price.
  4. Record the dues position in the agreement of sale: who pays society, property tax, water and power dues up to registration, an indemnity from the seller, and a sum held back until the no-dues letter is produced.
  5. Close the civic accounts. See our pages on property tax transfer in AP, GVMC water charges and the electricity meter name change, and pull an IGRS encumbrance certificate.

Common mistake: treating the association letter as a formality to collect after registration. Once the deed is registered you have lost your best leverage, and section 22 lets the association pursue you for the old dues as well as the seller.

FAQ

Am I liable for the previous owner's maintenance arrears in Vizag or Vijayawada?

Possibly. Under section 22 of the AP Apartments Act, 1987 a purchaser shares liability with the vendor for common-expense arrears up to the sale and can then recover them from the vendor. Check whether your complex registered a Declaration (to verify).

Does the AP Sub-Registrar ask for the society NOC?

The AP IGRS precautions note recommends a no dues certificate from the society to protect the buyer. We did not find a rule making it a registration condition.

Who issues the no-dues letter if the association is not registered yet?

Usually whoever collects maintenance, often the builder or its maintenance agency. Under RERA s.11(4)(d) the builder maintains essential services until the association takes over.

What happens to the sinking fund when a flat is resold?

The RERA Act text we read does not mention it. The builder's agreement and the bye-laws decide, so get the treasurer's written answer and record any refund in the sale agreement.

Can unpaid association dues rank ahead of my home loan?

Section 20 makes assessed common expenses a charge on the flat ahead of other charges, except Government and municipal taxes and sums unpaid on a first mortgage. Ask your lawyer how this affects your loan.
