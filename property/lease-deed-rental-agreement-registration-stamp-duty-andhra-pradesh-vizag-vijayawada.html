Lease deed / rental agreement registration in Andhra Pradesh (Vizag, Vijayawada): stamp duty and fee

Official documents read 14 to 20 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: In Andhra Pradesh, a lease or rental agreement of less than 1 year carries 0.4% stamp duty on the total rent, a 0.10% registration fee and Rs 100 user charges. For 1 to 5 years the duty is 0.5% of the average annual rent for a home (1% in other cases), and it climbs to 6% for 10 to 20 years and 15% for 20 to 30 years (IGRS duty and fee table, rows 23 to 33).

Rows 23 to 33 and row 52 read from the duty and fee table on the AP IGRS home page, saved by us on 15 Oct 2026 and re-read live on 20 Oct 2026. Any later change is to verify with the IGRS Duty Fee Calculator or the Sub-Registrar office.

The lease rows, as published

  • Row 23, less than 1 year: 0.4% on total rent; fee 0.10%; user charges Rs 100.
  • Rows 24 and 25, 1 to 5 years: residential 0.5%, other cases 1%, on the average annual rent (A.A.R.).
  • Rows 26 and 27, 5 to 10 years: residential 1%, other cases 2%, on A.A.R.
  • Row 28, 10 to 20 years: 6%; row 29, 20 to 30 years: 15%, on A.A.R.
  • Row 30, more than 30 years or perpetual: 3% on the market value of the property.
  • Rows 31 to 33: 2% on a fine, premium or money advanced (with or without rent), and 2% on a lump sum for improvements handed to the owner at the end, in addition to the rent duty.
  • Row 52, surrender of lease: Rs 30 duty, Rs 1,000 fee, Rs 100 user charges.

Every lease row lists the same 0.10% fee and Rs 100 user charges. The table does not say whether the fee is on the total rent or the A.A.R., so the examples below use the same base as the duty (to verify).

How it adds up

Example only, not a quote: from the rows above; e-stamp or vendor charges and rounding are extra or to verify.

  • 11-month home rental at Rs 25,000 a month: total rent Rs 2,75,000; duty 0.4% = Rs 1,100; fee 0.10% = Rs 275; user charges Rs 100; total Rs 1,475.
  • 3-year home lease at a fixed Rs 30,000 a month: A.A.R. Rs 3,60,000; duty 0.5% = Rs 1,800; fee Rs 360; user charges Rs 100; total Rs 2,260.
  • 3-year shop lease at Rs 50,000 a month: A.A.R. Rs 6,00,000; duty 1% = Rs 6,000; fee Rs 600; user charges Rs 100; total Rs 6,700.
  • Ending a registered lease early by surrender: Rs 30 + Rs 1,000 + Rs 100 = Rs 1,130 (row 52).

Three things to ask the Sub-Registrar first

  • Security deposit: whether a refundable deposit counts as "money advanced" under rows 31 and 32 is not stated in the table (to verify). If it does, Rs 1,00,000 would add Rs 2,000.
  • Band edges: if your term is exactly 5 or 10 years, confirm the band, since the rate can jump from 2% to 6%.
  • Must it be registered? The AP Registration FAQ lists "leases of immovable property" among the documents that must be registered, without a duration in that line. Ask the office or your lawyer for your term (to verify).

Whether your rental needs to be registered, what the lease should say, and how rent is taxed are questions for your own lawyer and tax adviser. This page only lists the published duty and fee rows and what the department's own FAQs say. Not legal advice.

Checklist before a lease in AP

  • The term, rent, yearly increase and deposit written down, then run through the IGRS Duty Fee Calculator
  • Stamp paper or e-stamp for the confirmed duty (AP e-stamp guide)

FAQ

What is the stamp duty on an 11-month rental agreement in AP?

IGRS table row 23 lists 0.4% of the total rent, a 0.10% registration fee and Rs 100 user charges. On Rs 25,000 a month for 11 months that is about Rs 1,475 in all.

What is the stamp duty on a 3-year residential lease in Andhra Pradesh?

0.5% of the average annual rent (row 24), with a 0.10% fee and Rs 100 user charges. Non-residential leases of 1 to 5 years are 1% (row 25).

What does it cost to surrender a lease in AP?

Row 52 lists Rs 30 stamp duty, a Rs 1,000 registration fee and Rs 100 user charges.

Is stamp duty charged on the security deposit in AP?

Rows 31 and 32 charge 2% on a fine, premium or money advanced. Whether a refundable deposit counts is not stated in the table; ask the Sub-Registrar.

What is the duty on a lease of more than 30 years in AP?

Row 30 lists 3% of the market value of the property, not of the rent.
