FAQ

What is loading in a flat?

Loading is the gap between the area a seller quotes (built-up or super built-up) and the carpet area the RERA Act defines. This tool shows it as a percentage of the carpet area and of the quoted area, so you can compare two flats on the same basis.

Which area does the RERA Act define?

Only carpet area. Section 2(k) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 defines it as the net usable floor area of a flat, excluding external walls, service shafts and the exclusive balcony, verandah or open terrace, but including internal partition walls. The Act does not define super built-up, saleable or plinth area.

Where do I get the carpet area of a flat?

From the project's registration on the state RERA website. Section 4(2)(h) asks the promoter to give the carpet area of each apartment type, with the exclusive balcony or verandah and open terrace areas, if any, shown separately.

What loading percentage is normal?

We don't publish a normal or acceptable figure, because the Act sets none. Use the tool to compare flats like with like, and ask the seller in writing what the extra area includes.
