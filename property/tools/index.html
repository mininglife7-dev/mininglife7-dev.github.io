FAQ

Are these tools free?

Yes. They need no signup and no card, and they run in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded.

Which tool should I open first?

If you are about to buy, start with the property buying checklist generator. It lists the official checks in order and links each one to its government portal and our guide.

Do the results replace a lawyer or the registration office?

No. They are estimates and checklists built from the official sources each tool cites. Rates, rules and portal steps can change, so confirm on the official portal or with the Sub-Registrar, RERA authority or a qualified professional before you pay. Not legal or tax advice.
