FAQ

How is the refund worked out if I cancel a flat booking?

If the builder is not at fault, clause 7.5 of the Telangana RERA model agreement for sale lets the builder forfeit the booking amount. The rest of what you paid comes back within three months, or when the flat is resold, whichever is later. The tool subtracts the booking amount stated in your agreement from what you paid.

What if the builder is at fault?

Section 18 of the RERA Act: if possession is not given as agreed and you withdraw, the builder returns the amount received with interest and compensation. The tool adds simple interest from each payment date at the rate you enter. Telangana rule 15 sets the rate at the highest SBI MCLR plus 2%. Compensation is decided separately and is not included.

What is the 10% rule?

Section 13(1) of the RERA Act: a builder shall not take more than 10% of the cost as an advance or application fee before a written agreement for sale is signed and registered. AP-RERA's FAQ (Q66, Q85) says the same. The tool flags any payment above 10% made without a registered agreement.

Is the interest simple or compound?

The rules we read give the rate but not the method. The tool shows simple interest as an estimate, so treat the method as to verify.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is arithmetic on your numbers. Your agreement, the RERA authority or a court decides the actual refund. Not legal advice.
