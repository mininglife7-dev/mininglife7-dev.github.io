FAQ

Is this tool free?

Yes. No signup, no card, and nothing you tick leaves your browser.

Does a clean result mean the land is safe to buy?

No. The tool only lists warning signs from the official documents it cites. Whether a parcel is assigned land is decided by the Revenue authorities. Run the official searches and have a lawyer read the title.

What is DKT or lavuni patta?

Terms commonly used for land the Government granted to a person, usually with a condition that it must not be sold. The Bhu Bharati Act refers to "assigned or lavuni lands" in section 16.

Where do the AP rules come from?

G.O.Ms.No.444, Revenue (Registration-I), dated 22.07.2026, paras 4.1, 5.1 to 5.11, 7 to 9 and 12. We read a copy on 24 Oct 2026; check the current G.O. before relying on it.
