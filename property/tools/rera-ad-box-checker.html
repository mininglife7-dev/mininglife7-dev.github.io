FAQ

Does this tool make my ad RERA compliant?

No. It helps you spot missing details before you publish. Only TG-RERA or AP-RERA decides compliance, and this isn't legal advice.

Is my project data sent anywhere?

No. The checker runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored by us.

Which rules does it use?

For Telangana, TG-RERA's public notice under Section 37 and its FAQ (checked on official PDFs). For Andhra Pradesh, AP-RERA's Annexure-I 'RERA Registered' stamp rule and FAQs. Links are in Official sources below.

Do agents have to show their own agent number in every ad?

We didn't find a TG-RERA rule that says so. Showing it is good practice, but we won't tell you it's mandatory.

Is this legal advice?

No. Not legal advice. Government websites change; we checked these sources on 14 Oct 2026.
