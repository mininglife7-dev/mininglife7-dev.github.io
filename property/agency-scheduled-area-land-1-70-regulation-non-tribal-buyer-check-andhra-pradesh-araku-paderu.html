Agency (Scheduled Area) land in Andhra Pradesh: can a non-tribal buy near Araku or Paderu? 1/70 rule check

Official texts read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh's Scheduled Areas (agency areas), for example the 11 scheduled mandals of the Paderu ITDA division in Alluri Sitharama Raju district, which includes the Araku belt, the 1/70 rule makes a transfer of land by a tribal to a non-tribal void. Land held by a non-tribal there is presumed to have come from a tribal unless proved otherwise, and officials can order eviction. Before you buy a resort plot or farm land near Araku, Paderu or other hill villages, confirm whether the village is in a Scheduled Area.

Who this is for: Vizag, Vijayawada and NRI buyers offered hill-station plots, coffee land or homestay sites in the agency areas, and anyone buying from a non-tribal seller there.

Five checks before you buy land near Araku, Paderu or other AP agency villages

  1. Confirm the status in writing from the Tahsildar or the ITDA: is this village and survey number in a Scheduled Area?
  2. Read the record chain in Meebhoomi and the EC. A tribal owner earlier in the chain followed by a non-tribal one is a red flag.
  3. Search the IGRS prohibited list and run an eCourts search.
  4. Avoid "lease for 99 years" or "agreement only" offers. Leases, mortgages and contracts are transfers under s.2(g), and a tribal front buyer is benami under s.3(4).
  5. Take a lawyer's written view on the survey number. Assigned land carries a separate bar; see our DKT patta and 22-A page.

Common mistake: assuming that a resort or homestay "project" with a brochure must be legal. A non-tribal company or individual is not exempt; ask for the written Scheduled Area status before you pay.

FAQ

Can outsiders buy land in Araku?

If the land is in a notified Scheduled Area, a transfer by a tribal to a non-tribal is void under the 1/70 rule, and non-tribal possession is presumed to come from a tribal. Confirm the village status with the Tahsildar or ITDA Paderu.

Which AP areas are Scheduled Areas?

The Regulation names the old East Godavari, West Godavari, Visakhapatnam and Srikakulam districts' agency tracts. The ASR district site says the Paderu ITDA division has 11 scheduled mandals. Village lists: to verify.

Does inheritance count as a transfer?

No. Section 3(4) excludes partition and devolution by succession.

Can a bank loan be taken on agency land?

Section 3-A allows a mortgage without possession to a co-operative society or approved bank, but on default the land can be sold only to a tribal or an ST-only society.

Will the Sub-Registrar register a sale to a non-tribal?

Section 3-B bars registration unless the buyer declares, in the prescribed form, that they are an ST member or ST-only society.
