E-stamp / franking for agreements in Telangana / Hyderabad (sale, rent, POA)

Checked against official sources on 14 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: In Telangana / Hyderabad, stamp duty for a sale, rent or power-of-attorney agreement is paid on the Registration & Stamps portal, not through StockHolding (SHCIL) e-Stamping. StockHolding's own e-stamping state list (as shown on 14 Oct 2026; list dated 20 Mar 2025) names many states including Andhra Pradesh — Telangana is not on that list. For deeds that will be registered, the department's home page says payments go through the Pre Registration / slot-booking module. For non-registration stamp payments the same banner points to e-STAMPS (and the Meeseva App). Traditional low-denomination non-judicial stamp paper, adhesive stamps, challan / DD, and licensed franking-machine holders are also listed on the department site. We don't sell stamp paper or stamp documents for anyone. This page shows what the official pages showed when we read them on 14 Oct 2026.

Three different routes people mix up

  • Registration path (sale deed and most deeds at the SRO): pay stamp duty and registration fee inside Pre Registration on registration.telangana.gov.in, then book the SRO slot. See our registration slot booking guide.
  • Non-registration e-STAMPS: the home-page tile e-STAMPS opens eStamp_Dashboard.htm (citizen login: User Type, Mobile/Email ID, Password, captcha). The Downloads menu also links an eSTAMPS / eChallan user manual. Post-login payment screens are (to verify). The banner on the home page says e-STAMPS / Meeseva App is for non-registration services; registration payments must go through Pre Registration.
  • Paper / adhesive / franking: FAQs-Stamps Q4 lists impressed stamps (treasury, licensed stamp vendors, Sub-Registrar offices), adhesive stamps, or payment by DD / pay order / challan. The Stamp Vendors / Notaries / Franking Services page links Stamp Vendor List, Franking Machine Holders and Franking Machine eChallan (for franking-machine licence holders).

What FAQs-Stamps says about stamp paper in Telangana

  • Q4 / Q21 (checked 14 Oct 2026 on faqsStamps.htm): Non-Judicial stamp papers of Rs.10, Rs.20, Rs.50 and Rs.100 only are available from Sub-Registrar offices, licensed stamp vendors or post offices. Higher duty is paid by challan (the FAQ's worked example: buy five Rs.100 papers for Rs.500, pay the rest by challan).
  • Q5: a stamp paper used for one purpose cannot be reused for another.
  • Q6: duty is generally paid before or at the time of execution; plain-paper + DD/challan within one year, then District Registrar endorsement under ss.41–42, is also described for documents not coming for registration.
  • Q20: no time limit stated for using purchased stamp papers.
  • Q7 (document executed outside India for use in Telangana): duty can be paid within three months of receipt in India, endorsed by the District Registrar under s.18.

Exact rupee rates for sale, gift, lease or POA are on our Telangana stamp duty and registration charges guide and the department's published fee pages — we don't invent rates here. For NRI POA wording see NRI power of attorney (GPA/SPA) stamp duty.

Step by step: get the stamp right before registration day

  1. Decide if the agreement will be registered. Sale deeds and most property deeds go through Pre Registration + slot booking. Unregistered rent / POA / other non-registration instruments are the e-STAMPS / paper / franking path — confirm with your lawyer which route applies.
  2. If it will be registered: open Pre Registration on registration.telangana.gov.in, complete the online entry, pay stamp duty + registration fee there, then book the SRO slot (slot guide). Login / payment-gateway screens are (to verify).
  3. If it is a non-registration stamp payment: use the e-STAMPS dashboard (or Meeseva App as named on the home banner). Create / use a citizen login. What you see after captcha is (to verify).
  4. If you need physical stamp paper or franking: check the official Stamp Vendor List and Franking Machine Holders lists. Low-denomination NJ papers only (up to Rs.100); pay the balance by the challan route the FAQ describes. An Indent for Stamp form is also published on the site.
  5. Don't reuse paper. FAQ Q5: one purpose only.
  6. Refunds: stampProcedure.htm says challan refund claims have a maximum limitation of 6 months, with 10% of the total stamp duty deducted. Apply through the Sub-Registrar to the District Collector / Sub-Collector / Deputy Collector / RDO / Tahsildar with original challan and bank receipt.
  7. Before you pay the seller: still run the encumbrance certificate (EC) check and the other DIY checks — stamping a bad deed does not fix title.

What it means for you

  • Green: you know whether this instrument is registration or non-registration; payment is on the department's Pre Registration or e-STAMPS path (or a listed vendor / franking holder); denominations and challan match the FAQ; names and property details match the deed draft.
  • Amber: someone offers only a high-value stamp paper from a private shop, or asks you to "pay by SMS link" — the home banner says no SMS payment link is sent; you are redirected to the portal's own net-banking option.
  • Red: a counterfeit high-denomination stamp paper, or a seller who wants the full consideration before stamp duty / Pre Registration is done and before you have an EC. Stop and straighten that out first.

FAQ

Does Telangana use SHCIL / StockHolding e-Stamping?

StockHolding's e-stamping page lists the States/UTs where e-Stamping is operational (list shown as of 20 Mar 2025 when we read it on 14 Oct 2026). Telangana is not on that list. Telangana runs its own e-STAMPS dashboard on registration.telangana.gov.in for non-registration payments, and Pre Registration for registration payments.

Where do I pay stamp duty for a sale deed in Hyderabad?

On registration.telangana.gov.in through the Pre Registration / slot-booking module (home-page banner: registration payments must be made through Pre-Registration Modules). Then book the SRO slot. See our registration slot booking guide.

What is e-STAMPS on the Telangana portal?

A department service linked from the home page as e-STAMPS (eStamp_Dashboard.htm), for payments on non-registration services; the banner also names the Meeseva App. Citizen login asks for User Type, Mobile/Email ID, Password and captcha. Post-login screens are (to verify).

Which stamp paper denominations are sold in Telangana?

FAQs-Stamps on registration.telangana.gov.in says Non-Judicial stamp papers of Rs.10, Rs.20, Rs.50 and Rs.100 only. Remaining duty is paid by challan (example in the FAQ).

Where do I find franking machine holders in Telangana?

On stampNotaryFranking.htm → Franking Machine Holders (frankingMachineList.htm). Franking Machine eChallan is listed for franking-machine licence holders. We did not run a live search on a named holder.
