Assigned land (DKT, lavuni patta) check before buying in Hyderabad and Telangana

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: assigned land is Government land granted to a person by the Government, usually with a condition that it must not be sold. In Telangana, transfers of such land are governed by the Telangana Assigned Lands (Prohibition of Transfers) Act, 1977 (Act No. 9 of 1977). The Bhu Bharati Act, 2025 says in section 5(4) that agricultural land is registered only when the transfer is not prohibited under that 1977 Act, and the Registration department's FAQ (Q18) lists transfers "prohibited under any statute" as a class barred from registration under section 22-A. If the papers say "DKT", "assignment patta" or "lavuni patta", stop and ask for the original assignment order and its conditions before you pay any advance.

Seven assigned-land checks before you buy in Hyderabad and Telangana

  1. Note the identifiers: survey number, sub-division, village, mandal and extent from the seller's pattadar passbook and deed.
  2. Read the land record: open the Bhu Bharati land record and save a dated screenshot of the details shown.
  3. Ask the direct question: was this land ever assigned by the Government (DKT, lavuni, assignment patta)? Get the answer in writing.
  4. Collect the orders: the original assignment order and any later order that changed the conditions or granted a patta to an alienee.
  5. Check the prohibited list: use our prohibited property check and keep the result.
  6. Read the chain: pull the encumbrance certificate and look for gaps filled by GPAs or unregistered papers.
  7. Protect your advance: ask your lawyer to add a seller declaration that the land is not assigned land and a refund clause if registration is refused.

Common mistake: assuming that a passbook in the seller's name means the land can be sold. An assignee can be the recorded pattadar and still be barred from selling. Ask what kind of land it is, not only whose name is on it.

FAQ

Can I buy assigned land in Telangana?

Transfers of assigned land are governed by the Telangana Assigned Lands (Prohibition of Transfers) Act, 1977. Section 5(4) of the Bhu Bharati Act, 2025 allows registration of agricultural land only where the transfer is not prohibited under that Act. Ask a lawyer about any exception before paying.

What does DKT or lavuni patta mean?

They are terms commonly used for land granted by the Government. The Bhu Bharati Act itself refers to "assigned or lavuni lands" in section 16. Ask for the original order to see its conditions.

Can a sada bainama make an assigned-land purchase valid?

No. Section 6(3) of the Bhu Bharati Act says that process does not validate an alienation that contravenes the 1977 Assigned Lands Act.

Is a GPA from the assignee enough?

No. The Registration FAQ (Q32) says a GPA does not transfer ownership; the buyer must get a sale deed registered.
