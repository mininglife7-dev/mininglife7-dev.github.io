Sub-Registrar refused to register your document in Hyderabad or Telangana? Appeal to the District Registrar

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: if a Sub-Registrar in Hyderabad or anywhere in Telangana refuses your sale deed, gift deed or other document, first ask for the written reasons. Under section 71 of the Registration Act, 1908 the Sub-Registrar must record the reasons in Book No. 2, endorse "registration refused" on the document and give you a copy of the reasons free on request. What you do next depends on the reason. If the seller (executant) denied signing or did not turn up, you apply to the District Registrar within 30 days (section 73). For most other reasons you appeal to the District Registrar within 30 days (section 72). If the District Registrar also refuses, a civil suit can be filed within 30 days (section 77). The Telangana Registration FAQ (Q25) describes the same path.

This guide is for buyers whose deed was stopped at the counter. If you have not reached the office yet, see the documents checklist for the Sub-Registrar office first.

What to do in the first 30 days after a refusal in Telangana

  1. Day 0: keep the document. Note the date of the refusal order. The 30-day limits in sections 72, 73 and 77 count from the order.
  2. Ask for the reasons in writing. Section 71 says a copy is given free and without unnecessary delay to the executant or claimant.
  3. Sort the reason into one of the rows above. A missing witness or paper is often fixed by presenting again; a denial of execution or a dispute needs the District Registrar.
  4. File with the District Registrar to whom that Sub-Registrar is subordinate. Attach the reasons, the deed, ID and challan copies. For a denial of execution, the application must be verified like a plaint (section 73(2)).
  5. If the District Registrar orders registration, present the document again within 30 days of that order (sections 72(2) and 75(2)). It then counts as registered from the date you first presented it.
  6. If the District Registrar refuses, section 77 allows a suit in the civil court within 30 days. This needs a lawyer.
  7. Protect the money. Do not release the balance to the seller until the deed is registered. Read your agreement's refund clause with your lawyer.

Common mistake: paying an agent to "manage" the refusal. The Registration home page warns that payments go only through the pre-registration slot booking module, eSTAMPS or the Meeseva app, and that no payment link is sent by SMS. Refusals are reversed on the record, not off it.

FAQ

Can the Sub-Registrar refuse without giving reasons?

Section 71 of the Registration Act says the Sub-Registrar must record the reasons in Book No. 2 and give a copy free on request (except where the property lies outside that sub-district).

How long do I have to appeal a refusal in Telangana?

30 days from the order, to the District Registrar, under section 72 (most grounds) or section 73 (denial of execution). A civil suit against the District Registrar's refusal must also be filed within 30 days (section 77).

The seller backed out and did not come to sign. What now?

TG Registration FAQ Q25: the office issues a notice or summons, then refuses if the seller still does not come; you can apply to the District Registrar, who holds an enquiry, and then go to the civil court if refused again.

My deed was signed more than four months ago. Is it too late?

FAQ Q21: up to eight months it can still be presented with the District Registrar's permission and a penalty of up to ten times the registration fee. After eight months it cannot be accepted.
