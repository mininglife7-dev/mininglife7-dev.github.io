Property tax check in Andhra Pradesh (Vizag, Vijayawada, Guntur, Tirupati): assessment number and dues

Checked against official sources on 14 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: Before you pay for a flat or house in Visakhapatnam (Vizag), Vijayawada, Guntur or Tirupati, ask the seller for the property tax assessment number and look it up on the city's official eMunicipal "Search your property" page. That page searches by Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name or Door Number ("search property by any one criteria"). The state CDMA Andhra Pradesh portal lists the related services: Know Your Dues, Mutation, Self Assessment and more. We don't check properties for anyone. Step by step: check the assessment and dues

  1. Get the assessment number from the seller's latest property tax receipt or demand notice.
  2. Open your city's search page above and search by Assessment Number (or Door Number / Owner Name). The page notes: "The information provided online is updated, and no physical visit is required."
  3. Vizag: GVMC's Citizen Services page tells house-tax and vacant-land-tax payers to "Enter your Assessment number in place of 'Old Assessment Number' & note your New Assessment Number". If the seller's receipt is old, look for a new number.
  4. Compare owner name and door number with the sale deed. Any arrears should be cleared by the seller before you pay further money. Payment pages (BillDesk and others listed on the GVMC page) are (to verify). You don't need to pay anything just to look.

State services on CDMA Andhra Pradesh

The CDMA AP home page lists these property services: Know Your Dues, Self Assessment, Revision Petition, General Revision Petition, Mutation, Addition or Alteration, Demolition, Tax Exemption, Vacancy Remission, Download Proceedings, Know Your Application Status, Property Tax Calculator. It also links the Puramithra app. Services need a Citizen Login (to verify: signup, OTP). Fees, timelines and documents for municipal mutation in AP were not stated on the pages we read, so we don't quote any (to verify).

Rebates and notices

  • The GVMC home page carries a notice that a 5 percent rebate on property tax for FY 2025-26 was extended till 31-03-2026. That date has passed, so whether a rebate applies for the current year is (to verify) on GVMC's latest notices.

What it means for you

  • Green: the assessment shows the seller (or the name on the title deed) at the right door number, with no arrears, and the receipt matches the online record.
  • Amber: arrears due, an old vs new assessment number mismatch, or an earlier owner's name still on the tax record (mutation never done). Ask the seller to fix it and show the receipt first.
  • Red: no assessment number at all for a finished building, or the assessment points to a different property or owner with no paper trail. Stop and have a lawyer read the file.

Then finish the other checks

  • Encumbrance certificate (EC) online, IGRS AP.
  • Building permission and BPS 2025 check.
  • Mutation / name transfer on Meebhoomi: the revenue land record (separate from the municipal tax record).

FAQ

How do I check property tax online in Visakhapatnam?

From the GVMC home page, e-Payment → Property Tax / Vacant Land opens visakhapatnam.emunicipal.ap.gov.in Search your property, which searches by Assessment Number, Old Assessment Number, Owner Name or Door Number (checked 14 Oct 2026). Result screens are (to verify).

The Vijayawada VMC website is down. Where else can I check?

vmc.ap.gov.in returned 503 when we tried on 14 Oct 2026. The eMunicipal page vijayawada.emunicipal.ap.gov.in Search your property loaded with the same search form. Whether results are Vijayawada-only is (to verify).

What does 'Old Assessment Number' mean on GVMC?

GVMC's Citizen Services page tells house-tax and VLT payers to enter their assessment number in place of 'Old Assessment Number' and note the New Assessment Number. An old receipt may carry the old number.

Can I apply for property-tax mutation online in AP?

The CDMA AP portal lists Mutation, Know Your Dues, Self Assessment and Download Proceedings under property services, behind a Citizen Login. Fees and timelines were not on the pages we read (to verify).

Does a clean tax record mean the title is clear?

No. Property tax is a municipal record, not proof of title. Check the EC and Meebhoomi too, and a lawyer must confirm title. Not legal advice.
