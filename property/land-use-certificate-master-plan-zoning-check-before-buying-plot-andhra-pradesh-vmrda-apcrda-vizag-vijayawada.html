Land use and Master Plan zoning check before buying a plot in Andhra Pradesh: VMRDA Vizag, APCRDA Vijayawada

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh, what you may build on a plot depends on the Master Plan and zoning regulations of the development authority it falls in: VMRDA around Visakhapatnam, APCRDA around Vijayawada, Guntur and Amaravati, or one of the other urban development authorities. DTCP's Master Plans page (read 24 Oct 2026) lists 23 of them, each with a Master Plan link and a Zoning Regulations link. For Vizag, VMRDA's own page lists G.O.Ms.No.144, dated 08.07.2026, Revised Master Plan-2041. Before you pay an advance, find the plot's survey number on the authority's Master Plan and check the zone and any road lines.

Why it matters: title, EC and even a layout number don't tell you the zone, and a plot under a proposed Master Plan road can lose land later. This page goes with our AP layout and LRS check and our AP building permission check.

Buyer: five zoning checks for a plot in Vizag, Vijayawada or Guntur

  1. Find the authority. From the village and mandal, work out whether the plot is under VMRDA, APCRDA or another authority on DTCP's list.
  2. Open that authority's Master Plan and find the survey number. For VMR, use the Revised Master Plan-2041 notified in GO 144. Note the zone and any proposed road or water body line.
  3. Check zoning against your plan. A house needs a zone that allows residential use under the zoning regulations. If it doesn't, the seller's promise of a future CLU is not enough. APCRDA itself says CLU "is not a right".
  4. Check the layout list. In APCRDA, look for the layout in "Approved Layouts". Elsewhere, ask for the layout permission number and check it with the authority. See our split plot approval check.
  5. Ask the authority in writing for anything unclear on the map, and keep the reply in your deal file. Then do the title, EC and tank buffer checks.

Common mistake: relying on an old plan. VMR's Master Plan was revised in July 2026 and APCRDA publishes modified zonal plans, so a zone your friend checked two years ago may have changed. Always open the current plan from the authority's own site.

FAQ

How do I check the master plan zone of a plot in Visakhapatnam?

Open the VMRDA Master Plan from DTCP's Master Plans page or VMRDA's site and find the survey number. VMRDA lists G.O.Ms.No.144 dated 08.07.2026 for the Revised Master Plan-2041.

Which authority's plan applies in Vijayawada or Guntur?

Generally APCRDA, which publishes CLU rules and modified zonal development plans. Confirm the authority for your village on DTCP's list of 23 authorities.

Can I get the land use changed later?

You can apply for CLU, but APCRDA's own page says CLU 'is not a right' and is decided on merit under the rules.

Which zoning regulations apply under the VMR Master Plan-2041?

VMRDA lists Common Zoning Regulations-2026 (G.O.Ms.No.157, 17.07.2026), while the GO 144 text refers to G.O.Ms.No.216 of 22.10.2025. Confirm which applies to your plot with VMRDA.

Does a layout number prove the plot is in a residential zone?

No. Check the zone on the Master Plan and the layout approval separately.
