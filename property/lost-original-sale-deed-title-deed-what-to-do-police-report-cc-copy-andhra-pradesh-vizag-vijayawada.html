Lost the original sale deed in Andhra Pradesh? Police complaint, CC copy and buyer checks for Vizag and Vijayawada

Official pages read on 24 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: in Andhra Pradesh, losing the original sale deed does not cancel the registration. The Sub-Registrar's office keeps the record, and FAQ Q41 of the AP Registration and Stamps Department says any person may obtain a copy of a registered document relating to immovable property. For the loss, AP Police take missing or lost document complaints through MeeSeva: the National Government Services Portal lists "Missing / Lost Documents / Articles by Police, Andhra Pradesh" as a fully online service. Get both the police record and a copy (CC) of the registered deed before you sell, borrow or transfer.

Owner: what to do in Vizag, Vijayawada or anywhere in AP

  1. Rule out the bank. If there was ever a home loan, ask whether the lender still holds the original.
  2. Collect the identifiers: document number, year, Sub-Registrar office, survey or door number.
  3. File the MeeSeva police complaint with a clear reason, place and date of loss, and ID and address proof.
  4. Get a CC or Signed CC through IGRS AP or a MeeSeva kiosk, and match it to your photocopies.
  5. Pull a fresh EC (how) and check the land record on Meebhoomi so the owner name still matches.
  6. Keep one file with the police record, CC, EC, tax receipts and mutation papers.
  7. Re-check the EC periodically until you sell or find the original.

Buyer: seller says the original is lost

  • Ask for the police complaint record, a CC and a recent EC, and check that the document number, parties and schedule agree.
  • Run a CERSAI search for a hidden mortgage; an original held by a lender can look "lost".
  • Run the prohibited property search as you would for any purchase.
  • Hold the balance until your lawyer has read the full chain.

Common mistake: treating a notarised affidavit from the seller as a replacement for the deed. It records the seller's statement; the registered record and the CC are what show title.

FAQ

Who can get a copy of a registered sale deed in AP?

FAQ Q41 of the AP Registration and Stamps Department: any person may obtain a copy of a registered document relating to immovable property. Wills and GPAs have tighter rules.

How do I report a lost sale deed to AP Police?

Through the MeeSeva police service for missing or lost documents. The application form asks for the missing document, the district, the reason for the loss, and ID and address proof.

What does a CC cost in AP?

The 2024 CC module manual shows Rs 200 registration fee, Rs 100 user charges and Rs 20 stamp duty per online request. FAQ Q41 mentions Rs 20 stamp paper plus Rs 100 and user charges. Re-check before paying.

Does the bank still have my original?

If you ever took a home loan against the property, it may. Ask the bank before filing a loss report, and ask for its release letter if the loan is closed.
