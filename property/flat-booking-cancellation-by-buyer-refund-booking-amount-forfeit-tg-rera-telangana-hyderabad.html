Cancelling a flat booking in Hyderabad: booking amount forfeiture and refund under TG-RERA

Official texts read on 26 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: if you cancel a flat booking in a RERA-registered Telangana project without any fault of the builder, the model agreement for sale in the Telangana RERA Rules, 2017 lets the builder forfeit the booking amount. The rest of what you paid must be returned within three months of the cancellation, or when the builder resells the flat, whichever is later. If the builder is at fault (for example, possession is late), the refund is your whole amount with interest (RERA Act section 18). Telangana sets that interest at SBI's highest MCLR plus 2% (rule 15), and the refund is due within 90 days (rule 16). By law the builder cannot take more than 10% of the price before a registered agreement (section 13(1)).

Who this is for: Hyderabad buyers who paid a booking or "token" amount and now want to cancel (loan not sanctioned, job change, second thoughts), and buyers whose builder is offering much less back than they paid.

What to do in Telangana, step by step

  1. Find out what you signed. A booking form only, or a registered agreement for sale? The agreement's clause numbers and the booking amount it states decide your refund. Our agreement of sale checklist explains the difference.
  2. Work out whose fault it is. If the builder is late, has changed the plan without consent, or its registration has lapsed, you are in s.18 territory: full refund plus interest. If you simply changed your mind, expect the booking amount to be kept.
  3. Cancel in writing. Send an email and a signed letter giving the flat number, the amounts paid with receipt numbers, and the date. Ask for the refund calculation and the date it will be paid.
  4. Check the arithmetic. The deduction should be no more than the booking amount stated in your agreement. GST, stamp duty and loan charges are separate questions (see our GST on flats guide and stamp refund guide).
  5. If nothing is paid in time, a complaint to TG-RERA is the route for a registered project. Our Form M / Form N guide lists the papers.

Common mistake: paying 20% or 30% "to lock the price" on a booking form, then finding that the form lets the builder keep a large part of it. By law no more than 10% should be taken before a registered agreement (s.13(1)), so ask for the agreement before you pay more.

FAQ

How much can a builder keep if I cancel a flat booking in Hyderabad?

Under clause 7.5 of the TG RERA model agreement, the booking amount, if the builder is not at fault. The balance is due within three months or on resale, whichever is later.

Can the builder take 20% at booking in Telangana?

Not before a registered agreement for sale. RERA Act s.13(1) caps advances at 10% of the cost.

What if the builder is delaying possession and I want to cancel?

RERA Act s.18 gives a refund of the amount paid with interest. Telangana rule 15 sets the interest at SBI's highest MCLR plus 2%, and rule 16 gives 90 days to pay.

Is there a limit on the booking amount that can be forfeited?

We found no Telangana cap on our checked pages. Check the booking amount stated in your agreement (to verify).
