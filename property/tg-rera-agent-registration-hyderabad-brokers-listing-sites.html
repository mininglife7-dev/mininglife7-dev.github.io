TG-RERA agent registration in Hyderabad: do brokers, listing sites and property expos need it?

Checked against official sources on 14 Oct 2026 · Not legal advice · Government websites change, so re-check before you rely on a step.

Short answer: TG-RERA's FAQ (dated 7 July 2023) says that anyone who facilitates the sale or purchase of a plot, apartment or building in a registered project in a planning area needs agent registration under section 9. That includes listing websites, newspapers and property exhibitions if they facilitate those sales (FAQ Q29). Your ad copy doesn't need TG-RERA's approval, but no project may be advertised until the promoter has registered it under section 3 (Q30).

What TG-RERA's FAQ says about agents

  • Listing sites, newspapers, expos (Q29): registration is needed if they intend to facilitate, or act for anyone to facilitate, the sale or purchase of a plot, apartment or building in a registered project being sold by the promoter in any planning area (section 9).
  • Your ads (Q30): agents advertising for a builder don't need prior approval of print, radio, TV or other promotions, but no ad may go out before the project is registered with TG-RERA under section 3. Don't advertise services you don't intend to offer.
  • False information from the builder (Q28): under section 12 the promoter is responsible for the truth of the ad and prospectus. The agent is liable if the agent makes a false or misleading representation about the agent's own services.
  • Signing (Q32): an agent is not authorised to sign on behalf of the promoter or builder.
  • Public record: the FAQ says that under section 34 TG-RERA's website must show details of registered agents, with photographs, and of agents whose registration has been revoked.

Hyderabad broker's 5-point check

  • You hold your own TG-RERA agent registration before you facilitate any sale in a registered project.
  • If you run a listing page, WhatsApp group business or expo stall that facilitates sales, you've checked whether Q29 applies to you.
  • Every project you promote shows as registered on rera.telangana.gov.in. No pre-launch ads for unregistered projects.
  • Each creative has the top-right box with the permit number, the TG-RERA registration number and the website. See our TG-RERA ad box guide.
  • You never sign sale documents for the builder (Q32).

What we didn't find in TG-RERA's FAQ: the validity period and fees for agent registration. Check these on rera.telangana.gov.in before you apply. (For comparison only, AP-RERA says agent registration there is valid up to five years; see our AP-RERA agent guide. Don't assume Telangana is the same.)

Buying in Hyderabad? Ask the broker for their TG-RERA agent registration number and look for it on rera.telangana.gov.in (to verify: the exact search screen). For land records, see our Bhu Bharati guide.

FAQ

Do property listing websites in Hyderabad need TG-RERA agent registration?

TG-RERA's FAQ (Q29) says yes, if they intend to facilitate the sale or purchase of a plot, apartment or building in a registered project in a planning area (section 9).

Does TG-RERA have to approve my ad before I publish it?

No (FAQ Q30). But no ad may go out for a project until the promoter has registered it with TG-RERA under section 3.

If the builder gives me wrong project details and I advertise them, am I liable?

TG-RERA's FAQ (Q28) says the promoter is responsible for the truth of ads under section 12. The agent is liable for false or misleading statements about the agent's own services. Check what you can against the project's TG-RERA page.

Can a broker sign the sale agreement for the builder?

No. TG-RERA's FAQ (Q32) says an agent isn't authorised to sign on behalf of the promoter or builder.

Do resale or rental posts need a RERA number?

We didn't find a TG-RERA rule requiring it. The FAQ answers above are about registered projects sold by promoters.
