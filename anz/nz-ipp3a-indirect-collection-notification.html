NZ IPP3A: telling people when you collect their information from someone else (from 1 May 2026)

The Privacy Amendment Act 2025 added IPP3A to New Zealand's Privacy Act 2020. From 1 May 2026, if your organisation collects personal information about someone from another source (a partner, a list, a website, a referee), you must take reasonable steps to make sure that person knows, unless an exception applies. It applies to information collected from 1 May 2026 onwards.

Where it bites

Lead lists, referrals, background checks, data bought or scraped from websites, and AI tools that enrich records.

What to do

  1. Map your indirect sources (an inventory again).
  2. Decide which exception, if any, applies to each source, and write down why.
  3. Add notification steps to your onboarding and first contact.
