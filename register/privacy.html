EURO AI Register privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Short version: what you type into EURO AI Register stays in your own browser. We do not run accounts, we do not receive your register, and the app contains no analytics or tracking.

1. What the app stores, and where

Your AI systems, screening answers, policy text and evidence records are saved in your browser's local storage on your device, under keys starting with euroai-register-. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you pick a file in the evidence log, the app reads it locally to calculate its name, size and SHA-256 fingerprint; the file itself is not uploaded.

The app also stores, locally, your licence key (if you enter one), the result of the last licence check, and the date of your last backup (used only for the backup reminder).

Because the data is only on your device, we cannot recover it. Clearing your browser data deletes it. Use Data > Export JSON backup regularly. You can delete everything at any time with Data > Delete all data.

2. The only network request: the licence check

If you enter a membership licence key, your browser sends that key and the product ID to Gumroad's licence API (api.gumroad.com) to check that the membership is active, and re-checks it every few days. No register content is included. Gumroad processes that request under its own privacy policy: gumroad.com/privacy. The page's security policy blocks every other connection.

3. No tracking

The app and these pages load no analytics, advertising, social media or third-party fonts and scripts, and set no tracking cookies.

4. Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when it delivers the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

5. Buying a membership

Purchases happen on Gumroad. Gumroad collects your name, email and payment details, and shares with us the information sellers normally receive about a sale (for example your email address and licence key), so we can provide the membership, send the monthly update and answer support requests. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.

6. If you email us

If you write to mininglife7@gmail.com, we use your message and address only to reply and, where relevant, to fix the issue you raise. Please do not send personal data about other people.

7. Your rights

For anything we hold about you (purchase and email data), you can ask for access, correction or deletion at mininglife7@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority. Data in your browser is under your own control.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. If a change ever means the app sends new data off your device, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
