Short answer (checked 14 Oct 2026): Under the EU AI Act, banned AI practices and the AI literacy duty have applied since 2 February 2025, and most other rules, including the Article 50 transparency duties for chatbots and AI-generated content, since 2 August 2026. High-risk rules start on 2 December 2027 for Annex III uses such as hiring, credit scoring and education, and on 2 August 2028 for AI built into regulated products like lifts or toys, after the AI Omnibus moved those dates (European Commission: AI Act timeline). Key dates (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)

  • 2 February 2025: prohibited practices (Art. 5) and AI literacy (Art. 4) apply.
  • 2 August 2026: Article 50 transparency duties apply (chatbot disclosure, marking AI-generated content, deepfake labelling, emotion recognition notice).
  • 2 December 2027: high-risk rules for Annex III use cases apply.
  • 2 August 2028: high-risk rules for AI in Annex I regulated products apply.

Official sources: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on EUR-Lex · Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 on EUR-Lex. Dates checked on 11 Oct 2026.
